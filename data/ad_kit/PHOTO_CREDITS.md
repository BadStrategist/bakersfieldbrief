# Photos used in ads and cards: source and licence

Never use an AI-generated image as a picture of Bakersfield. A real place needs a real
photograph, and the licence has to allow commercial use.

## `downtown_bakersfield_fox.jpg`

- Shows: the Fox Theater and the street front on Chester Avenue, downtown Bakersfield.
- Source: Wikimedia Commons, <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fox_Theater_-_Bakersfield,_CA_-_DSC00484.jpg>
- Author: Daderot, photographed 2026-05-28.
- Licence: **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication). No attribution is required and no
  share-alike condition applies, so it can be cropped, graded and used in paid ads.
- Local copy: `data/ad_kit/downtown_bakersfield_fox.jpg` (1920 x 1280 download of the
  5180 x 3453 original).
- Used in: `make_static_ad_hermes.py` (the `SIRENS AT 3 AM` ad).

## `scanner_hero_night.png`

- Shows: a handheld digital scanner and a desktop scanner on a dark desk at night.
- Source: **generated with an image model (fal.ai), 2026-10-14.** Retouched in the same
  model to strip the fake `POLICE` lettering the first pass produced, so the hardware now
  carries no text, no logo and no brand mark at all.
- It is an illustration of a type of device, not a photograph of a specific product, and
  not a photo of anywhere. Never caption it as Bakersfield, and never present it as an
  actual SDS100 or SDS200. It is used in `make_static_ad_scanner.py` and nowhere else.
- The screens in it are deliberately blank. Do not paint fake channel text or a fake
  incident onto them and call it real traffic.

## Candidates that were checked and rejected

- `File:BakersfieldSkyline.jpg`, Robert Hale, 2006, CC BY-SA 2.0. A real night skyline of
  downtown Bakersfield, the only real night photo found, but the share-alike condition
  would force the ad image itself to carry a CC BY-SA licence. Not used.
- The `shot1` to `shot5` plates in this folder are AI-generated. They are fine as generic
  illustrations, but they are never labelled or used as a photo of Bakersfield.

## Better than any of these

A photo the owner takes himself, at dusk, of Chester Avenue, the Padre or the Fox marquee.
Free, current, and owned outright. Ask before the next ad.
