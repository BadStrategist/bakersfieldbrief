# The Kern Overnight — August 10, 2026

A loud night in East Bakersfield. Just after 3 a.m., an electrical-line fire turned into a working commercial structure fire at Williams Street and Eureka Street, drawing a five-engine, four-truck response that kept crews talking until morning. Earlier, the city saw a reported shooting that turned out to have no victims, a domestic call in which a woman said she was pushed down stairs and cut, and the usual scatter of medical aids and welfare checks. Most of the action was east of downtown, around the Williams, Niles, and Chester corridors.

## The big one

Shortly after 3 a.m., callers reported electrical lines on fire in the area of Williams Street and Eureka Street, near 19th Street. The fire, which crews said was underneath a telephone pole, had already caught the back part of a large building by the time the first units arrived.

The response grew quickly: Engines 2, 4, 8, 41, and 42; Trucks 1, 7, 41, and 65; Rescue 2; and three battalion chiefs, with a safety officer. The buildings in the block are metal shop-type structures, and crews had to pop gates — at one point cutting a lock — to reach the rear of the fire. Command was set up with divisions Charlie and Alpha, and an incident commander reported fire spreading to an adjacent building. Crews called for a supply line and worked through the roof line ("there is definitely in one building this time," one commander said).

At the same time, a caller reported heavy smoke from the Family Dollar at 2110 Niles Street, a few blocks south. That was folded into the same command: commercial structure, moderate smoke, no flames initially. No injuries were heard on the air. Crews were still working the fire when the radio traffic on this feed wrapped up.

## Around the county

**Reported shooting, Francis Street.** Just after 2:14 a.m., a caller said a subject had come to the window of a residence and shot through it, and that a 17-year-old had blood on them. Units responded, but dispatchers and officers confirmed on the air there were no victims of a shooting, and the scene was cleared.

**Domestic with injury, Baker Street near Sumner.** Around 2:26 a.m., a female caller said she had been pushed down stairs and cut, and that a male was following her. She told dispatchers she was hiding inside a residence. Units were sent en route; no disposition was heard on the air.

**Assault report involving a child, Hops Lane.** At 9:40 p.m., units were sent to the 1400 block of Hops Lane on a report of an assault involving a five-year-old, with a suspect description broadcast. No arrest or further outcome was heard on the air.

**Attempted arson, Deal Avenue.** At 10:50 p.m., a caller reported a subject pouring a substance — possibly gas — on items to set them on fire. There was no active fire, and the subject was reportedly walking away. Officers searched the area.

**Attempted burglary, Calloway Drive.** Just after 11 p.m., a caller reported a subject inside a business that had not cleared; units set up around the building. Later, crews confirmed an attempted burglary with no entry made at a related location.

**Structure fire report, Mount Vernon Avenue.** At 10:58 p.m., a fire was reported on the north side of a building on Mount Vernon Avenue. Crews were sent; little further detail was heard.
