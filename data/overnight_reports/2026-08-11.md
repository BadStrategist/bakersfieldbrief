# The Kern Overnight — August 11, 2026

A busy early-morning shift across Kern County: a semi-truck fire on Interstate 5 near the Grapevine, a three-vehicle crash with multiple patients near Kimberlina Road, and a vehicle fire on Mount Vernon Avenue. Sixteen incidents tracked between 9 PM and 6 AM.
