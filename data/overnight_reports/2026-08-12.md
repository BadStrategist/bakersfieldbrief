# The Kern Overnight — August 12, 2026

A busy night split across two waves — a violent evening in east and west Bakersfield, then a quieter post-midnight stretch punctuated by a fire, a stolen-firearms search, and a car through a building. The biggest single event was a house fire on Far Vista Avenue that grew to half the structure before crews got water on it. The night also brought a vehicle-versus-pedestrian crash in Kern Canyon Estates, a reported shooting on Bernard Street, a domestic assault with a vehicle used as a weapon, and an early-morning search for a tow-truck driver connected to four missing long guns.

## The big one

Just after 10:30 p.m., dispatchers sent a reinforced response to a house fire in the 100 block of Far Vista Avenue in east Bakersfield: Engines 81, 84, 83, 85, and 71, two battalion chiefs, and a safety officer. By 10:44 p.m. crews had water on the fire and reported roughly 50 percent involvement of the structure. An interior attack with a two-out crew was established, and breathing support was requested — a working fire that kept east-side companies tied up well past 11 p.m. No injuries were announced on the air.

## Around the county

**Vehicle versus pedestrian, Grand Canyon Road.** At 9:39 p.m., fire and CHP units were sent to a vehicle-versus-pedestrian crash in Kern Canyon Estates. Arriving crews found a single patient on the roadway; a full medical response was staged and the patient was taken by ambulance.

**Reported shooting, Bernard Street.** Around 10:12 p.m., a caller reported that someone had been shot in the face in the 300 block of Bernard Street. Multiple units were sent en route; no disposition was heard on the air before the feed moved on.

**Domestic assault with a vehicle, west Bakersfield.** Just after 10:30 p.m., a woman said a suspect had tried to hit her with her own vehicle. The suspect — described as a Hispanic male, 54, in a black shirt and blue shorts — was being sought by patrol units.

**Semi truck hit-and-run, Main Drane Road.** Around 9:46 p.m., a semi truck with a white box trailer was reported driving recklessly northbound on Main Drane Road, then exiting westbound Highway 46 toward the truck stop. CHP was searching for the vehicle.

**Missing long guns, tow-truck search.** Just before 4 a.m., deputies and CHP were searching for a flatbed tow truck — white cab, driver in a blue jumpsuit and a black hat with a skull-and-crossbones logo — connected to reports of four long guns missing. No capture was heard on the air.

## Night notes

Traffic came in two waves: a heavy 9–11 p.m. stretch anchored by the pedestrian crash, the reported shooting, and the Far Vista house fire, then a second pulse after midnight with the Main Drane suspicious-person call and the 3 a.m. tow-truck search. East Bakersfield carried most of the load, and the structure fire was the only event that kept a large multi-company response working for an extended stretch. No fire-related injuries were announced on the air.
