# The Kern Overnight — August 18, 2026

A truck fire that climbed into hillside vegetation west of Bakersfield drew the night's heaviest response — dozers, a helicopter and water tender among the resources — and a 53-foot trailer went fully involved north of town a few hours later. A barrage of roughly 30 shots near Panama Lane and South Allen Road kept officers busy in the south end, a welfare check on Columbus Street boiled over into a three-person fight, and a big-rig hauling table grapes shut down a roadway in Delano. The rest of the night was the usual spread: domestic calls, ShotSpotter activations, welfare checks and small fires.

## The big one

**Truck fire spreading into hillside vegetation, west of Bakersfield.** Around 9:47 p.m., a truck fire that reportedly tipped over and burned down a trailer pushed into the hillside and escalated into a wildland response — multiple engines, a dozer, helicopter 409 and a water tender among the ordered resources. The reporting party pointed out lights on the hill as crews worked to access the burn.

## Around the county

**Fully involved trailer fire, Garzoli Road.** Just before 3:45 a.m., a 53-foot trailer north of Pond on Garzoli Road was fully involved, with a spot of grass fire spreading alongside it. Battalion crews responded to what dispatch dubbed the Garzoli Incident and remained on scene for hotspots.

**Roughly 30 shots heard, Panama Lane & South Allen Road.** Just before 10:45 p.m., a caller reported about 30 shots fired, mostly in a field in the area. The call stood for service while units investigated.

**Welfare check becomes a fight, Columbus Street.** Around 3:06 a.m., a welfare check near the Wells Fargo on Columbus Street escalated — a man on a bicycle was approached by subjects and an impact sound was heard, followed by a fight involving three people. Two suspects fled northbound Columbus; units searched.

**Roadway closure after grape load crash, Delano.** Just after 4 a.m., a big-rig incident with a load of table grapes brought a roadway closure in Delano, with Environmental Health notified.

## Night notes

The overnight was bookended by fires — the hillside vegetation fire west of town just before 10 p.m. and the fully involved trailer on Garzoli Road in the small hours — with the wildland response to the first being the heaviest package of the night. Gunfire reports came in three separate times, topped by the 30-round burst in a south Bakersfield field. The early-morning feed transcribed cleanly after the first pass, so this edition covers the full overnight window.
