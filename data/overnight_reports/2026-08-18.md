# The Kern Overnight — August 18, 2026

A truck fire that climbed into hillside vegetation west of Bakersfield drew the night's heaviest response — dozers, a helicopter and water tender among the resources — while a barrage of roughly 30 shots near Panama Lane and South Allen Road kept officers busy in the south end. Deputies searched a fenced business property after cameras caught subjects slipping in and never coming back out, and fire crews handled a quarter-acre grass fire near Edison. The rest of the night was the usual mix: domestic calls, a runaway juvenile in Rosamond, welfare checks and a smattering of small fires.

## The big one

**Truck fire spreading into hillside vegetation, west of Bakersfield.** Around 9:47 p.m., a truck fire that reportedly tipped over and burned down a trailer pushed into the hillside and escalated into a wildland response — multiple engines, a dozer, helicopter 409 and a water tender among the ordered resources. The reporting party pointed out lights on the hill as crews worked to access the burn.

## Around the county

**Roughly 30 shots heard, Panama Lane & South Allen Road.** Just before 10:45 p.m., a caller reported about 30 shots fired, mostly in a field in the area. The call stood for service while units investigated.

**Burglary in progress with live camera search, K Street / Chester Avenue.** At 10:48 p.m., an alarm company and video surveillance showed subjects enter a business yard from the south and never exit. Deputies set up containment and began a search of the property.

**Runaway juvenile, Rosamond Boulevard.** At 10:38 p.m., a 15-year-old was reported missing from a residence in Rosamond. Units responded to search.

**Quarter-acre grass fire near Edison.** Just before midnight, crews with a battalion response found a grass fire in the sump northwest of Wheat Patch and Edison highways, making access off Mills Road.

The headline of the night was the hillside vegetation fire west of town, which pulled in the heaviest wildland package of the overnight — helicopter, dozers and a water tender.
