# The Kern Overnight — August 19, 2026

A grass fire east of Bakersfield that swelled to 5–10 acres drew the night's heaviest response — dozers, helicopter 409 and a water tender among the resources dispatched to what crews dubbed the Apollo Incident — while a fire in a downtown alley climbed into a garage and sent a Station 1 assignment rolling. A burglary suspect fled toward Union Avenue on a beach cruiser, and the early hours carried the usual mix of domestic calls, welfare checks and disturbances.

## The big one

**Grass fire grows to 5–10 acres, east of Bakersfield.** Just after 1 a.m., crews got word of a grass fire burning roughly 2 miles east of the county dump and north of the railroad tracks. The first update put it at 3–5 acres, growing to 5–10 as units arrived — engine 345, water tender 12, dozers, a safety officer and helicopter 409 among the response, with crews working access through corral roads. Union Pacific train traffic was held while crews mopped up.

## Around the county

**Fire in an alley spreads into a garage, downtown.** Around 2:22 a.m., a report of a fire in an alley near 9th & K streets — a fire that spread into a garage — drew the Station 1 assignment: Engine 1, Truck 1, Rescue 1, Battalion 1 and Engine 6.

**Burglary suspect on a beach cruiser, Union Avenue.** Just before 2:20 a.m., a burglary in progress sent units searching for a suspect described as a white male, 5-foot-11, about 150 pounds, in a white tank top and black gym shorts, last seen riding a blue beach cruiser and exiting onto Union Avenue.

**Person in crisis, reportedly nude, downtown.** Around 12:24 a.m., units responded to 21st & O streets for a person in crisis, reportedly nude with a shirt draped over them and possibly in the middle of a psychiatric episode.
