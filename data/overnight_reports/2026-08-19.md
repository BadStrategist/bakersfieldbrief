# The Kern Overnight — August 19, 2026

A grass fire east of Bakersfield that swelled to 5–10 acres drew the night's heaviest response — dozers, helicopter 409 and a water tender among the resources dispatched to what crews dubbed the Apollo Incident — while a fire in a downtown alley that spread toward a house on Bell Avenue upgraded to a structure response in the early hours. A two-vehicle head-on on Copus Road west of Old River pulled a multi-engine assignment with an air ambulance placed on standby, a vehicle plowed into a building on South Union Avenue, and shots were reported fired overnight. The rest of the night ran to the usual spread: burglaries, domestic calls, medical aids and disturbances.

## The big one

**Grass fire grows to 5–10 acres, east of Bakersfield.** Just after 1 a.m., crews got word of a grass fire burning roughly 2 miles east of the county dump and north of the railroad tracks. The first update put it at 3–5 acres, growing to 5–10 as units arrived — engine 345, water tender 12, dozers, a safety officer and helicopter 409 among the response, with crews working access through corral roads off Newmark. Union Pacific train traffic was held while crews mopped up.

## Around the county

**Alley fire spreads toward a house, Bell Avenue.** Around 4:34 a.m., a fire in an alley across from the 100 block of Bell Avenue spread toward a house and was upgraded to a structure response — Station 64, Truck 1, medic squad 63 and Battalion 6 among the assignment.

**Head-on collision, Copus Road west of Old River.** Just after 5:30 a.m., a two-vehicle head-on roughly 5.5 miles west of Old River Road pulled multiple engines plus a truck. An air ambulance was placed on standby and later stood down, with one ground ambulance cancelled as crews handled it.

**Vehicle into a building, South Union Avenue.** Around 3:48 a.m., a vehicle reportedly drove into a building on South Union Avenue, with CHP en route and Battalion 4 dispatched.

**Shots reported fired overnight.** Just before 3:52 a.m., a single reporting party reported multiple shots heard; the exact location was unclear on the air as units responded.
