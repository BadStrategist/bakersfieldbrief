# The Kern Overnight — August 19, 2026

A grass fire east of Bakersfield that swelled to 5–10 acres drew the night's heaviest response — dozers, helicopter 409 and a water tender among the resources dispatched to what crews dubbed the Apollo Incident — and a fire in an alley climbed into a house on Belle Avenue in the pre-dawn hours. A vehicle crashed into a building on South Union Avenue, a head-on collision shut down attention on Copus Road west of Old River, and a pursuit ended with the driver bailing over a fence near 3rd & Union. The rest of the night was the usual spread: domestic calls, welfare checks, car meets and small disturbances.

## The big one

**Grass fire grows to 5–10 acres, east of Bakersfield.** Just after 1 a.m., crews got word of a grass fire burning roughly 2 miles east of the county dump and north of the railroad tracks. The first update put it at 3–5 acres, growing to 5–10 as units arrived — engine 345, water tender 12, dozers, a safety officer and helicopter 409 among the response, with crews working access through corral roads and gates. Union Pacific train traffic was held while crews mopped up.

## Around the county

**Alley fire spreads into a house, Belle Avenue.** Around 4:35 a.m., a fire reported in an alley across from the 100 block of Belle Avenue spread to a house, upgrading the call to a structure response with Engine 63, Truck 1, Medics Squad 63 and Battalion 6.

**Vehicle into a building, South Union Avenue.** Just before 3:50 a.m., a vehicle crashed into a building in the 9800 block of South Union Avenue, with CHP en route and a fire battalion responding.

**Head-on collision, Copus Road.** Just after 5:30 a.m., a head-on collision between two vehicles about 5.5 miles west of Old River drew additional engines and Truck 54, with an air ambulance standing by before being cancelled.

**Burglary suspect on a beach cruiser, Union Avenue.** Just before 2:20 a.m., a burglary in progress sent units searching for a suspect described as a white male, 5-foot-11, about 150 pounds, in a white tank top and black gym shorts, last seen riding a blue beach cruiser and exiting onto Union Avenue.

**Pursuit ends with a foot chase, 3rd & Union.** Around 3:30 a.m., a pursuit ended near 3rd & Union when the driver bailed and jumped a fence into a business yard; the female passenger was uncooperative as officers searched.
