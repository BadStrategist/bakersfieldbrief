# The Kern Overnight — August 20, 2026

A train struck a pedestrian near the rail line in south Bakersfield in the night's heaviest response — a multi-engine assignment that had crews working access around the train — while a vehicle-versus-pedestrian collision downtown left a pedestrian down at the scene, and a domestic call on Butterfield Avenue escalated when a man struck a woman and tried to take an infant. The rest of the night ran to the usual spread: burglaries, domestic violence calls, a preschool alarm, a flooding home and a string of medical aids.

## The big one

**Train strikes pedestrian, south Bakersfield.** Just before 11:34 p.m., a train hit a pedestrian near the rail line off Lynwood and Height streets. Engines 37, 34 and 33 went out with Battalion 3, and crews worked access around the train to reach the patient on the far side.

## Around the county

**Vehicle versus pedestrian, downtown.** Around 9:22 p.m., a pedestrian was down at the scene of a vehicle-versus-pedestrian collision near Main Street, described as a woman on probation or supervised release. Officers had surveillance video archived as part of the investigation.

**Man strikes woman, attempts to take infant, Butterfield Avenue.** Just after 12:39 a.m., a man hit a woman and attempted to take an infant before officers arrived, with a kitchen knife mentioned on the air. The subject was described as a 34-year-old Hispanic man in a white t-shirt and gray shorts; a heavy-duty response was requested.

**Burglary with suspects inside, Baker Street.** Around 9:19 p.m., a burglary at a furniture business on Baker Street had suspects possibly still inside, with a silver Prius spotted in an alleyway nearby. Officers set a perimeter at Baker and Kentucky with a second boundary at Baker and Jackson.

**Person in crisis with knife reported, Di Giorgio Road.** Just before midnight, a person in crisis on Di Giorgio Road reportedly made threats against family members and was possibly in possession of a knife, with a child present in the residence. The reporting party said an uncle had seen the knife.
