# The Kern Overnight — August 21, 2026

A fast-moving brush fire on McCombs Road north of Wasco drew the night's heaviest fire response — multiple engines, a water tender and extra wildland equipment for what crews expected to be an extensive mop-up — while an armed robbery in north Bakersfield, a fully engulfed semi on I-5 near the Grapevine and a domestic call at the Panama Lane Winco kept law enforcement busy. The night also saw a pair of separate vehicle fires along the Interstate 5 corridor.

## The big one

**Brush fire, McCombs Road.** Around 10:34 p.m., a quarter-acre brush fire on McCombs Road (State Route 43 north of Wasco) was burning with a fast rate of spread and potential to run west. Multiple engines and a water tender responded, additional wildland equipment was ordered, and crews split into Division Bravo and Division Yankee for what command described as a lengthy mop-up.

## Around the county

**Semi fully engulfed, northbound I-5 south of Grapevine.** Just before 1 a.m., a semi truck with a trailer was fully engulfed in the number three lane of northbound I-5 south of Grapevine. CHP and Engine 56 were on scene, with command established as the Tejon Incident.

**Armed robbery, Denise Avenue.** Around 9:50 p.m., a robbery at gunpoint was reported on Denise Avenue in north Bakersfield. The suspect was described as a transient white male in his late 30s, wearing a baseball cap and black face mask, pulling a wagon, and accompanied by a white female.

**Domestic incident, Panama Lane Winco.** Shortly after 1 a.m., a man chased a woman through the parking lot of the Winco on Panama Lane before both left in a vehicle. Officers broadcast a description and were investigating.

**Domestic violence victim search, 17th & Union.** Around 10:27 p.m., officers were looking for the victim of a domestic incident near 17th Street and Union Avenue — a Hispanic woman in her 30s in a blue dress — with the suspect reported in a red pickup truck in a nearby alley.

## The log

**21:47** — Semi truck roughly 50 percent involved with a detached trailer and about an eighth of an acre of grass burning on Interstate 5 in the Grapevine area; Engine 26 on scene with CHP.

**21:43** — Neighbor dispute on Kernita Road following a backyard fire; officers asked to back the reporting party.

**23:38** — Man whispering that he needed police on South Haley Street, then the call disconnected.

**00:25** — Alarm company reported two subjects near a business on Ming Avenue, one holding a gas can.

**01:16** — Three subjects seen jumping a fence on King Street.

**01:36** — Dodge Ram with a car hauler on the right-hand shoulder of southbound I-5 near the Grapevine with a possible engine fire; information secondhand from the driver's brother, CHP asked to respond.

## Night notes

August heat and dry brush kept grass-fire risk elevated, and the McCombs Road fire showed how quickly a small start can escalate a large response. Truck fires along the I-5 Grapevine grade are a recurring summer hazard, with two reported overnight.
