# The Kern Overnight — August 23, 2026

A grass fire that grew to three or four acres in a field south of Highway 46 near Wildwood Road drew a battalion-level wildland response and was the night's biggest call. Before that, a vehicle rolled over in the median just south of Twisselman Road with power lines down across the lane, and fire crews answered an outside fire near the railroad tracks on Sumner Street.

## The big one

**Grass fire grows to several acres, south of Highway 46 near Wildwood Road.** Around 4:30 a.m., a grass fire broke out in a field near a small trailer community just east of Wildwood Road, reachable off Highway 46. Crews reported three to four acres burning with roads on three sides of the fire; Battalion 3 was on scene and asked for additional wildland units as Engine 931 worked its way in from the south.

## Around the county

**Vehicle rollover with power lines down, south of Twisselman Road.** Just after 3:30 a.m., a vehicle rolled over in the median on a northbound highway just south of Twisselman Road, with reports of power lines down across the lane. Units were en route.

**Outside fire near the railroad tracks, Sumner Street.** Around 1:55 a.m., an outside fire was reported on the 100 block of Sumner Street near the railroad tracks, and engine companies were dispatched.

**Suspicious person at a campground.** About 2:10 a.m., someone was reported coming into a campground wearing a gray t-shirt, gray pants and no shoes; units were checking the area.
