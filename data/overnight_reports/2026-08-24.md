# The Kern Overnight — August 24, 2026

A dispute at a wellness facility in southwest Bakersfield turned violent late Saturday night when a roommate reportedly poured hot water on another resident, bringing officers to the scene. Earlier, a brush fire in the county drew dozers and two battalions of firefighters, and through the early hours police handled a domestic call on Riverlakes Drive, a residential burglary in north Bakersfield and a welfare check on Beardsley Avenue.

## The big one

**Assault at a southwest Bakersfield wellness facility.** Just before 11 p.m., officers responded to a wellness facility on Evanston Court after a dispute escalated, with a roommate reportedly pouring hot water on another resident and on bedding and equipment at the scene.

## Around the county

**Brush fire draws dozers, unincorporated Kern County.** Around 9:20 p.m., a brush fire was reported in a field, possibly being put out by residents. Fire officials dispatched multiple engines, two battalions and dozers to the scene.

**Domestic disturbance on Riverlakes Drive.** Shortly after 11 p.m., officers responded to a domestic call in southwest Bakersfield in which residents were pushing and throwing property; a woman on scene said she was pregnant and had been struck.

**Residential burglary, north Bakersfield.** About 5:54 a.m., a caller reported that a subject who had just fled had burglarized his residence. Officers were investigating.

**Welfare check on Beardsley Avenue.** Just after midnight, officers checked on a 19-year-old woman at a residence in southeast Bakersfield who was under the influence and had made suicidal statements.

## Night notes

Overnight activity clustered on the southwest side of town, where a wellness-facility dispute and a domestic call drew police responses within the same hour, while the county brush fire was reported to be largely under control by residents before crews arrived. Fire crews handled a pair of medical calls in the early hours, and officers closed out a residential burglary in north Bakersfield just before dawn.
