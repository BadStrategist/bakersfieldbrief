# The Kern Overnight — August 25, 2026

Two men in an older tan Toyota Camry were reported trying to get an 18-year-old woman into their vehicle near Union Avenue and Columbus Street late Monday night, sending officers searching the area. Into the early hours of Tuesday, police also responded to a knife disturbance on the 2800 block of C Street where a resident barricaded herself in a bathroom, chased a minibike at low speed through east Bakersfield, and investigated a report of a brick thrown through a woman's car and patio door on Coventry Drive.

## The big one

**Possible attempted abduction near Union Avenue and Columbus Street.** Shortly before 11 p.m., a caller reported two men in a tan older Toyota Camry attempting to get an 18-year-old woman into their vehicle at the intersection of Union Avenue and Columbus Street. The vehicle was last seen heading northbound on Union Avenue as officers searched the area.

## Around the county

**Knife disturbance on the 2800 block of C Street.** Around 1 a.m., officers responded to a disturbance on the 2800 block of C Street where a subject was reported in possession of a butcher knife, with additional knives inside the garage, while the reporting party stayed barricaded in a bathroom.

**Slow-speed minibike chase through east Bakersfield.** Shortly before 2 a.m., officers pursued a minibike at low speed — westbound on Oregon Street, then northbound on Gage Avenue and across Niles Street — with speeds holding between roughly 15 and 37 mph under light traffic.

**A 2017 Nissan refused to stop in east Bakersfield.** Around 3:15 a.m., a unit began tracking a 2017 Nissan that refused to stop for officers in east Bakersfield.

**Brick thrown through a car and patio door on Coventry Drive.** Around 12:30 a.m., a woman reported her ex-boyfriend threw a brick through her vehicle and patio door on Coventry Drive. Officers were investigating.

## The log
