# The Kern Overnight — August 29, 2026

An orchard fire was reported actively spreading in rural Kern County shortly before 6 a.m. Saturday, while a strong-arm robbery near East California Avenue and Williams Street sent medical aid to the scene late Friday night. Overnight calls also included a vehicle crashing into a pole on Niles Street with a full rescue response, a structure fire in east Bakersfield tied to a burnt electrical outlet and a welfare check on a woman on oxygen whose husband hadn't returned.

## The big one

**Orchard fire spreading in rural Kern County.** Around 5:59 a.m., crews were working an orchard fire that was reported positively spreading, with units asking whether additional resources should be started.

## Around the county

**Strong-arm robbery near East California Avenue and Williams Street.** Around 11:42 p.m., officers were sent to the area for a strong-arm robbery, with medical aid also dispatched to the scene. The reporting party was standing by for contact; suspects were described in a gray BMW and a black-and-white Hyundai.

**Vehicle versus pole on Niles Street.** Shortly after 5 a.m., a full rescue response — engines, rescue, truck and a battalion chief — was sent to Niles Street for a vehicle that struck a pole. The reporting party later advised there was no patient on scene.

**Structure fire in east Bakersfield.** Shortly before 1:40 a.m., fire crews including multiple engines and a battalion responded to a reported structure fire in east Bakersfield, where they found a burnt electrical outlet.

**Attempt to locate a suicidal subject.** Around 6 a.m., officers were looking for a male subject who had called a crisis hotline and said he had attempted suicide by gas exposure a few hours earlier.
