# The Kern Overnight — August 29, 2026

An orchard fire was reported actively spreading in rural Kern County shortly before 6 a.m. Saturday, while a strong-arm robbery near East California Avenue and Williams Street sent medical aid to the scene late Friday night. Overnight calls also included a vehicle crashing into a pole on Niles Street with a full rescue response, a structure fire in east Bakersfield tied to a burnt electrical outlet and a welfare check on a woman on oxygen whose husband hadn't returned.

## The big one
