# The Kern Overnight — August 31, 2026

A working fire at Cottonwood Road and Watts Drive drew a multi-engine response in the overnight hours, and a second fire was ordered outside the Golden West Casino on South Union Avenue. Officers also handled a reported stabbing, a series of domestic calls, and a welfare check on a toddler seen running in and out of a Calloway Avenue business.

## The big one

**Working fire at Cottonwood Road and Watts Drive.** Around 11:25 p.m., a Fire 2 assignment was ordered at the Cottonwood Road and Watts Drive intersection, bringing multiple engines and a battalion chief to the scene. Additional callers reported smoke in the area, which crews confirmed was the same incident.

## Around the county

**Fire outside the Golden West Casino.** Just after midnight, a Fire 2 assignment was ordered for a fire outside the Golden West Casino on South Union Avenue, with multiple engines, a rescue unit and a battalion chief responding.

**Stabbing reported in Bakersfield.** Around 9:53 p.m., officers were asked to respond to a reported stabbing that had just occurred; a nearby unit was requested for the call.

**Camp fires reported in a homeless encampment.** Around 1:15 a.m., a caller reported people believed to be setting each other's camps on fire. Responders were told to treat it as a fire only if flames were actually seen.

**Domestic violence call on Polo Drive.** Around 1:48 a.m., officers responded to a report that a man shoved his wife to the floor. Additional callers in the area were being located.
