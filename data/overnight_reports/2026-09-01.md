# The Kern Overnight — September 1, 2026

A rollover on Highway 58 west of Tucker Road drew a multi-unit response with an air unit on standby shortly after midnight, and officers were sent to a residence in the Coburn Ridge area on reports of five to six shots fired. Crews also knocked down a grass fire near Wasco, ran a foot pursuit of a rider on a dirt bike along a Chester Avenue bike path, and worked a steady run of domestic, welfare and medical calls across the county.

## The big one

**Rollover on Highway 58 west of Tucker Road.** Around 12:41 a.m., a vehicle rollover with unknown injuries on westbound Highway 58 west of Tucker Road brought a multi-unit response including a battalion chief, with an air unit on standby. Westbound lanes were reduced as crews worked the scene.

**Shots fired in the Coburn Ridge area.** Around 1:48 a.m., callers reported five to six shots fired at a residence, with subjects in ski masks seen at the door. The reporting party was checking cameras as a vehicle fled the area; officers responded.

## Around the county

**Grass fire near Wasco.** Around 1:30 a.m., a grass fire off Wasco Pond Road north of Cecil Avenue drew an engine, a patrol unit and additional assistance. Fire progression was stopped and crews were released.

**Court order violation with a firearm threat.** Around 9:36 p.m., a call at Virginia Avenue and Powell Street reported a male subject making threats to obtain a firearm. Additional officers responded.

**Foot pursuit along a Chester Avenue bike path.** Around 9:58 p.m., officers ran a short pursuit of a male on a dirt bike riding along a bike path in north Bakersfield; visual was lost at Chester Avenue and units were searching.

**Suicidal teenager on Marble Street.** Around 10:48 p.m., reports of a 17-year-old making suicidal statements at a residence drew officers and medics, with family members contacted.

## Night notes

Monday's heat held into the evening with a clear, warm overnight across Kern County. Activity was dominated by the Highway 58 rollover and the Coburn Ridge shooting report, alongside a grass fire near Wasco and a string of domestic, welfare and mental-health calls into the early morning.
