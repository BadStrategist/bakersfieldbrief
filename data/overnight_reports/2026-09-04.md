# The Kern Overnight — September 4, 2026

A driver led officers on a prolonged chase up Madison Street without stopping, crews knocked down a building fire near Edwards Air Force Base, and a commercial burglary caught on surveillance sent two hooded suspects out of view after the camera feed cut. Overnight, deputies and patrol also handled several domestic disputes and welfare checks across the metro area.

## The big one

**Long pursuit on Madison Street.** Around 9:30 p.m., a driver who refused to yield led officers northbound along Madison Street without stopping. The chase stretched past the 10 o'clock hour, with speeds climbing to roughly 55 mph on roadways that were otherwise empty of pedestrians and traffic. Units picked up the pursuit after the initial stop was refused, and there was a brief concern over a possible gunshot before the chase continued north.

## Around the county

**Structure fire near Edwards Air Force Base.** Around 12:26 a.m., reports of smoke from a building pulled a large fire assignment — multiple engines and a battalion — to the Edwards Air Force Base area. Crews found only light residual smoke reaching into the attic, cancelled reinforcements and scaled the response back to a regular structure fire.

**Burglars caught on camera cutting their own feed.** Around 12:37 a.m., an employee watching live surveillance saw two hooded males force their way into a business on 15th Street West and begin taking items. The employee then lost connection to the cameras — possibly because the suspects had removed them — and units began clearing the area.

**Domestic incident in progress.** Around 11:40 p.m., a domestic call turned urgent when a caller could be heard yelling in the background before the suspect began chasing her. Officers responded to the East 21st Street area, where the caller reported being struck near 21st and D Street.

## Night notes

The overnight span into the early hours was marked by one long-running pursuit and a steady string of domestic and welfare calls rather than any single large-scale emergency. A structure fire near Edwards was contained quickly, keeping fire crews' overnight work relatively brief outside the metro traffic incidents.
