# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A wanted subject's high-speed run up Airport Drive ended in a major-injury crash with CPR underway, and crews spent the night working a roughly 15-to-20-acre brush fire near Pozo Creek. Here's what came over the scanner from sundown Friday into early Saturday.

## The big one

A pursuit that reached 91 mph on Airport Drive ended in a major-injury crash near the intersection with Olive Drive, where responders performed CPR. The chase began when officers tried to stop a subject wanted on a Ramey warrant, with an air unit and K9 available as the driver turned onto North Chester Avenue. Crews had been preparing spike strips as the pursuit rolled north through Bakersfield.

## Around the county

**Brush fire near Pozo Creek** — A wildfire burning near Pozo Creek and the Granite area drew a large Kern County assignment including engines, a strike team and a type-2 helicopter. Crews reached the flames over rough country by way of ranch gates and dirt roads, with the fire reported at roughly 15 to 20 acres.

**Gunshot wound** — Emergency medical units staged for a reported gunshot wound at Acres Road and Barbara Avenue as officers positioned nearby.

**Vehicle vs. bicycle** — A vehicle-versus-bicycle collision was reported on White Lane between Monitor and Union avenues, drawing a response.

**Domestic dispute** — A caller said her sister-in-law's ex-boyfriend would not let her leave a residence on Chester Lane; officers responded as the situation escalated.

## The log

- **Copper theft** — People have been cutting down utility poles in the Bell Terrace area to pull copper wire from them, authorities said.
- **Street takeover** — Roughly 30 vehicles gathered in a Colony Drive parking lot before units dispersed them.
- **Residential alarm** — A panic alarm at an Adelaide Avenue home was triggered over a man outside; officers also checked a report of a similar man nearby.

## Night notes

Dry conditions kept fire crews busy through the small hours, with the Pozo Creek blaze burning in hard-to-reach terrain. No rain was in the forecast for Kern County on Saturday, and the overnight calls were dominated by the pursuit, the gunshot response and the brush fire.
