# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

The last night of the long Labor Day weekend was a quiet one for dispatchers, dominated by firework complaints that kept crews checking neighborhoods into the early hours, a handful of welfare checks, and a couple of more serious calls — a reported rural burglary near 230th and Gaskell and the theft of a semi truck from northwest Bakersfield. Here's what came over the scanner from Sunday evening into Monday morning.

## Around the county

**Loud boom shakes La Cresta area** — Residents near La Cresta Avenue called in a loud blast shortly before 10 p.m., several saying it was strong enough to shake their doors open. Multiple neighbors reported the same boom, and crews treated it as a firework report as others across the city logged firework complaints through the evening.

**Rural burglary near 230th and Gaskell** — Deputies were sent to a property near 230th Street and Gaskell Avenue in the early hours after three people in dark clothing were reported cutting through a chain-link fence toward heavy equipment and storage sheds. The group was said to have arrived in a pickup and an SUV pulling flatbed trailers.

**Semi truck stolen from northwest Bakersfield** — A white 2018 Freightliner semi truck was reported stolen from a property in northwest Bakersfield, with the owner telling officers a firearm was inside the cab when it was taken.

**Possible DUI crash** — A two-vehicle collision left both cars off the roadway shortly before midnight. No injuries were reported, and one driver was taken into custody on suspicion of driving under the influence.

## Night notes

The holiday weekend wound down quietly, with firework calls the most common complaint through the evening and overnight hours. Routine welfare checks and a handful of domestic calls made up much of the rest, while the two more serious calls of the night — a reported burglary at a rural property and the theft of a semi truck — drew the biggest responses.
