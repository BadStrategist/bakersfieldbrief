# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Fire crews answered calls on two fronts late Tuesday, one burning in brushy terrain off Gephardt Road and another beside Dreyer's Ice Cream on District Boulevard, while a person found not breathing at a Buck Owens Boulevard restaurant drew a heavy emergency response in the early hours. Here's what came over the scanner.

## The big one

**Overdose at Denny's, Buck Owens Boulevard** — Fire and police units responded to a Denny's on Buck Owens Boulevard shortly before 4 a.m. after a person was reported not breathing in an apparent fentanyl overdose. The caller was transferred to fire crews for CPR instructions, and an ambulance was called to the scene.

## Around the county

**Vehicle into wall, Silver Crossing Street** — A vehicle crashed into a wall in the 5500 block of Silver Crossing Street just after midnight, drawing a full fire assignment alongside Bakersfield police. Both occupants were out of the vehicle when crews arrived.

**Fire off Gephardt Road** — A fire was reported off Gephardt Road, reachable by a dirt road that passes abandoned structures. Crews headed in while command kept a visual on the flames from a distance.

**Fire near Dreyer's Ice Cream, District Boulevard** — Firefighters were sent to the 7300 block of District Boulevard after a fire was reported next to the ice cream shop, drawing a response from the county.

**Person with knife, Goodman Street** — Officers responded to a report of a person armed with a knife on the Goodman Street block and moved to make an arrest.

**Assault, North Chester Avenue** — Officers responded to an alley off North Chester Avenue near Washington Avenue after a report that two men were beating a victim on the ground, one of them holding an unknown object.

**Burglary in progress, White Lane** — Deputies responded to a White Lane home after a caller reported a person entering through the roof. A second subject in a ski mask was also described, and crews requested a ladder while a K9 was sought, though one was not available.

## The log

- **Water rescue, Miracle Hot Springs area** — A medical aid call sent crews to the hot springs east of Bakersfield after a person went down while diving, prompting a squad response.
- **Medical aid, Brentwood Drive** — Firefighters were sent to Brentwood Drive for a medical aid call involving a person reported possibly unconscious.
- **Disabled truck, Interstate 5 near Grapevine** — A box truck carrying about 20,000 pounds was stopped in the slow lane northbound on Interstate 5 with mechanical issues, and a tow was arranged.
- **Homicide-related check, East 19th at Baker** — An officer flagged an area near East 19th and Baker streets as potentially connected to a homicide investigation, and units moved in to check the scene.
- **Disturbance, Beardsley Avenue** — Officers responded to Beardsley Avenue after a dispute in which one person was reportedly attempting to take another's vehicle.
- **Disturbance, Buena Vista Boulevard** — Officers responded after a report of a man under the influence knocking on doors near nearby residences; a suspect was detained.

## Night notes

Fire activity picked up toward midnight with two separate responses keeping county crews moving into the late hours. The busiest stretch came around 4 a.m., when a medical emergency at an all-night restaurant drew fire, police and an ambulance to Buck Owens Boulevard. Temperatures stayed warm and dry overnight, keeping conditions favorable for brush fires in the outlying areas.
