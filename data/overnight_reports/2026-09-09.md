# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Fire crews handled two separate fires late Tuesday evening, one burning in brushy terrain off Gephardt Road and another reported beside Dreyer's Ice Cream on District Boulevard. Officers also moved in on a person reported armed with a knife on Goodman Street. Here's what came over the scanner.

## Around the county

**Fire off Gephardt Road** — A fire was reported off Gephardt Road, reachable by a dirt road that passes abandoned structures. Crews headed in while command kept a visual on the flames from a distance.

**Fire near Dreyer's Ice Cream, District Boulevard** — Firefighters were sent to the 7300 block of District Boulevard after a fire was reported next to the ice cream shop, drawing a response from the county.

**Person with knife, Goodman Street** — Officers responded to a report of a person armed with a knife on the Goodman Street block and moved to make an arrest.

## The log

- **Traffic stop turned medical aid, SR-58 near MLK** — A CHP traffic stop eastbound on State Route 58 turned into a medical emergency when a family member in the stopped vehicle needed aid; units cleared the scene.
- **Welfare check, Bakersfield** — Dispatchers handled a welfare check involving a Regional Center client who expressed suicidal thoughts but had no access to medication; units cleared the call.

## Night notes

Fire activity picked up toward midnight, with two separate responses keeping county crews moving into the late hours. Temperatures stayed warm and dry overnight, keeping conditions favorable for brush fires in the outlying areas.
