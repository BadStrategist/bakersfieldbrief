# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A report of someone inside a vacant Bakersfield unit turned into a search for two people who fled toward the north road, and fire crews handled smoke inside a lodging room plus a reported structure fire before dawn. Here's what came over the scanner overnight.

## The big one

**Search after screams reported on Mohawk Street** — Officers were sent to the Mohawk Street block at 2:16 a.m. after a caller reported someone inside a vacant unit, then heard a loud argument, a woman screaming for help and a man yelling. Two subjects were seen on a rear landing before fleeing toward the north road, and a search of the area followed. Descriptions of both were broadcast to responding units.

## Around the county

**Smoke inside a lodging room** — A caller reported smoke coming from inside a room at a Bakersfield lodging property around 9:20 p.m. Crews arrived to confirm a small fire in a restroom and handled it.

**Motorcycle crash at Hart Park** — A passerby flagged down a park ranger about a motorcycle crash just beyond the east entrance of Hart Park. A fire engine was sent, but crews found no patient at the scene and the sheriff's office remained to handle the call.

**Bicyclist struck on the Olive Drive overpass** — An officer working the Olive Drive overpass called for a tow after a bicyclist was involved in a collision there, with traffic moving eastbound at the time.

**Structure fire response before dawn** — Units investigating a call at 2:27 a.m. requested a reinforced structure fire response and handled it as a structure fire.

## The log

- **Criminal threats, Linden Avenue near Magnolia** — A caller reported a man in a black BMW made threats toward them.
- **Disturbance, Bakersfield** — Officers were sent to a home where the victim was reported to be inside an attached garage.
- **Disturbance, Meeks Avenue block** — Two units responded to a disturbance between a woman and her ex-husband.
- **Hazardous condition, Barstow Road in Mojave** — Crews were sent to an apartment complex for an abandoned barbecue pit.
- **Welfare check, Bakersfield** — Officers responded after a caller reported a family member making threatening statements; the man reportedly told dispatchers he had firearms and explosives.
- **Assault with a pole, North Lincoln Street north of Pierce Road** — Deputies searched for a shirtless man in his 30s who was said to still be in the area.
- **Disturbance, Beardsley Avenue block** — A woman's ex-boyfriend fled northbound toward the airport in an older black SUV.
- **Criminal threats, Water Street apartment block** — A man reportedly threatened to shoot a victim's boyfriend and was seen leaving on foot to the east.
- **Hazardous condition, Teal Street block** — Firefighters were sent to a unit for a water heater leaking into the home.

## Night notes

The night's call load leaned on disturbances and welfare checks, with a string of domestic-related responses spread between 10 p.m. and 3 a.m. Fire crews handled smoke inside a lodging room in the evening and a reported structure fire before dawn, while a water heater leak damaged a Teal Street home. Outlying communities stayed busy too: a hazard call in Mojave and a motorcycle crash near Hart Park both drew responses.
