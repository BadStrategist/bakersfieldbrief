# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Crews battled a fully involved house fire in the city before dawn, a pedestrian was struck on Edison Highway, and officers worked two weapon-related assaults on the same night. Here's what came over the scanner.

## The big one

**Fully involved house fire** — Firefighters arrived at a single-story, single-family home shortly before 4 a.m. and found it fully involved. The first-arriving engine pulled an attack line while incoming companies secured a water supply and pulled a backup line, and a truck company worked on vertical ventilation and utilities. A battalion chief took command of the fire, which drew multiple engines and a truck company to the scene.

## Around the county

**Pedestrian struck on Edison Highway** — Fire companies and an ambulance were sent to a vehicle versus pedestrian collision on the Edison Highway block at 2:01 a.m. The California Highway Patrol took over the investigation.

**Fight on Allen Road leaves one person down** — Officers were sent to the Allen Road block at 1:29 a.m. after a report of a fight between two brothers, one of them armed with a metal stick. A caller reported one person was down and that several people were involved.

**Glass bottle used in attack on McDonald Way** — A woman told officers her ex-husband broke a window to get inside her home on the McDonald Way block and tried to stab her boyfriend with a glass bottle. The call came in at 1:01 a.m., and units searched the area after the caller said he jumped a fence on the west side of the property.

**Burglary in progress on Park Drive** — Deputies were sent to the Park Drive block at 1:26 a.m. after a caller reported a man trying to break into a residence while a woman inside spoke with him.

## Night notes

Fire activity anchored the back half of the night: a single-story home was fully involved when crews arrived just before 4 a.m., and a battalion chief ran the fire with multiple engines and a truck company. Traffic on the southeast side was disrupted at 2:01 a.m. when a pedestrian was struck on Edison Highway, drawing a CHP investigation. Between 9 p.m. and 1:30 a.m., police and deputies worked a run of domestic violence, assault and welfare calls across the metro, including two weapon-related assaults and two domestic violence calls within about three hours.
