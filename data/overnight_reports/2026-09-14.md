# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A vegetation fire along the railroad tracks north of Highway 58 pulled a full wildland assignment before dawn, a chase through town ended with a neighborhood search, and crews spent the evening on a brush fire in the river bottom. Here's what came over the scanner.

## The big one

**Vegetation fire near the railroad tracks north of Highway 58** — Fire crews were sent to a vegetation fire near the Union Pacific Railroad tracks north of Highway 58 just after 5 a.m., and the assignment grew fast. Two engines, three patrol units, a water tender and two dozers were dispatched, along with multiple battalion chiefs, a division supervisor and a safety officer. A helicopter was placed on standby and air-to-ground coordination was requested if needed, with additional strike-team resources held ready.

## Around the county

**Chase through town ends in a parking lot** — A pursuit ran westbound along California Avenue at 12:09 a.m. with speeds called out from about 60 to 95 mph, dispatchers noting light traffic and no pedestrians. The vehicle turned off the avenue, went north and pulled into a residence parking lot, where units moved in.

**Search through a neighborhood after a pursuit** — Around 2:11 a.m., officers searched a backyard and an attic after a pursuit ended at a home. One person was reported to be in the backyard, a second was last seen walking west, and a K-9 and additional units joined as officers fanned out on foot and checked spots where someone could hide.

**Brush fire in the river bottom** — A fire burning in heavy brush and grass in a riparian area was reported at roughly a quarter- to half-acre around 10:37 p.m., moving slowly while crews worked out how to reach it. Access from the river side was being weighed and additional resources were requested as the fire stayed in heavy fuels.

**Semi crash blocks lanes on Interstate 5** — A tractor-trailer went down in the number four lane of southbound Interstate 5 around 4:06 a.m. with the driver out putting out warning triangles, and a second semi was on the right shoulder. Units reported the crash and asked for a heavy-duty tow.

## The log

- **Domestic disturbance, Roseman Mobile Home Park** — Deputies were sent at 12:02 a.m. to a disturbance between a woman and her partner; the caller reported the man had access to a firearm and was refusing to let her leave.
- **Domestic battery report, Quincy Street block** — Officers took a report at 12:01 a.m. in which a juvenile said her mother battered her and put a knee on her neck.
- **Shots heard, central Bakersfield** — Officers were sent at 1:02 a.m. to investigate a report of shots heard; a related call was tied to a prior weapons report.
- **Open-line call, Delwood Avenue block** — A line came in at 3:55 a.m. with a woman asking someone to get off of her; it disconnected, a callback went unanswered, and officers checked the area.
- **Disturbance, Stine Road block** — Deputies were sent at 11:06 p.m. to an argument over a cell phone at a Stine Road address.
- **Theft report, Ming Avenue block** — A theft at a home improvement store was passed to patrol units at 11:02 p.m. with a suspect vehicle description and left pending.
- **Large gathering at a lake area** — Deputies requested a routine backup at 11:38 p.m. at a lake recreation area where about 60 other vehicles had gathered.
- **Suspicious circumstance, Panama Lane at Mountain Vista Drive** — A caller at 11:54 p.m. reported a bright light coming from an electrical box and believed someone was inside it.
- **Armed robbery investigation** — A caller at 10:15 p.m. reported being the victim of a recent armed robbery and gave a suspect description.
- **Disturbance, convenience store parking lot** — Officers were sent at 11:01 p.m. for a man with an open alcohol container who was refusing to leave and harassing customers.
- **Welfare check, Main Street block in Maricopa** — A deputy was assigned at 1:44 a.m. to check on a resident.
- **Disabled car on Interstate 5** — A car with a flat tire and no spare was reported in the center divide south of Grapevine at 1:19 a.m.; the driver declined a tow.
- **Suspicious subjects, Niles Street at Ashe Road** — Officers were sent at 4:20 a.m. for two people dressed in all black.
- **Assault investigation** — Officers took a call at 4:39 a.m. involving two 18-year-olds, a male and a female.

## Night notes

Fire drove both ends of the night: crews worked a slow-moving brush fire in the river bottom through the late evening, then a separate vegetation fire near the railroad tracks north of Highway 58 pulled engines, dozers, a water tender and a helicopter on standby just after 5 a.m. Two pursuits ran in the first three hours of the morning, and the second one turned into a neighborhood search with a K-9. Domestic and welfare calls ran from 11 p.m. through the early morning, including two domestic disturbances in the midnight hour and an open-line call at 3:55 a.m.
