# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

An assault with a baseball bat on the east side, a welfare check that deputies had to find through a phone ping, a rollover on Interstate 5 that was gone by the time crews arrived and a vehicle fire on Mount Vernon Avenue were the calls that stood out Sunday evening. Kern crews also helped Los Angeles County on a grass fire near the county line, and units worked a gas leak, a roadway hazard and a pair of welfare checks. Here is what came over the scanner.

## Around the county

**Assault with a bat**: Just after 11 p.m. a caller reported an assault with a baseball bat that had happened at Flower Street and Lincoln Street. The caller waited nearby for officers, and a suspect description went out over the radio.

**Deputies track a welfare check through a phone ping**: Late Sunday evening a woman told dispatchers her husband had said he planned to kill himself. Deputies were sent to the area east of Bakersfield, with the location found through a ping on the man's phone, and units were checking the area.

**Rollover on Interstate 5**: A rescue, engine, truck and battalion assignment went out with CHP for a reported rollover on southbound Interstate 5 south of Bakersfield. The first crew on scene reported the vehicle had rolled over and then left, and the rest of the response was cancelled.

**Vehicle fire on Mount Vernon Avenue**: Fire crews were sent for a gray SUV burning on Mount Vernon Avenue.

## The log

- **9:33 p.m.** Vegetation fire check, Interstate 5 near the Grapevine. Units reported nothing showing on the southbound side and the vegetation response was cancelled.
- **9:57 p.m.** Gas leak, Kern County. Crews were sent to a home for a report of gas coming from inside, possibly from the stove, and the homeowner said they could not turn it off. The response was cut to a single engine.
- **10:04 p.m.** Roadway hazard, Wilson Road, Bakersfield. A unit went back to clear debris out of the roadway.
- **10:11 p.m.** Grass fire, Highway 138 at Highway 14. Los Angeles County dispatchers asked Kern units to respond, and an engine and a water tender were sent.
- **10:51 p.m.** Disturbance, 300 block of Chester Lane, Bakersfield. A unit was sent to a disturbance at an apartment.
- **11:02 p.m.** Welfare check, 2100 block of Coventry Drive, Bakersfield. Deputies and an ambulance were sent to check on someone at a home.
- **11:06 p.m.** Traffic hazard, Highway 178 at Alfred Harrell Highway, Bakersfield. Three vehicles pulling jet skis were reported parked in the westbound lanes.
- **11:52 p.m.** Public assist, 800 block of 8th Street, Bakersfield. Officers were sent to assist at a home.

## Night notes

The busiest stretch of the evening came between 9 and 11 p.m., when crews ran a gas leak, checked the southbound side of Interstate 5 near the Grapevine and answered a mutual aid request from Los Angeles County for a grass fire at Highway 138 and Highway 14. Sheriff's deputies spent part of the evening working a welfare check they had to locate through a phone ping, and CHP handled two roadway hazards on the southbound side of Interstate 5. The rest of the night stayed calm, with calls clearing without anyone hurt.
