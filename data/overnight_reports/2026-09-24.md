# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A garage fire in southeast Bakersfield brought a reinforced structure assignment at 2:21 a.m., a vacant double-wide trailer burned at a Rosamond mobile home park at 1:37 a.m., and crews worked heavy smoke at a former discount store on Niles Street before midnight. Fires did most of the work overnight. Police traffic ran to disturbances, suspicious-person reports, a shots-heard call and a relayed domestic violence report from a hotel, and it thinned out after 3 a.m.

## The big one

****Garage fire on C Street draws four engines**: At 2:21 a.m. dispatchers sent a reinforced structure assignment for a garage on fire across the street from an address in the 2200 block of C Street, with multiple callers reporting the fire on the same block. Four engines, two truck companies, a rescue and two battalions were included in the first response. Units held the address until a crew cleared the scene shortly after 3 a.m.

## Around the county

**Vacant trailer burns at a Rosamond mobile home park**: At 1:37 a.m. crews were sent to a mobile home park on Rosamond Boulevard for multiple reports of a trailer on fire. The first medic squad on scene found a fully involved double-wide trailer at the back of the park with no exposures, described as a vacant trailer that had been left outside the park on a graded lot. Crews knocked the fire down with the first tank of water and held units for water supply. A county engine and battalion added to the call were cancelled once the scene was under control.

**Heavy smoke at the former Family Dollar on Niles Street**: At 11:46 p.m. a battalion and a city battalion were sent to heavy smoke coming from the former Family Dollar building at Niles Street and Mount Vernon Avenue. Crews reported a heavy smoke condition on arrival, and by 11:56 p.m. dispatchers had confirmation that the primary and secondary searches were complete and the building was all clear.

**Grass fire along the Highway 58 to Highway 99 on-ramp**: At 10:39 p.m. crews went out for smoke seen near the freeway and found a grass fire along the on-ramp from Highway 58 to Highway 99. An engine checked the area behind a nearby discount store and reported nothing burning there.

**Overdose call at the gate of Kern Valley State Prison**: At 12:59 a.m. an engine and an ambulance were sent to the gate of Kern Valley State Prison in Delano for a reported overdose. The ambulance was asked to stage at the gate in case transport was needed.

## Night notes

The night belonged to fire crews, with the trailer fire in the desert at 1:37 a.m., the garage fire on C Street at 2:21 a.m., and the smoke call at the former discount store on Niles Street all coming inside a four-hour window. Police traffic after 3 a.m. was down to a suspicious person with a flashlight, a gas odor investigation on Custer Avenue and two people reported messing with construction equipment near Hageman Road. The last hour before dawn was quiet.
