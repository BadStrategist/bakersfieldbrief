# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A structure fire in an alley behind Flower Street brought crews out shortly before 1 a.m., two vehicle fires burned at opposite ends of the county in the pre-dawn hours, a grass fire drew multiple reports southeast of the city, and deputies and officers worked a reported firearm at a Union Avenue motel, a crowbar assault and a family dispute that turned into a vehicle ramming call. The county ran a heavy fire shift and a moderate police shift, with most of the police traffic stacked before midnight and thinning out after 3 a.m.

## The big one

**Structure fire in an alley behind Flower Street at Owens Street**: At 12:50 a.m. dispatchers sent a structure assignment for a fire in an alley at Flower Street and Owens Street, and arriving companies were routed in from the alley side. A correction from the scene put the fire in the alley behind the 1000 block of Flower Street. Units reported all companies on scene and stood by while crews checked the building for extension, and the assignment held the address after the initial attack.

## Around the county

**Grass fire at East Bear Mountain Boulevard draws multiple reports**: At 4 a.m. crews were sent to a grass fire described as south of the 48th station area at East Bear Mountain Boulevard. Dispatchers took more than one call to the same area and passed along a report that the fire could reach an electrical pole, and an engine, two patrols and a battalion were assigned. Crews on Interstate 5 reported being just south of the fire.

**Assault with a crowbar reported to police**: At 2:47 a.m. a victim told dispatchers he had been struck with a crowbar by a man in his 20s wearing all black clothing. The report was taken for follow-up.

**Person reported with a firearm at a Union Avenue motel**: At 12:58 a.m. officers were sent to a motel on Union Avenue for a report of a person with a firearm. A description went out to units working the call, along with word that the subject was moving on foot, and officers were asked to coordinate on arrival.

**Reported firearm in a five-against-one disturbance**: At 10:26 p.m. officers were sent to a disturbance that dispatchers described as five or six females against one person, with one female reported to be holding a firearm. The group was associated with a black pickup parked in front of a residence, and the call had started as a report of a physical fight.

## The log

- **10:10 p.m.**: Disturbance, 100 block of 20th Street West, Bakersfield. A caller could hear subjects yelling outside a business and employees trying to keep two parties apart, with the details relayed secondhand from the manager.
- **10:17 p.m.**: Disturbance, 2700 block of Holland Street, Bakersfield. Officers were sent for a report of loud music.
- **11:18 p.m.**: Threats, Kern County. Units were advised before making contact that a subject had a prior mental-health-related call history and had said on that earlier call that she would shoot the people involved, and that she had a registered firearm.
- **11:35 p.m.**: Medical aid, Ashe Road at Panama Lane, Bakersfield. A fire unit was asked to respond to a caller requesting help.
- **11:40 p.m.**: Stolen vehicle, 1000 block of McDonald Way, Bakersfield. A reported stolen 2000 Honda Accord, painted primer black by hand, was recovered and cleared.
- **11:58 p.m.**: Disturbance, Roberts Lane at Central Avenue, Bakersfield. A caller reported that a family member in an older white vehicle was trying to hit her vehicle, and the call was still current when it went out to units.
- **2:00 a.m.**: Welfare check, Merced Avenue west of Highway 65, Bakersfield. A truck driver asked deputies to check on a person who appeared to be trying to flag down his rig, described as wearing a black jacket and beige shorts.
- **3:27 a.m.**: Medical aid, Oak Street at Brundage Lane, Bakersfield. An engine was sent near a discount store for two patients, with the caller reporting both were unresponsive and asking that responding units bring medication.
- **3:48 a.m.**: Vehicle fire, northbound Interstate 5 north of Gorman School Road. Engine 56 was sent on an outer-county assist for a vehicle fire and responded alongside an out-of-county engine.
- **4:16 a.m.**: Suspicious circumstances, Bakersfield area. A caller reported hearing a man in an alley yelling for help and saying someone had a gun, and said it had happened about ten minutes earlier.
- **4:45 a.m.**: Medical aid, 4400 block of Columbus Street, Bakersfield. Crews were sent for a medical aid with an ambulance responding from Lamont.
- **5:23 a.m.**: Vehicle fire, northwest of Hermosa Road, Kern County. A vehicle fire was assigned to the Station 46 area, with a deputy unit reporting a person seen, and crews asked for an updated closure estimate from the state highway department.

## Night notes

Fire crews carried the night, working a structure fire in an alley behind Flower Street before 1 a.m., a vehicle fire on the northbound side of Interstate 5 near Gorman on an outer-county assist, a second vehicle fire northwest of Hermosa Road before dawn and a grass fire that drew multiple reports at East Bear Mountain Boulevard. Police traffic peaked before midnight with a disturbance outside a 20th Street West business, a reported firearm at a Union Avenue motel and a five-against-one fight, then settled into a crowbar assault, a welfare check on Merced Avenue and a pair of medical aids. The two hours before dawn were the quietest stretch of the shift.
