# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Officers with helicopter support chased suspects who fled on foot across a property in Bakersfield, and fire crews worked a pair of vehicles that struck power poles in eastern Kern. Here's what came over the scanner overnight.

## The big one

Units were sent to a shooting southeast of Bakersfield just after 1:30 a.m., where dispatchers relayed that one victim had a gunshot wound to the arm and that there was no suspect description. An ambulance was routed to the scene and units were given the go-ahead to enter. Crews working the call also reported a gas leak inside the structure, and other units were started toward the scene to stage.

## Around the county

**Possible Structure Fire — senior living complex, Brookside Drive area, Bakersfield**
Engines, a truck and a battalion were sent to a senior living complex in the Brookside Drive area after a burnt smell coming from two vents and a fog of smoke inside one of the buildings were reported. Crews reported smoke inside the structure and worked the building, asking that the main breaker for the condenser unit on the east side be shut down while they investigated. About 50 to 60 vehicles remained parked in the lot during the response.

**Strong-Arm Robbery — 21st Street at Chester Avenue, downtown Bakersfield**
Downtown units were sent to a strong-arm robbery at 21st Street and Chester Avenue. The person reporting the robbery said a phone was taken from her, and the suspect briefly left in a black SUV before coming back to the scene, where the argument was still going as officers arrived.

**Large Fight — park area, Kern County**
Deputies were sent to a park area in the county for a disturbance reported to involve about 20 juveniles with unknown weapons and several subjects arguing near the park. A fire engine that had been started toward the call was canceled.

**Kidnapping Report — 10500 block of Meacham Road, Kern County**
Deputies were called to a kidnapping report in the 10500 block of Meacham Road. The person reporting told dispatchers the juveniles involved had gone missing again after entering through a side entrance of the property, and the suspect was still outstanding.

**Missing Child — carnival area, Bakersfield**
Units took a report of a missing 6-year-old girl last seen near the kids' carnival, wearing a pink cowboy T-shirt. Deputies were also told that a subject they were looking for was part of a missing juvenile case out of the agency.

**Person On A Moving Vehicle — 4500 block of Columbus Street, Bakersfield**
A priority welfare check in the 4500 block of Columbus Street turned into a report of someone on the hood of a car while the driver moved the vehicle backward, trying to throw her off. Units were sent to the location.

**Domestic Violence — Park Drive area, Kern County**
Deputies were sent to a domestic violence report in the Park Drive area, where a caller said a man 52 years old had kicked her in the ribs and punched her in the face. The caller reported the man was in an RV on the property, and dispatchers passed a description of him to responding units.
