# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Two police chases kept officers busy before dawn, one of them ending in a crash on South Union Avenue and a foot search through backyards, and a stabbing sent deputies to Oildale's Linda Vista area. A carnival drew a run of missing-child calls and a fight, crews handled vehicle fires on the southwest side, and a man was reported throwing knives and striking a relative with a bat at a home on Oregon Street. The rest of the log was medical aids, alarms and traffic checks.

## The big one

**Chase ends in a crash and a foot search in south Bakersfield**: Just after midnight a silver sedan reported at about 80 mph on southbound Highway 99 led officers onto city streets. The driver turned south on South H Street, went east on McKee Road and hit a dead end just east of Highway 99, made a U-turn, then crashed into the median on South Union Avenue near the 7500 block. He ran west along a fence line into a residential block near Acoma Way, and a sheriff's air unit followed him over backyards near Lazy River Drive before losing sight of him behind a home. Officers set a perimeter and worked through the yards.

## Around the county

**Second chase ends with three in custody**: At 12:38 a.m. a traffic stop on westbound Niles Street turned into a chase when a vehicle reported to be carrying at least five people took off. It ran east on Pioneer Drive through a red light at Fairfax Road, turned north on Meadows, and ended near the 4900 block of Pioneer Drive, where the occupants jumped a fence and ran. Officers detained three people and reported one still outstanding.

**Knives and a bat at an Oregon Street home**: At 11:27 p.m. officers were sent to an apartment on the 3900 block of Oregon Street after a caller reported her brother was having a mental breakdown, was throwing knives around, and had hit another relative with a baseball bat. A verbal argument could be heard in the background and the call drew a heavy police and fire response.

**Stabbing call in Oildale**: At 9:06 p.m. deputies were sent to the Linda Vista area for a stabbing. The caller reported a man had stab wounds to his arm and that the man's girlfriend was with him, and a suspect was reported leaving in a white Chevrolet Silverado.

**Missing-child calls at a carnival**: Through the evening a carnival drew a run of missing-child reports. A 3-year-old girl in a Dodgers jersey was reported missing and found at the main gate, a 9-year-old boy was missing for about 15 minutes before he was located, a 2-year-old was reported missing before being found, and an 8-year-old girl was reported missing later in the night. Officers also handled a fight between two groups of women at the Ferris wheel.

## Night notes

Chases and weapons calls carried the night, with the two pursuits before dawn and the knife-and-bat call on Oregon Street drawing the heaviest responses. Fire crews stayed busy with vehicle fires on the southwest side and a small grass fire in the Copas Road area, and officers spent much of the evening chasing missing-child reports across a carnival. Traffic was routine apart from several semis reported swerving on Highway 99 and Interstate 5. Details remain preliminary until confirmed by an official source.
