# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A shooting in north Bakersfield sent two people to the hospital with gunshot wounds, drawing a large police, fire and ambulance response to a block on Pembroke Avenue just after 1 a.m. Deputies in the same hour worked a knife attack on Lincoln Street where a man chased a woman down the street and a sister put herself in front of the blade, and officers took a man with a pocket knife into custody outside the Food Maxx on Niles Street. Earlier in the night, deputies in the Frazier Park area handled a strong-arm robbery at a travel stop on Taft Highway, and a stolen white Honda Accord brought a foot chase through a College Avenue neighborhood. The rest of the night was alarms, crashes, disturbances and medical aids.

## The big one

**Two shot on Pembroke Avenue in north Bakersfield**: Just after 1:20 a.m., officers and firefighters were sent to a residence in the 5400 block of Pembroke Avenue for a report of a shooting. Crews found two patients with gunshot wounds and quickly set up a perimeter around the block, holding family members back while units moved in. Hall Ambulance staged to enter the scene from the east while officers asked firefighters to light up the roof to check it for evidence. The scene was secured and turned over to investigators, and the department had not released any details on the victims' conditions.

## Around the county

**Knife attack on Lincoln Street leaves injuries in question**: Deputies were sent to the 1500 block of Lincoln Street for a fight involving a knife. Callers reported a man chasing a woman down the street and attacking others, and radio traffic indicated his mother was threatened with the knife and a sister stepped in front of it. Deputies found the suspect in a cluttered shed area at the back corner of the property and were still sorting out who was hurt. A separate 911 call from nearby reported only a verbal disturbance, and the woman on that call denied anything on the callback.

**Stolen car ends in a foot chase near College Avenue**: Officers picked up a newer white Honda Accord reported stolen and moved to stop it near College Avenue and Flint Ridge. About four people bailed out and ran into an open garage, which was then pulled shut, and officers ringed the block in case the car started moving again. One man was described running southbound in a gray shirt, black shorts and a face covering toward Culver and Pioneer. A second stolen-vehicle report, a white Honda Accord seen near Niles Road and McCurdy Drive, came in the same night.

**Man with a knife taken into custody outside a Niles Street grocery store**: Officers responded to the Food Maxx on Niles Street for a man with a knife, and a negotiator opened a line with him, calling for him to put the weapon down. The man was described as white, in his late thirties, dressed in black and carrying a pocket knife. Units were moved in from Lamont to cover the area while the call was worked.

**Strong-arm robbery outside a Taft Highway travel stop**: Deputies were sent to the Lowe's Travel Stop on Taft Highway for a strong-arm robbery that had just happened. The suspect was described as a 44-year-old man in a black shirt and gray pants who was still in the area at a bus stop near the Southland RV Park. The caller waited nearby to meet with deputies.
