# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A machete robbery at a Dollar Tree on Mount Vernon Avenue was the sharpest call of the night, with two suspects running off toward the lot next door. A crash on southbound Interstate 5 north of the rest area ejected two people from a vehicle, held traffic while crews worked, and sent one patient out by air. Deputies and firefighters spent the evening looking for a man and four friends who never came back from a dirt bike ride off Lancer Drive, and in Wasco a driver who tried to run from a crash was caught and detained. The rest of the night ran from a pedestrian struck on Main Avenue and a house fire in the county to a wrong-way driver on Highway 58 and a pickup taken from el Sereno Drive, with medical aids scattered across the county.

## The big one

**Armed robbery at a Mount Vernon Avenue store**: Officers were sent to a robbery in progress at the Dollar Tree on Mount Vernon Avenue in which the weapon was a machete. Two suspects were last seen on foot heading toward the fast-food lot next door. No arrests were reported, and units were still working the area.

## Around the county

**Crash on southbound Interstate 5 ejects two people**: A crash on southbound Interstate 5 north of the rest area left two people ejected from a vehicle. Traffic was held while crews worked the scene, and one patient was moved by air with the other taken by ground.

**Search for a man and four friends overdue from a dirt bike ride**: A caller reported that her husband and four of his friends had gone dirt biking in the area and that none of them had returned or answered their phones. Crews were sent to the Lancer Drive area for a possible search and rescue.

**Rollover on Rosedale Highway at Jenkins Road**: A vehicle rolled over on northbound Rosedale Highway just north of Jenkins Road, in northwest Bakersfield. Crews checked the area, and a reported black or blue sedan on the right shoulder could not be located.

**Crash in Wasco ends with the driver detained**: Wasco police handled a crash in which the driver tried to flee and was detained. There were no people pinned, and the driver complained of generalized pain.

## Night notes

Crashes carried a share of the night, with rollovers on both sides of Interstate 5, a wrong-way driver on Highway 58, and pedestrian strikes reported within minutes of each other on Main Avenue and Beechwood Way. Fire crews handled a house fire in the county, a vegetation fire burning in several spots, and a homeless camp fire that reached the grass. The sharper calls were the machete robbery on Mount Vernon Avenue and the search for five dirt bikers who had not come home.
