# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A rollover on the southbound Highway 204 connector onto Highway 99 was the biggest call of the night, bringing four patients and a second ambulance to the scene. A driver went off Highway 155 in the Kern River canyon and rolled over with poor cell phone contact, brush burned along eastbound Highway 58 near South Union Avenue, and firefighters found light smoke at a house fire on North Kern Street, in east Bakersfield. A man on a motorized bike who was reported to be carrying a machete fled eastbound on Beardsley Avenue, in north Bakersfield, before officers could catch up with him, and a caller reported impact sounds at a building on Westwind Drive, in central Bakersfield, followed by a sedan leaving the area. The rest of the night ran from a debris pile burning in Wasco and a driver who went off the road after hitting the mountain in the canyon to a car window broken by an object thrown from a semi on Highway 166, a stolen pickup out of Los Angeles, and medical aids scattered across the county.

## The big one

**Four patients in a rollover on the Highway 204 connector**: A single vehicle rolled over on the southbound Highway 204 connector onto Highway 99. Fire crews called for a second ambulance after reporting four patients, all tagged green for minor injuries, and canceled one engine once the scene was under control.

## Around the county

**Vehicle goes off Highway 155 in the Kern River canyon**: A vehicle hit the front of the mountain and rolled over on northbound Highway 155 about a mile south of Hungry Gulch, north of Boulder Gulch in the canyon. Radio traffic described poor cell phone reception with the patient, and the wrecked vehicle was reported blocking the roadway.

**Water flow at a cold storage building on Valpredo Avenue**: Firefighters were sent to a structure fire response at a cold storage building in the 5500 block of Valpredo Avenue. Truck 54 reported water flowing from one room and nothing from another, crews laddered the building looking for a heat source, and they got inside the room without finding anything visible.

**Brush fire along eastbound Highway 58 near South Union Avenue**: Multiple callers reported brush burning along eastbound Highway 58 near South Union Avenue. County engines were joined by city equipment sent to the grass fire.

**Machete report sends officers to Beardsley Avenue**: Officers were sent to Beardsley Avenue, in north Bakersfield, for a disturbance involving a man on a motorized bike who was reported to be carrying a machete. He was last seen fleeing eastbound on the bike with a checkered backpack, and he was not located.

## Night notes

Fire crews carried a share of the night, with a water flow at a cold storage building on Valpredo Avenue, brush burning along eastbound Highway 58, a debris pile in Wasco, a small grass fire near an auto parts store at East Brundage Lane and Oswell Street, light smoke at a house fire on North Kern Street, and an outside fire on the curb at Roberts Lane and Airport Drive. Highway calls ran from the four-patient rollover on the Highway 204 connector and the rollover in the Kern River canyon to a semi throwing an object at a passing car on Highway 166 and an unbalanced trailer reported on Interstate 5. Officers and deputies also worked a man with a knife on Cherry Creek Lane, a report of a man who battered a woman and left with a juvenile in a white pickup, and welfare checks on juveniles. Details remain preliminary until they are confirmed by an official source.
