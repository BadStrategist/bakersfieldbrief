# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A man fired five shots at officers working at Monitor Street and Pacheco Road, in south Bakersfield, and went down with a gun still in his hand, then a second person ran north through the apartment complex and touched off a search with a drone and a Bearcat that ran almost an hour. After 3 a.m. a stack of pallets burned in a yard off Kernita Road and the fire reached a Public Storage, with high-tension power lines at risk and the street closed in both directions. A small apartment complex on Niles Street caught fire with children reported inside, a crash on Truxtun Avenue east of Westside Parkway left a pedestrian struck and medics tagged four patients, and crews also went to an infirmary fire at the Lerdo Justice Facility.

## The big one

**Shots fired at officers, Monitor Street and Pacheco Road, south Bakersfield**

Officers working at Monitor Street and Pacheco Road reported that a subject had fired about five shots at them, and the man was down with a gun still in his hand. A second person in a black hoodie ran northbound through the apartment complex just west of the scene, and police said three other people may have gone into the neighborhood to the north. A drone and a Bearcat were brought in, and officers worked through the buildings, the carports and the parking area by hand before reporting the complex and the neighborhood to the west clear. The call went out as an assault on an officer.

## Around the county

**Pallet yard fire that spread into a storage business, Kernita Road**

Crews went to a large stack of pallets burning in a yard off Kernita Road, with adjacent buildings and high-tension power lines in the path of the fire. Battalion 4 took command and made the power lines the priority, water was stretched from a hydrant to the west, and the response was reinforced when the fire reached the Public Storage on the property. Police were asked to shut the street down in both directions while hose lines were laid through the aisles of the storage yard.

**Apartment fire, 400 block of Niles Street**

Multiple callers reported a small apartment complex on fire in the 400 block of Niles Street, with reports of people trying to save children and children screaming inside. Crews found heavy smoke venting from a second-story window of a middle unit, pulled attack lines and checked the structure next to it. A reconnaissance group evacuated the units beside the fire and PG&E was asked to isolate power to the single unit. The three units not involved were found habitable, which kept those families from being displaced.

**Crash and pedestrian struck, Truxtun Avenue east of Westside Parkway**

A vehicle crash was reported on Truxtun Avenue east of Westside Parkway, and police advised that a pedestrian had also been struck at the same location. Medics tagged four patients, a couple of people were taken to the hospital by ambulance, and one engine stayed on scene.

**Person with a knife, 7000 block of Eucalyptus Drive, east Bakersfield**

Officers were sent to the 7000 block of Eucalyptus Drive for a report of a 65-year-old man alone in an apartment and in possession of a knife. The caller had separated himself from the man before officers arrived.

## Night notes

Fire carried most of this night: the pallet yard fire off Kernita Road that reached the storage business, the apartment fire on Niles Street, the infirmary fire at the Lerdo Justice Facility, and small outside fires on Richards Avenue, at Miller Street and East California Avenue, on Stockdale Highway and off Calloway Drive. The search at Monitor Street and Pacheco Road ran nearly an hour, with a drone overhead and a Bearcat on scene, before officers reported the complex clear. Police also worked welfare and crisis calls, the Quincy Street foot chase, a carjacking report at 8th Street and Maurice Avenue and a report of a knife in an apartment on Eucalyptus Drive. Details remain preliminary until confirmed by an official source.
