# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A two-vehicle crash on East Bear Mountain Boulevard at General Beale Road in Lamont left a large amount of blood on both cars and no one who needed an ambulance, and deputies searched the wreckage and the ground around it for anyone who may have been thrown out. Fire carried the middle of the night: a mobile home in the 200 block of Oswell Street burned with moderate smoke showing and the street was closed while crews worked, a second house fire drew a full assignment to the 1400 block of Ridgeview Street in east Bakersfield, and a report of a building on fire at 34th Street and San Dimas Street came in just before dawn. A woman swung an ax at a man in the 1400 block of Calcutta Drive in south Bakersfield and then left on foot still carrying it, a driver was found unresponsive behind the wheel at White Lane and Buena Vista Road in southwest Bakersfield with CPR under way, and a man walked into his home in the 3400 block of Granada Avenue in southwest Bakersfield with blood on his hands.

## The big one

**Crash with blood and no victims found, East Bear Mountain Boulevard and General Beale Road, Lamont**

Crews and Lamont-area deputies were sent to East Bear Mountain Boulevard and General Beale Road for a two-vehicle crash, and the first units on scene found a large amount of blood on both vehicles and inside one of them, but no injured people. Deputies said the people involved claimed no one else had been riding in the cars, and they did not trust that account. The wreckage and the ground around it were searched for anyone who may have been thrown out, and a sheriff's helicopter was called to help look over the area. Deputies worked out that the crash happened in a rural stretch of the road east of town, and the investigation stayed open.

## Around the county

**Mobile home fire, 200 block of Oswell Street**

Callers reported a house on fire in the 200 block of Oswell Street. Battalion 4 arrived to find a single-wide mobile home with moderate smoke showing, named it the Oswell incident, assigned Engine 41 to pull a line as fire attack and split the rest of the assignment between water supply and search. Crews reported a knockdown a few minutes later and cancelled the second battalion and a truck. Traffic was shut down in both directions on Oswell while hose lines were laid across the road.

**Person down on the tracks, Truxtun Avenue approaching Baker Street, east Bakersfield**

A person was reported down on the railroad tracks on Truxtun Avenue approaching Baker Street in east Bakersfield. A crew came through the area at speed and reported the person was still on the tracks, and a train crew was asked how long the person had been there. Police and medics were sent to find the person, with units working both sides of the crossing.

**Woman with an ax, 1400 block of Calcutta Drive, south Bakersfield**

Officers were sent to the 1400 block of Calcutta Drive in south Bakersfield for a woman who had swung an ax at a man and then stayed in the parking lot still holding it. There were no injuries, and the man went back to his apartment. A woman on foot was later reported heading north in a plaid shirt and brown sweatpants, still with the ax, and police ran her name against a prior case before looking for her.

**Unresponsive driver, White Lane and Buena Vista Road, southwest Bakersfield**

A driver was found not responsive behind the wheel of a car at White Lane and Buena Vista Road in southwest Bakersfield, and CPR was in progress when crews upgraded the call to a life-threatening medical aid. The car was sitting partly in the eastbound lanes and the scene was held while medics worked.

**Man with blood on his hands, 3400 block of Granada Avenue, southwest Bakersfield**

Officers were sent to the 3400 block of Granada Avenue in southwest Bakersfield after a man arrived at his residence with blood on his hands, and the call went out as a possible assault. Medics were asked to respond as well, and there were numerous people outside the address when units arrived.

## Night notes

Fire ran the middle of the night: the mobile home fire on Oswell Street, house fires on Ridgeview Street and at 34th Street and San Dimas Street, and a smoke check on Minnatree Lane that turned out to be nothing. The crash on East Bear Mountain Boulevard at General Beale Road is the one still open, because crews found a large amount of blood on two wrecked cars and no injured people to account for it, and a helicopter was brought in to search the ground around the scene. Police also worked a long list of crisis and violence calls, from a man reported to have a gun on a transit bus to an ax attack on Calcutta Drive, a family fight at a home holding several firearms, and a man who walked in with blood on his hands on Granada Avenue. Details remain preliminary until confirmed by an official source.
