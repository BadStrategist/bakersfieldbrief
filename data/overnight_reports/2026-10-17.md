# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A commercial building near 30th Street and Union Avenue burned before dawn in the biggest call of the night. Fire carried the night: an apartment in the 3400 block of Wible Road in southwest Bakersfield caught fire with smoke showing from a window, and a grass fire was reported near Cottonwood and Bradshaw. Police ran a foot chase that ended in east Bakersfield and booked a person on the 1200 block of Lincoln Street, a domestic call in the 7000 block of Willis Avenue turned up a report of a gun, and officers also worked an assault in which a caller said she was held down and had her phone taken. Two crashes drew responses, one at 18th and H streets that left vehicles blocking the road and a single-car wreck on Mohawk Street that sent two people to the hospital, and a semi was reported driving the wrong way with bright headlights on Highway 58.

## The big one

**Commercial building fire, 30th Street and Union Avenue**

A commercial building near 30th Street and Union Avenue caught fire shortly after 1:15 a.m. Crews found a loading dock involved and power lines down along one corner, set up a command post at 30th and Union and closed the northbound side of the road. Firefighters knocked the fire down on the outside, then went in after the interior fire and put it out, and reported the primary search clear. No injuries were reported.

## Around the county

**Apartment fire, 3400 block of Wible Road, southwest Bakersfield**

Multiple callers reported smoke coming from an apartment window in the 3400 block of Wible Road. Truck 7, Truck 1 and two battalion companies were assigned to apartment 26, and crews knocked the fire down. The fire was in a two-story complex and drew the trucks for the upper floor.

**Foot chase, 1200 block of Lincoln Street, east Bakersfield**

A foot chase ended near Lincoln and Robinson streets, and Air 1 overhead talked units onto one person detained on the 1200 block of Lincoln Street. Officers were told the man came out of the passenger seat of a car that had been stopped, and that he had tried to get into a house before he was caught.

**Crash with the road blocked, 18th and H streets**

Two vehicles collided at 18th and H streets and came to rest blocking the roadway, with at least one car left stranded in the intersection. The crash drew police and fire, and traffic was worked around the wreckage while the tow was arranged.

**Single-car crash, Mohawk Street**

A single car left the roadway on Mohawk Street and crashed, hurting two people. Both were taken by ambulance, and the car was the only vehicle involved.

## Night notes

Fire ran the night: the commercial building on Union Avenue, the apartment on Wible Road, and a grass fire near Cottonwood and Bradshaw. The call volume was steady from the dinner hour through dawn, with a long tail of alarms, welfare checks and medical aids after the two building fires were knocked down.
