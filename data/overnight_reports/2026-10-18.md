# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Gunfire drew the night's biggest police response: callers reported shots near a court in south Bakersfield, officers found a shell casing on the ground, and two men were already gone on foot. Fire ran alongside it. Crews were sent to a structure fire at a home in Ridgecrest before dawn, to a fire behind a house on Sumner Street in east Bakersfield, and to grass and lot fires around the county. Officers also worked a robbery at a mini mart on Wible Road where the suspect ran off with cash, a fight reported in front of a Whitley Drive home with a caller counting about 40 juveniles, a vehicle against a pedestrian on Fremont Street, and a foot chase near Lincoln and Baker streets that put one person in custody.

## The big one

**Shots fired, 900 block of Karma Court, south Bakersfield**

Officers were sent to Karma Court after several callers reported shots in the area, and units were told to broadcast for officer safety as they headed in. A shell casing was found on the north side of the court. A witness told officers two men had left the area on foot to the north, and no victim was found.

## Around the county

**Structure fire, West Moyer Avenue, Ridgecrest**

Crews were sent to a structure fire at a 1,500-square-foot single-story home on West Moyer Avenue in Ridgecrest. The first engine arrived to find nothing showing and took command, and a second engine was diverted from a call on Highway 14 to help. The call drew a full assignment of engines and a battalion chief.

**Structure fire, 1000 block of Sumner Street, east Bakersfield**

Crews were sent to a structure fire reported behind a home in the 1000 block of Sumner Street, with two engines, a truck and a rescue assigned to the address.

**Robbery, 3300 block of Wible Road, southwest Bakersfield**

Officers were called to a robbery at a mini mart on the 3300 block of Wible Road. The suspect, a man in a white shirt and red pants, fled on foot with cash and a metal rod, and officers found a person nearby shortly after.

**Fight, 2700 block of Whitley Drive, southwest Bakersfield**

Officers were sent to a fight reported in front of a home in the 2700 block of Whitley Drive, where a caller counted about 40 juveniles. One person had a cut above an eyebrow, and a neighbor reported ATVs, fireworks and possible gunshots in the area.

## Night notes

Fire calls carried the county again: two structure fires, an outside fire under an overpass, a small fire on Pacheco Road and a slow-moving grass fire east of Wasco Pond. The largest police response of the night was the gunfire on Karma Court, with no victim found. Fights, family disturbances and prowler calls kept officers moving from the dinner hour through dawn, and details remain preliminary until confirmed by an official source.
