# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A man holding a knife refused to put it down and kept officers at a standoff for about half an hour before he was taken into custody. Earlier in the evening, airport police cleared the terminal at Meadows Field after a report of a possible firearm, a caller on Pond Road reported five people firing guns into the air, and a mini-bike and a vehicle collided at Bliss and Texas streets, hurting two people. Fires kept crews moving all night: a U-Haul on Lake Street, a vehicle on Mayer Avenue, a patch of grass and brush larger than a car near Alfred Harrell Highway, and a structure fire call after 4 a.m.

## The big one

**Armed standoff ends with a man in custody**

Officers went to a call where a man was holding a knife in his left hand and would not cooperate. He was not advancing, but he stayed armed and refused commands, so supervisors set up command and held the scene rather than closing in. About 35 minutes later officers moved in and detained him. Charges including assault with a deadly weapon, criminal threats and child endangerment were requested.

## Around the county

**Report of a firearm at Meadows Field**

Airport police called for additional units at the terminal over a report of a possible firearm on the property. A man in a black hoodie and black shorts was seen heading for the employee parking area below the front of the terminal. Officers held the terminal while they searched, then reported all channels clear within a few minutes.

**Shots fired into the air, Pond Road**

A caller reported about five people shooting firearms into the air on Pond Road. Three vehicles were described leaving the area: a gray Volkswagen, a black truck and a red Chevrolet.

**Mini-bike and vehicle, Bliss and Texas streets**

Crews went to Bliss and Texas streets for a mini-bike against a vehicle with possibly two patients. Engine 6 found one person down in the roadway and two vehicles involved, and staged incoming units while medics went to work.

**Machete report, Center Street, east Bakersfield**

A caller reported a man hitting windows on Center Street with a machete he kept in his vehicle. The man was described as 35 years old and believed to be under the influence of alcohol.
