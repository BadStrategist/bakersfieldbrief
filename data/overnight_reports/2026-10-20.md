# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A semi hauling a box trailer burned on northbound Interstate 5 at the Copus Road off-ramp, with the trailer about 75 percent involved, a fuel line leaking and firefighters calling for a second engine for water and for environmental health because of the load. Earlier, a semi and an SUV crashed on Highway 65 and a driver was hurt. Deputies spent part of the night searching a storage yard after two people in masks got in, one of them going over the back wall toward the railroad tracks, and a man whose car was stolen on Jeffrey Street called it in and followed it himself while deputies set a perimeter. Fire crews answered a garage fire on Jackson Street in east Bakersfield and an electric car that burned fully involved. A ten-acre vegetation fire near Highway 119 was the largest single burn of the night, and by 6 a.m. the calls had moved back out to the edges of the county.

## The big one

**Burning semi on Interstate 5**

Firefighters were sent to northbound Interstate 5 at the Copus Road off-ramp for a semi whose trailer tires were on fire, with the driver still inside and a fuel line leaking. The box trailer went from the tires to about 75 percent involved, and a second engine was started for water supply. Because of what the trailer was carrying, dispatchers were asked to bring environmental health out to the scene.

## Around the county

**Semi and SUV collide on Highway 65**

A semi and an SUV crashed on Highway 65 and a driver was hurt.

**Ten-acre vegetation fire near Highway 119**

A vegetation fire of about ten acres was reported near Highway 119 before sunrise.

**Two in masks in a storage yard**

Deputies searched a storage facility shortly before 3 a.m. after two people in sweatshirts and masks got in. One of them went over the back wall and was last seen heading southeast toward the railroad tracks, and deputies set a perimeter south of the line.

**Stolen car, owner following**

A man called in his own stolen car and followed it after a 2006 Ford Coupe was taken from Jeffrey Street near Waco Avenue. Deputies set a perimeter around the area.

## Night notes

Fires ran the night: a trailer burning on northbound Interstate 5, a garage on Jackson Street, an electric car fully involved, a trash barrel three feet from a mobile home and a trash can that took the back of a house on Kentucky Street. Vehicles left the road more than once, with a rollover on a dirt road near Valpredo Avenue and a car that burned after going off the roadway in the Dustin Acres area. The largest single burn before sunrise was a vegetation fire of about ten acres near Highway 119.
