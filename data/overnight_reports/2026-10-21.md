# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Gunfire was the night's worst. A man was shot at a store on Highway 58 in Mojave a little after 1 a.m. and was unresponsive when deputies reached him, and they locked the business down while witnesses described a burgundy car with a disabled plate pulling out and two people running toward the apartments behind the store. A single gunshot drew deputies to 22nd Street in central Bakersfield and turned up nothing behind it, officers were sent to a bar where a man with a knife in his pocket was threatening a customer, and a man who threatened a resident and a roommate at an apartment complex left with a handgun in a black backpack. A thief who assaulted the worker who confronted him on South Union Avenue and a report of three or four people walking around with a shotgun rounded out the weapons calls. Fire filled the rest of the night: a rig burning on the Brundage bridge, a transit bus off the road at Belle Terrace and South Washington in southeast Bakersfield with six passengers hurting, an east Bakersfield apartment fire after 3 a.m. that put five people out of their homes, and a house fire on Angela Way.

## The big one

**Shooting in Mojave**

Deputies were sent to a store on Highway 58 in Mojave just after 1 a.m. for a shooting and found a man on the ground who was unresponsive; an ambulance was called and the store was locked down. Witnesses told them a burgundy car with a disabled plate left the lot, driven by a light-skinned man with long hair. Callers also reported two people running from the store toward the apartments behind it, and one person detained at the scene said he saw the car leave.

## Around the county

**Fire in an east Bakersfield apartment**

Multiple callers reported fire in an apartment on Monterey Street a little after 3 a.m. and crews started a rescue. The Red Cross was brought in for three women and two men who were displaced.

**Transit bus off the road in southeast Bakersfield**

A transit bus went off the road at Belle Terrace and South Washington just before 8 p.m. Six passengers reported pain and dispatchers started a third ambulance.

**House fire on Angela Way**

A reinforced response went to a house on Angela Way in the Station 8 area before 5 a.m. Crews found the house charged with smoke on the east side with fire showing at a rear corner, knocked down a contents fire in the garage and secured the gas and electric, then cancelled the rest of the response.

**Grass fire on Elk Hills Road**

A grass fire on Elk Hills Road just before midnight brought two dozers, a water tender and several patrols. Crews had to go in through oil field gates, and the company opened one for them. Battalion 2 kept three engines and the equipment on the fire and cancelled the rest of the response.

## Night notes

Gunfire and weapons calls carried the night: a man shot in Mojave, a single gunshot on 22nd Street that turned up nothing behind it, a knife in a bar, a handgun in a backpack, a shotgun report and a worker assaulted over a theft on South Union Avenue. Fires took most of the radio time, from a rig on the Brundage bridge to a garage on Delwood Avenue, a brush fire on Highway 33 that pulled two dozers and a helicopter, and two east Bakersfield structure fires after 3 a.m. that put five people out of an apartment and brought a reinforced response to Angela Way. The county was quiet by sunrise, with the last hours down to medical calls, a trespassing report and a traffic stop.
