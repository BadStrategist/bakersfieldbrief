# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Gunfire was reported twice overnight. Four to eight shots were reported in the area of River Boulevard and Panorama Drive in east Bakersfield a little after 12:30 a.m., and at midnight two callers reported hearing two shots near Ming Avenue and Barrington Street in southwest Bakersfield. A stabbing was reported near K Street at 4:32 a.m. Deputies went to Harrington Street in southwest Bakersfield for an assault with a deadly weapon, where a man was reported swinging a metal pole at a woman and nobody was hurt, and another weapons call drew deputies to the 2nd and 3rd street area. A traffic stop on Fairfax Road became a pursuit through southeast Bakersfield that ended at Shellmacher Avenue, and both stolen-vehicle calls came in the small hours, a Tahoe from a convenience store lot on Olive Drive and a Kia from a delivery driver on Wilson Avenue in north Bakersfield. Rain brought a roof down on an occupied apartment on Actis Street in southwest Bakersfield before 6 a.m.

## The big one

**Gunfire in east Bakersfield**

Four to eight shots were reported in the area of River Boulevard and Panorama Drive a little after 12:30 a.m. and officers were sent to look. A second gunfire report came in at midnight from the area of Ming Avenue and Barrington Street in southwest Bakersfield, where two callers reported hearing two shots.

## Around the county

**Stabbing reported near K Street**

Officers were sent to a stabbing report near K Street at 4:32 a.m.

**Assault with a deadly weapon in southwest Bakersfield**

Deputies were sent to Harrington Street in southwest Bakersfield shortly after 1 a.m. on an assault with a deadly weapon report. A man was reported swinging a metal pole at a woman and nobody was hurt.

**Pursuit through southeast Bakersfield**

A traffic stop on Fairfax Road turned into a pursuit that ran south on Madison Street and then through southeast Bakersfield on Watts, Lotus and Cottonwood before the driver pulled over westbound at Shellmacher Avenue before 8 p.m. Three people were in the car and all were compliant.

**Rain brings a roof down in southwest Bakersfield**

Part of a roof came down at an apartment on Actis Street and water was running inside the unit before 6 a.m. Crews told the occupants to get out.

## Night notes

Rain ran the night. It brought part of a roof down on an occupied apartment on Actis Street in southwest Bakersfield before 6 a.m., broke a water main that left a bus stranded in the roadway and closed the street, set a transformer on a Quincy Street pole on fire and left the signals dark at Brundage Lane and Chester Avenue, with drivers spinning out on wet pavement and a tree reported down. The last hours before sunrise were down to medical calls, alarms and traffic stops.
