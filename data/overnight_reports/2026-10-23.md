# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A woman was reported hitting vehicles with a stun gun at Worthington Avenue and Lowell Drive in north Bakersfield at 8:17 p.m., one of two weapons calls overnight. Deputies were sent to the Love's truck stop on Boron Frontage Road North at 10:29 p.m. for a man reported to be walking through the parking lot with a sword. A man who reportedly left a cell phone store at Niles Street and Glenwood Avenue without paying for phones was chased by other customers just after midnight. Two men in masks and all black were reported chasing a woman in the 6200 block of Brahma Street in south Bakersfield at 8:02 p.m., in a report that came to officers second hand. Domestic violence calls came in from an apartment in the 2700 block of North Chester Avenue in north Bakersfield, from Baker Street in east Bakersfield, from Beardsley Avenue in north Bakersfield, from the 3400 block of Anderson Street in southeast Bakersfield, and from a Bakersfield address where the man was already the restrained party on a served protective order. A pursuit dead ended into a cul-de-sac near the 1300 block of Chamberlain Avenue in east Bakersfield, and deputies went to a Union Pacific train at Comanche and Edison at 3:37 a.m. after the crew reported someone had cut the train's air flow regulator, forcing an emergency stop.

## The big one

**Woman with a stun gun reported hitting vehicles in north Bakersfield**

Officers were sent to Worthington Avenue and Lowell Drive in north Bakersfield at 8:17 p.m. for a woman reported to be hitting vehicles with a baton style stun gun and screaming. She was described to dispatchers as wearing a black hoodie with dark pants.

**Man with a sword at a truck stop in Boron**

Deputies were sent to the Love's truck stop on Boron Frontage Road North at 10:29 p.m. for a man reported to be walking through the parking lot with a sword.

## Around the county

**Robbery report at a cell phone store**

Officers were sent to a cell phone store at Niles Street and Glenwood Avenue at 12:12 a.m. after a man who reportedly walked out without paying for phones was chased by other customers. The call came in as an attempted robbery at the store.

**Cut air line forced an emergency stop on a Union Pacific train**

Deputies were sent to a Union Pacific train at Comanche and Edison at 3:37 a.m. The crew reported the train's air flow regulator had been cut, which forced an emergency stop, and they asked that units clear the area. The call went out as a report of vandalism to the train.

**Two chases in the small hours**

A traffic stop just before 2 a.m. turned into a pursuit that ran eastbound at 42 mph and then north at 18 mph with no traffic ahead before it dead ended into a cul-de-sac, and officers took one person into custody and cleared the car by the 1300 block of Chamberlain Avenue in east Bakersfield. Around 1:20 a.m. officers stopped a car, several people ran, and a perimeter was set before two of them were proned out.

**Domestic violence calls ran through the night**

A woman told officers at 7:47 p.m. that her boyfriend battered her at an apartment in the 2700 block of North Chester Avenue in north Bakersfield and then hung up on the call. Just after midnight, an apartment in the 1000 block of Baker Street in east Bakersfield drew officers for a domestic violence call where the caller's son's girlfriend was refusing to leave. Officers went to Beardsley Avenue in north Bakersfield at 1:34 a.m. for a domestic violence call and dispatch showed prior calls at that address. The 3400 block of Anderson Street in southeast Bakersfield drew officers at 2:48 a.m. for a call in progress where the caller's husband was the suspect. At 4:11 a.m., a call that started as an open 911 line with a woman telling a man not to put his hands on her was upgraded to domestic violence, and dispatch found the man had prior domestic violence cases and was the restrained party on a served protective order. Officers were sent to an apartment in the 2300 block of Chester Lane at 4:16 a.m. where a woman was refusing to leave and there was an unserved domestic violence restraining order in the case.
