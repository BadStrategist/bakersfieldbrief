# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A drive-by shooting in the 500 block of East Belle Terrace in southeast Bakersfield at 3:55 a.m. left one person with major injuries, the most serious of a night of gunfire calls. Officers and fire crews were sent to Martin Avenue at 1:05 a.m. for a shooting where one person was reported hit, with crews held back while officers worked and an air ambulance asked to stand by. A caller near 10th Street and Lincoln Street at 8:28 p.m. counted about six shots over five minutes, and officers went to the 7500 block of a residential street at 12:41 a.m. on a report of multiple gunshots, where a unit that had been in the area heard none. A fight at Flower Street and Alta Vista Drive in east Bakersfield at 4:30 a.m. grew until a caller reported about 30 people there with firearms and bats. Weapons calls ran the whole night: a woman asked deputies to a parking lot at 7:31 p.m. where she said her boyfriend had battered her and was armed, a man on a scooter with a handgun was reported at a Family Dollar on Highway 58 at 8:20 p.m., and four juveniles with firearms were reported walking northbound on Airport Drive at Washington Avenue at 9:31 p.m. The night's worst crash was a head-on on Highway 46 east of Curt Road at 1:50 a.m. that hurt three people. A second head-on north of Sycamore Road at 11:38 p.m. left two cars off the roadway. Deputies went to a home on Southland Court at 12:16 a.m. for a man reported to have a large kitchen knife, and officers followed a lifted SUV on Gosford Road at 12:41 a.m. after a report of a possibly armed man before a subject ran on foot.

## The big one

**Drive-by shooting in southeast Bakersfield left one person with major injuries**

A drive-by shooting in the 500 block of East Belle Terrace in southeast Bakersfield left one person with major injuries at 3:55 a.m. The suspect vehicle, a black four-door, was last seen northbound on South Real Road toward Stockdale Highway.

## Around the county

**A second shooting, and gunfire reported in two more places**

Officers and fire crews were sent to Martin Avenue at 1:05 a.m. for a shooting where one person was reported hit. Crews were held back while officers worked the scene, and an air ambulance was asked to stand by. Earlier, a caller near 10th Street and Lincoln Street reported hearing about six gunshots over five minutes at 8:28 p.m., and officers were sent to the 7500 block of a residential street at 12:41 a.m. on a report of multiple gunshots, where a unit that had been in the area reported hearing none.

**Head-on crash on Highway 46 hurt three people, and fog turned the helicopter back**

A head-on crash on Highway 46 east of Curt Road at 1:50 a.m. hurt three people. One was trapped in a vehicle and tagged red, a second was tagged yellow, and a third was also worked on. Mercy Air was launched and the highway was shut down for a landing zone, but fog turned the helicopter back to base, and both lanes were left blocked with debris and a car.

**A fight grew to about 30 people with firearms and bats**

A fight reported at Flower Street and Alta Vista Drive in east Bakersfield at 4:30 a.m. grew until a caller said about 30 people were there with firearms and bats. Officers were sent.

**Head-on crash north of Sycamore Road left two cars off the roadway**

A two-vehicle head-on crash north of Sycamore Road at 11:38 p.m. left both vehicles off the roadway with major front-end damage. One person was a yellow tag, and Engine 46, Engine 48 and Battalion 4 responded, with the call upgraded to a heavier response.

## Night notes

Gunfire was reported at five points between 8:28 p.m. and 4:30 a.m., and two of those calls ended with someone hurt: a person with major injuries after the drive-by on East Belle Terrace, and one person hit on Martin Avenue. Rain left a lane of White Lane under water at 7:51 p.m., and fog over the east side of the county turned a Mercy Air helicopter back to base from the head-on crash on Highway 46, so the highway was shut for a landing zone that was never used. Deputies also worked a knife call on Southland Court and a person held at gunpoint at 4:25 a.m., while officers ran down a lifted SUV on Gosford Road after a report of an armed man.
