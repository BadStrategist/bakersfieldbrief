# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Gunfire calls ran through the small hours. At 3:37 a.m. officers were sent to a large party after a caller said his father had been shot and a victim was down in a backyard; multiple callers reported hearing gunshots, and officers found no one in the yard. About half an hour earlier, callers counted five gunshots in the 1800 block of Hendricks Lane in south Bakersfield, and a four-door car was seen driving away, with a witness reporting that four or five people got out of it and went into a home at the end of the cul-de-sac. Weapons calls filled the hours around them: a caller at 7 p.m. told deputies his mother was armed with a firearm and that he believed she was preparing to use it while a 2-year-old and a 4-year-old were with her, and she refused to come down; officers were sent to a home at 8:16 p.m. where a man had barricaded himself in a back room with two juveniles and would not come out; a man doing donuts in a Mustang at 8:25 p.m. was reported to have a firearm in his waistband; a fight in the 5500 block of College Avenue in east Bakersfield at 9:12 p.m. brought reports that adults had supplied juveniles with alcohol and that a man had a knife; a woman with a knife jumped a fence onto church property on North Chester Avenue at 9:30 p.m.; and at 12:27 a.m. someone locked themselves in a restroom at a hospital emergency room lobby. The night's worst medical call came earlier, when a crash at a park at 8:41 p.m. left at least four people hurt, two of them tagged black, and pinned a child's legs under the front of a vehicle.

## The big one

**Gunfire at a large party, and no victim found**

Multiple callers reported hearing gunshots at 3:37 a.m., and one caller told officers his father had been shot, with a report of a victim down in a backyard. Officers went to a large party and found no one in the yard.

## Around the county

**Five gunshots on Hendricks Lane, and a car drove off**

Callers reported about five gunshots in the 1800 block of Hendricks Lane in south Bakersfield at 3:01 a.m. A four-door car was seen driving away after the gunfire, and a witness reported that four or five people got out of it and entered a home at the end of a cul-de-sac.

**A man barricaded with two juveniles, and a locked restroom at the ER**

Officers were sent to a home at 8:16 p.m. where a man was reported to be in a back room with two juveniles barricaded with him. He had said his 12-year-old daughter let someone into the house, and he refused to come out until officers were there. Just after midnight, at 12:27 a.m., officers went to a hospital emergency room lobby where a person had locked themselves in a restroom and appeared to be in a mental health crisis.

**A crash at a park hurt four, and a child was pinned under a vehicle**

A crash at a park at 8:41 p.m. left at least four people hurt, two of them tagged black, and a child's legs were pinned under the front of a vehicle. Crews called three ambulances and asked for air support.

**A woman armed while two young children were with her**

A caller told deputies at 7 p.m. that his mother was armed with a firearm and that he believed she was preparing to use it, while a 2-year-old and a 4-year-old were with her. Deputies reached her by phone, and she refused to come down. Children could be heard crying in the background.

## Night notes

Gunfire was reported twice in the small hours, at 3:01 a.m. on Hendricks Lane in south Bakersfield and at 3:37 a.m. at a large party, and officers found no one hurt at the party. Weapons calls and fights carried most of the rest: a man with a knife at a party on College Avenue, a woman with a knife on church property on North Chester Avenue, and a fight with seven people and more than 15 vehicles on McArthur Drive. The heaviest call of the night was medical, a crash at a park at 8:41 p.m. that hurt at least four people and pinned a child under a vehicle.
