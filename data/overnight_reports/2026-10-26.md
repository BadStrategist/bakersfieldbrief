# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A gunshot call at 11:30 p.m. became a fight and a confirmed offense on South Union Avenue, where officers ringed the block and went into the alley behind the fire station looking for shell casings. Before dawn, at 5:25 a.m., a caller reported a man trying to force a woman into the back seat of a car in an alley behind a residence, and there was no description of the vehicle. The night's worst assault was reported at 1:24 a.m., a possible victim of an attack with a baseball bat on Chester Lane, a 40-year-old man. Two weapons calls landed hours apart: at 10 p.m. officers were sent after a man who claimed to have a firearm in his pocket and had arrived in a gray SUV, and at 1:07 a.m. deputies went to the 8200 block of Kimber Avenue in southeast Bakersfield over people with a small black handgun. The night's heaviest fire call came at 8:57 p.m., heavy smoke from a building of about 1,500 square feet in the 2000 block of Lake Street in east Bakersfield, and crews kept one engine on it with smoke still in the attic.

## The big one

**A gunshot, a fight, and a confirmed offense on South Union Avenue**

A caller reported hearing a gunshot and seeing three people fighting across the street at 11:30 p.m., one of them in a red shirt and possibly armed. Officers set a perimeter around South Union Avenue and Serrano Drive, with units posted on Acres, Millwood Court, Birchwood Court and Juniper, and the air unit over the area. Officers held the block, then confirmed an offense and searched the alley behind the fire station for shell casings.

## Around the county

**A man reported forcing a woman into a car before dawn**

At 5:25 a.m. a caller reported a man trying to force a woman into the back seat of a car in the alley behind a residence, and another caller reported the same thing. There was no description of the vehicle, and officers were sent back to the block.

**A possible victim of a baseball bat attack on Chester Lane**

Officers were sent at 1:24 a.m. to a report of an assault with a baseball bat on Chester Lane, where a 40-year-old man was reported as a possible victim. Half an hour later, officers asked a local hospital to check whether any victims of an assault with a deadly weapon had come in.

**Two weapons calls, a man with a claimed firearm and a handgun on Kimber Avenue**

At 10 p.m. officers were sent after a caller reported a man who said he had a firearm in his pocket and had arrived in a gray SUV. Deputies were sent at 1:07 a.m. to the 8200 block of Kimber Avenue in southeast Bakersfield over people with a small black handgun. A blue newer model Subaru SUV left, and no firearm was recovered.

**Heavy smoke from a building on Lake Street**

Crews were sent to a structure fire in the 2000 block of Lake Street in east Bakersfield at 8:57 p.m. and found heavy smoke coming from a building of about 1,500 square feet. The fire was reinforced and worked as a high-priority search, then scaled back to one engine with smoke still in the attic.
