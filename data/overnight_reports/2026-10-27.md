# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

The night's one gunfire call came at 9:53 p.m., when a unit asked dispatchers to check for a ShotSpotter activation and an officer nearby reported hearing what sounded like 10 to 12 rounds, or fireworks. Two minutes later a man was attacked with a pipe in the 700 block of Oak Street in central Bakersfield, and the suspect was still across the street at a bus stop with the pipe. A man in his late 20s, listed at risk because of a medical condition, left a home on foot about 7:10 p.m. and was still missing. Officers chased someone seen on the roof of a house at 9:09 p.m., and two fire companies were sent at 1:25 a.m. to a structure fire in progress in the 4900 block of Corrine Street in south Bakersfield.

## The big one

**A gunfire call at 9:53 p.m., and an officer who heard 10 to 12 rounds, or fireworks**

At 9:53 p.m. a unit asked dispatchers to check with a second channel whether there was a ShotSpotter activation. An officer nearby reported hearing what sounded like 10 to 12 rounds, or fireworks. It was the only gunfire call of the night.

## Around the county

**A man attacked with a pipe on Oak Street**

A caller reported at 9:55 p.m. that a man had been attacked with a pipe in the 700 block of Oak Street in central Bakersfield. The suspect, a white man in his late 20s or early 30s in a black hooded sweatshirt and black pants, was still across the street at a bus stop with the pipe about 20 minutes later.

**A man listed at risk, missing from a home since about 7:10 p.m.**

A man in his late 20s, 5-foot-7 and about 130 pounds, left on foot at about 7:10 p.m. in a white shirt and black shorts, heading an unknown direction. He is listed at risk because of a medical condition.

**A structure fire in the 4900 block of Corrine Street**

Two fire companies were sent at 1:25 a.m. to a structure fire in progress in the 4900 block of Corrine Street in south Bakersfield, and Metro units were asked to respond as well. Crews were still on the call more than 45 minutes later.

**A haystack fire near Adobe Road holds several companies**

Crews worked a large haystack fire at 11:28 p.m. near Adobe Road and Brent Avenue in southeast Bakersfield. A pile of timber behind the stack was the remaining exposure, and the structure on the property was protected. A water tender was added, crews ran a supply line from a hydrant about 200 feet away, and access was made through a locked gate.

## Night notes

The night's only gunfire call came at 9:53 p.m. and was never confirmed, and the most serious confirmed offense was the pipe attack on Oak Street at 9:55 p.m. with the suspect still holding the weapon at a bus stop. Fires ran from a backyard fire that reached inside a wall just after 8:21 p.m., to a haystack fire near Adobe Road at 11:28 p.m. that held several companies, to a structure fire on Corrine Street at 1:25 a.m. that crews were still working an hour later. The rest of the night was mostly medical calls, from difficulty breathing and possible strokes to an unconscious patient and a hemorrhage.
