# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A man with a knife tried to stab another man near Wible Road at 9:32 p.m., the most serious call of the night. Officers were sent to a home on Wainwright Drive in north Bakersfield at 1:52 a.m. for a woman known to carry a knife who was climbing through a window. Two domestic violence calls came in, at an apartment on California Avenue at 9:13 p.m. with two juveniles inside, and on Pierce Road in north Bakersfield at 11:25 p.m., where the man left on a scooter. A person listed at risk left a home on Cherry Street in central Bakersfield on foot at about 6 p.m. saying he wanted to die, and was still missing. The night's heaviest fire came at 2:08 a.m., with heavy fire from the rear of a structure that crews could not reach with an attack line.

## The big one

**A man with a knife reported to be trying to stab someone near Wible Road**

Officers were sent at 9:32 p.m. for a man in a tan shirt and black pants armed with a knife, reported to be trying to stab someone near Wible Road. Medical units staged nearby in case they were needed.

## Around the county

**A woman with a knife at a window on Wainwright Drive**

Officers were sent to a home on Wainwright Drive in north Bakersfield at 1:52 a.m. for a woman in her late 30s known to carry a knife who was trying to climb through a window.

**Two domestic violence calls, on California Avenue and on Pierce Road**

Officers went to an apartment in the 9200 block of California Avenue at 9:13 p.m. for a domestic disturbance, with two juveniles in the residence and a history of domestic violence at the address. At 11:25 p.m. officers were sent to the 5000 block of Pierce Road in north Bakersfield for a domestic violence call, and the man left on a red and black scooter.

**A person listed at risk, missing from a home on Cherry Street**

A person listed as at risk left a home in the 2600 block of Cherry Street in central Bakersfield on foot at about 6 p.m. after a disturbance, saying he wanted to die. He was last seen in a burgundy or black sweatshirt and dark shoes.

**Heavy fire from the rear of a structure at 2:08 a.m.**

Crews found heavy fire from the rear of a structure at 2:08 a.m. and could not reach it with an attack line. Battalion 1 took command, a truck was asked to ventilate the building, and building utilities and the gas company were notified.

## Night notes

Six of the night's calls were welfare checks on people reported to be suicidal or at risk, from a 10-year-old on Lake Superior Drive at 7:52 p.m. to a woman who disconnected twice while reported to be trying to kill herself at 10:51 p.m., and a person who left a Cherry Street home saying he wanted to die was still missing at dawn. Fire crews went out seven times, from a gas leak inside a Piute Street home at 7:04 p.m. and light smoke in an attic at 8:05 p.m., to heavy fire from the rear of a structure at 2:08 a.m. that crews could not reach with a hand line. Hazardous materials calls came in pairs: the diesel spill at a truck stop at 5:12 a.m. was contained to a fuel pump, and the gas leak inside the Piute Street home was handled at 7:04 p.m.
