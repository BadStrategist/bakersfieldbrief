# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Police worked an apartment complex through the early hours, bringing residents of several units out through a garage door at the rear and sending an entry team in from the back while an armored vehicle sat in front. Two reports of shots heard came in at 4:33 a.m., six to eight rounds in the 10800 block of Royal Ascot Avenue and about 12 rounds in the 10000 block of Laurel Park Avenue in northwest Bakersfield. Officers were sent to a mobile home park at 7:17 p.m. for a 15-year-old reported to have a gray handgun, and at 9:08 p.m. a driver flagged down an officer on northbound Interstate 5 north of Fort Tejon and said a man had tried to force him out of his vehicle and take his keys and wallet. At 9:58 p.m. a semi rear-ended another semi on northbound Interstate 5 half a mile south of the Grapevine, pinning a driver who firefighters had to free from the tractor-trailer.

## The big one

**A standoff at an apartment complex that ran into the early morning**

Officers were working an apartment complex at 1:55 a.m., moving through the building and bringing residents of several units out through a garage door at the rear. An armored vehicle was moved into position in front of the building, and the entry team came in from the back. Officers found bullet holes in the doorway of an upstairs unit.

## Around the county

**Two reports of shots heard at 4:33 a.m.**

Two calls came in back to back, six to eight rounds in the 10800 block of Royal Ascot Avenue and about 12 rounds in the 10000 block of Laurel Park Avenue in northwest Bakersfield. No further information was available on either.

**A 15-year-old reported with a handgun at a mobile home park**

Officers were sent to a mobile home park at 7:17 p.m. for a 15-year-old in a white shirt and black pants reported to be holding a gray handgun.

**A driver flagged down officers on Interstate 5 after a man tried to take his keys**

A driver on northbound Interstate 5 north of Fort Tejon flagged down an officer at 9:08 p.m. and said a man had tried to force him out of his vehicle and take his keys and wallet.

**A semi rear-ended another on the Grapevine and pinned a driver**

A semi rear-ended another semi on northbound Interstate 5 half a mile south of the Grapevine at 9:58 p.m. One driver was pinned and firefighters freed him from the tractor-trailer. Crews asked for a hard closure of the lanes.

## Night notes

Three of the night's calls were welfare or mental-health checks on people reported to be off medication or in crisis, on Hermosa Drive in east Bakersfield at 7:22 p.m., on Country Club Drive in east Bakersfield at 10:39 p.m. and at an apartment complex at 1:48 a.m. Crews were sent to 12 fires and fire alarms overnight, among them the two vehicle fires at Inyo Street at 7:32 p.m. and Hageman Road at 9:12 p.m., outside fires at 10:06 p.m., 12:49 a.m., 2:02 a.m., 4:43 a.m. and 5:30 a.m., and a wildland fire near the railroad by Horseshoe Bend at 6:44 a.m. Seven crashes came in overnight, from a minor one near Workman Street at 9:16 p.m. to a hit-and-run with an injury at 23rd and Chester at 6:24 a.m., with the worst of them on northbound Interstate 5 south of the Grapevine and on Highway 99 at 3:07 a.m.
