# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Officers were sent to Kentucky Street and Bale in east Bakersfield at 9:26 p.m. for seven juveniles, one of them reported to have a firearm, and searched the railroad tracks and nearby streets for him. A man with a machete on a bicycle was reported on Cedar Street in central Bakersfield at 11:45 p.m., and on Fillmore Street a few minutes later on foot. At 8:37 p.m. officers went to Beardsley Avenue in north Bakersfield for a juvenile reported to be suicidal and holding a knife, at 9:13 p.m. to Haven Avenue in north Bakersfield for a fight between roommates in which a woman had a sword, and at 3:53 a.m. to Country View Lane in southwest Bakersfield for a woman reported to be armed with a brick. Two people cut through a razor-wire fence into a business yard at 8:53 p.m., and a pursuit ran eastbound on Edison Highway at 11:40 p.m. at up to 120 mph.

## The big one

**Seven juveniles, one with a firearm reported, along the railroad tracks on Kentucky Street**

Officers were sent to Kentucky Street and Bale at 9:26 p.m. for seven juveniles reported in the area, and dispatch put their age at 15. One of them was said to have a firearm, and he was last seen walking south toward the railroad tracks in a white jumpsuit. Officers were sent to search the tracks and the streets around them.

## Around the county

**Two cut through a razor-wire fence into a business yard, and a police dog and an air unit joined the search**

Two people cut through a razor-wire fence and got into a business yard at 8:53 p.m. Units set a perimeter, and a police dog and an air unit joined the search. One of the two was reported hurt getting over the wire.

**A pursuit ran eastbound on Edison Highway at 85 to 120 mph**

A pursuit was reported on Edison Highway at 11:40 p.m., past Sterling and then Fairfax, at speeds from 85 up to 120 mph, and an air unit was launching to follow it.

**A hit-and-run crash left a person with moderate to major injuries**

A hit-and-run crash was reported at 8:34 p.m. with injuries described as moderate to major. The car that fled was described as a black Toyota sedan heading east.

**An air unit and a police dog answered a stolen-vehicle call at a business yard on South Union Avenue**

A call about a possible unreported stolen vehicle with a firearm on the seat brought an air unit and a police dog to a business yard in the 5800 block of South Union Avenue at 7:01 p.m. A man was found hiding under a fifth-wheel trailer, and one person was taken into custody.

## Night notes

Five of the night's calls were weapons calls, three of them involving a knife, a sword or a machete, and seven were welfare or mental-health checks, among them a man in crisis at a gas station at 10:14 p.m., a woman talking about wanting to die at a 33rd Street apartment at 3:12 a.m. and two calls about people reported to be suicidal on Sierra Highway at 12:11 a.m. and on East Brundage Lane at 11:04 p.m. Crews went to ten medical calls, including two cardiac arrests and an overdose on Lake Street at 4:09 a.m., and to three fire alarms, one of them a charcoal grill left smoldering in a garage at 1:12 a.m. Six crashes came in, the worst of them the hit-and-run at 8:34 p.m. with moderate to major injuries, a pedestrian hit by a fleeing pickup in Taft at 8:28 p.m. and a truck crash on Gosford Road at 8:50 p.m.
