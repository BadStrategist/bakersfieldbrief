# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A caller reported five shots and screaming on South Half Moon Drive in southwest Bakersfield at 3:37 a.m., deputies found a victim, and by 4:53 a.m. dispatch was working the call as a homicide, with subjects reported to have fled in vehicles and one who ran on foot crossing to an apartment complex across the street. At 5:43 a.m. officers were sent to the 3500 block of Country Club Drive in east Bakersfield, where a man was reported to have stabbed his brother in the face with a nail and to still be trying to stab people. Shots fired on Mohawk Street at 11:49 p.m. ended with two people detained, and a possible shooting was reported on Eureka Street in east Bakersfield at 12:29 a.m. Four calls about people with firearms came in: at the Camilla Village apartments at 10:25 p.m., at Valley Plaza and Castro Lane in south Bakersfield at 10:36 p.m., at an apartment complex on Terrace Way at 11:33 p.m. and on Quincy Street in east Bakersfield at 4:12 a.m., where a neighbor was reported to have pointed a laser at the caller. A vehicle hit a pedestrian at 30th Street and Union Avenue in central Bakersfield at 11:33 p.m., and two separate traffic stops turned into foot chases, at Fremont Street and 9th Avenue in south Bakersfield at 7:58 p.m. and at South Fairfax Road and Oasis in southeast Bakersfield at 12:41 a.m.

## The big one

**Five shots and a scream on South Half Moon Drive in southwest Bakersfield, and by 4:53 a.m. a homicide investigation**

Deputies were called at 3:37 a.m. after a caller reported five shots and screaming. They found a victim, and subjects were reported fleeing in vehicles. One who ran on foot was reported to have crossed to an apartment complex across the street, and a caller reported blood in an alleyway. A briefing was staged at Savory Lane and South Half Moon, and by 4:53 a.m. dispatch was working the call as a homicide.

## Around the county

**A man stabbed his brother in the face with a nail and was still reported to be armed with them**

Officers were sent to the 3500 block of Country Club Drive in east Bakersfield at 5:43 a.m. where a man was reported to have stabbed his brother in the face with a nail and to still be trying to stab people. He had several nails with him.

**Shots fired on Mohawk Street ended with two people detained and a search of a field and a house**

Officers were sent to Mohawk Street at 11:49 p.m. for shots fired and a chase. Two more people were detained, and officers said no one else was outstanding. A search of the field and a house south of it continued.

**Deputies spent an hour searching a Hageman Road backyard for a man sought on a protective order**

Deputies searched a backyard, a shed and an overhang at a home on Hageman Road at Ledgeview Lane from 12:05 to 1:07 a.m. for a man wanted on a served criminal protective order and reported to be hiding there. An air unit watched the yard, and the search was cleared at 1:07 a.m.

**A two-vehicle crash shut White Lane down in both directions**

A two-vehicle crash at White Lane and Cernan Court at 2:45 a.m. shut White Lane down in both directions, and one person was reported to be getting CPR at the scene. Crews blocked White Lane at Lily while they worked.

## Night notes

Ten of the night's calls were weapons or assault calls, among them two stabbings, three reports of shots and four calls about people with firearms, and ten more were disturbances or welfare checks, including two where someone was reported to be suicidal. Crews went to ten fire calls, five of them outside fires in fields, grass or vegetation, plus a garage fire on South Silver Ridge Street at 8:53 p.m., a utility truck on Main Street at 9:36 p.m., a living-room fire on University Avenue at 4:09 a.m. and a commercial fire alarm at 1:02 a.m. Eight crashes came in, the worst a two-vehicle crash that shut White Lane down in both directions at 2:45 a.m. and a pedestrian hit at 30th Street and Union Avenue at 11:33 p.m.
