# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A shooting with two victims at the 4000 block of Rosedale Highway at 11:45 p.m. brought officers and an ambulance, and a suspect vehicle was described as a black four-door Mercedes. Five other reports of gunfire came in: 3 to 4 loud explosions and 3 gunshots in the 12200 block of Margalo Avenue at 7:53 p.m., four shots with a vehicle reported driving east at 8:19 p.m., 3 to 4 shots near Del Pino and Belle Terrace at 8:27 p.m., a possible shooting toward a person at 10:38 p.m., and two men setting off fireworks at each other in the street just after midnight. A business in the 3700 block of Ming Avenue was robbed at 8:47 p.m. by a suspect reported to have a knife who fled east in a gray van, and a man walked into a store in the 300 block of South Martin Luther King Boulevard covered in blood at 4:16 a.m. as a possible assault victim. Weapons calls also came in at 7:23 p.m., where a man was reported to have a firearm in a red pickup that left before officers arrived, at the 400 block of Geraldine Street at South Union Avenue at 10:01 p.m., where a man in a gray sedan was reported to have a firearm with two juveniles in the car, at 1:39 a.m., where a caller said his wife was being threatened by a woman with a firearm, at 2:17 a.m., where a resident was reported to be telling another woman to shoot her, and at 2:49 a.m. at a Rio Morada apartment. Wrong-way drivers were reported twice: southbound in the northbound lanes at 2:24 a.m., and westbound in the eastbound lanes of Highway 58 at Fairfax Road at 3:59 a.m., where dispatch took multiple 911 calls.

## The big one

**A shooting with two victims on Rosedale Highway, and a search for a black Mercedes**

Officers and an ambulance were sent to the 4000 block of Rosedale Highway at 11:45 p.m. after a shooting with two victims was reported. Units moved in on emergency traffic, and a suspect vehicle was described as a black four-door Mercedes.

## Around the county

**A man walked into a store covered in blood and asking for help**

Officers were sent to a store in the 300 block of South Martin Luther King Boulevard at 4:16 a.m. where a man walked in covered in blood and asking for help, a possible assault victim.

**Crews were sent to a structure fire**

Kern County Fire crews and an ambulance were sent out at 2:05 a.m. for a structure fire.

**A business on Ming Avenue was robbed by a suspect reported to have a knife**

Officers were sent to the 3700 block of Ming Avenue at 8:47 p.m. for an armed robbery. The suspect, reported to have a knife, fled east in a gray van.

**A woman was found dead at a home on Lamberton Street**

Crews were called to the 12000 block of Lamberton Street in northwest Bakersfield at 5:49 a.m. where a woman was reported dead, and an ambulance was requested at the highest priority.

## Night notes

Five reports of shots or a shooting came in overnight, along with five calls about people with firearms, an armed robbery and a stabbing, and several fights.
