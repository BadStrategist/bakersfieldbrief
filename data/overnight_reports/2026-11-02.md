# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A man barricaded in a home in Wasco brought a K9 request at 8:49 p.m., and the night that followed produced two assaults with weapons, a domestic in which one person was cut with a box cutter, three priority domestic calls and a man with a gun reported near an auto shop. At 11:33 p.m. officers were sent to the 5000 block of Pierce Road in north Bakersfield after a juvenile reported his mother had hit him with a meat cleaver; two juveniles were to be checked on. Sixteen minutes later, officers were sent to the 1400 block of East 9th Street in east Bakersfield where a victim of an assault with an iron object was reported. A man with a gun was reported threatening people near an auto shop at 11:22 p.m. At 2:36 a.m. officers were sent to a priority domestic in the 8100 block of Seminole Court in southwest Bakersfield where one person reported being cut with a box cutter and the other was reported to be sitting on a bed with the box cutter. A fight between a man and a woman in the 3000 block of Glenwood was reported out of control at 12:41 a.m., and two more priority domestic calls came in later, at the 300 block of Dr Martin Luther King Jr Boulevard at 2:50 a.m. and the 2900 block of Descanso Street in east Bakersfield at 3:08 a.m. A battery at a grocery store in the 7900 block of White Lane was reported at 3:58 a.m.

## The big one

**A man was barricaded in a Wasco home and police asked for a K9**

Wasco police asked for a K9 unit at 8:49 p.m. for a subject barricaded in a home in the 2200 block of 3rd Street. The subject was wanted on a protective order violation.

## Around the county

**A hit and run left a cyclist hurt in north Bakersfield**

A vehicle hit a cyclist at North Chester Avenue and Roberts Lane at 7:40 p.m. and left. The vehicle, described as a maroon or red Toyota with front-end damage and its bumper missing, was last seen eastbound on East Roberts Lane, and a white Honda CR-V was later reported trying to pick up the missing bumper.

**Crews were sent to a vehicle fire in northwest Bakersfield**

A vehicle fire in the 3600 block of Sewell Street was reported at 7:42 p.m.

**A vehicle rolled onto its roof in Delano**

Delano police and crews were sent to a rollover at 10:47 p.m. where a vehicle was on its roof in the roadway; one patient was reported.

**A woman was reported to have set a fire at the back of a market**

Crews were sent to a market in the 9500 block of South Union Avenue at 4:19 a.m. where a woman was reported to have set a fire at the back of the building and was being argumentative.

## Night notes

A standoff in Wasco, two assaults with a weapon and three priority domestic calls carried the night, along with a man with a gun reported near an auto shop.
