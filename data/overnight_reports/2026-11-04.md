# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A man was reported to be holding a firearm at 10:44 p.m., with people screaming in the background and the caller's line down. Violence carried the rest of the night: officers were sent to the 600 block of Casa Loma Drive in southeast Bakersfield at 11:39 p.m. where a man in his 30s was reported to have pushed his mother and then walked toward Brundage Lane, a fight inside a home with several people was reported at 5:49 a.m., and a battery at a market in the 400 block of Baker Street in east Bakersfield was reported at 7:29 p.m. A 3-year-old boy was reported missing from a home on El Toro Drive in south Bakersfield at 7:45 p.m., a traffic stop became a pursuit at 10:47 p.m. and ended with two men running off on foot, and officers chased a store burglary from Niles Street to Pacific Street after 1 a.m. and took one person into custody. The two worst crashes arrived before daylight: a car crossed into oncoming traffic on northbound Interstate 5 near the Kern-Kings county line at 3:36 a.m. and hit a pickup head-on, leaving one person with a serious head injury, and a semi ended up across three lanes of northbound Highway 99 near the Highway 58 interchange at 4:52 a.m.

## The big one

**A man was reported to be holding a firearm, with people screaming in the background**

Officers were sent at 10:44 p.m. to a weapons call after a man was reported to be holding a firearm. People were heard screaming in the background, and the caller's line went down.

## Around the county

**A car crossed into oncoming traffic on Interstate 5 and hit a pickup head-on**

A car crossed from the southbound side of Interstate 5 into oncoming traffic near the Kern-Kings county line at 3:36 a.m. and hit a white Ford pickup. The two vehicles came to rest about 200 yards apart, and of the four people in them, three were unhurt and one had a serious head injury. An air ambulance was asked for and then cancelled, and an ambulance took the patient.

**A semi ended up across three lanes of Highway 99 before dawn**

Fire crews were sent at 4:52 a.m. to the Norris Road on-ramp to northbound Highway 99 for a semi crash with a possible gas leak and found the rig across at least three lanes just north of the east off-ramp to Highway 58. The CHP stopped traffic while crews worked, and fire units stuck in the backup reached the scene along the shoulder.

**A 3-year-old boy was reported missing from a home in south Bakersfield**

A 3-year-old boy was reported missing at 7:45 p.m. from a home on El Toro Drive. He was last seen in a black Lightning McQueen shirt, green cargo pants and orange and red shoes.

**A store burglary on Niles Street ended with a chase to Pacific Street**

A clerk at a store on Niles Street yelled for a man to come back at 1:11 a.m. as he ran to a waiting car carrying a box. Officers followed the car west on Niles Street, found it on Pacific Street and took one person into custody in the 900 block.

## Night notes

A weapons call, two batteries and a fight inside a home carried the night, with the theft calls scattered around them: a missing 3-year-old on El Toro Drive at 7:45 p.m., a pursuit that ended with two men on foot at 10:47 p.m., a store burglary chased from Niles Street to Pacific Street after 1 a.m. and a market alarm on Lincoln Street at 3:05 a.m. Both freeway crashes came in the last hours before daylight, a head-on on northbound Interstate 5 near the county line at 3:36 a.m. that left one person with a serious head injury, and a semi across three lanes of northbound Highway 99 near the Highway 58 interchange at 4:52 a.m. The last call before the morning was a semi into the guardrail on Highway 99 at 6:08 a.m.
