# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

An armed robbery at a gas station on Highway 46 opened the small hours: two masked juveniles, at least one armed, took cash from the register and from a customer at 12:11 a.m. and ran east on foot. Weapons calls carried much of the night, with a driver reporting a man who brandished a firearm from a white Honda at 1:55 a.m. and an officer searching a call for suspected drugs and weapons at 9:51 p.m. Violence was next: officers were sent to the 8300 block of California Avenue at 10:10 p.m. where a man was reported to have hit his head on a wall during an argument with his father and was not moving, to Gilmore Avenue and Buck Owens Boulevard in north Bakersfield at 3:47 a.m. where a man was reported to have pepper-sprayed the caller over a payment dispute, and to an assault at an Elmwood Avenue home in east Bakersfield at 6:50 a.m. Three pursuits crossed the night, a car that fled an officer east on Pioneer Drive in east Bakersfield at 9:44 p.m. and was lost, a traffic stop on Castro Lane that became a chase onto Highway 99 at 12:25 a.m., and a wrong-way driver on Highway 99 who was spun with a PIT maneuver at 2:06 a.m. but kept creeping along. The worst crash came in the dark, a single vehicle that rolled onto its roof on the 8,100 block of Old River Road in southwest Bakersfield at 4:52 a.m., with the driver out of the car and the patients refusing a hospital.

## The big one

**A gas station on Highway 46 was robbed at gunpoint overnight**

Deputies were sent at 12:11 a.m. to an armed robbery that had just happened at a gas station on the 21,900 block of Highway 46. Two juvenile suspects in masks, at least one armed, took cash from the register and from a customer, then ran east on foot toward a nearby restaurant.

## Around the county

**A wrong-way driver on Highway 99 kept going after a PIT maneuver**

The CHP went after a wrong-way driver on Highway 99 at 2:06 a.m. and spun the car with a PIT maneuver, but it kept moving, creeping southbound at only a few miles an hour near Houghton Road.

**A fire burned in a mechanic's yard on South Union Avenue**

Fire crews were sent at 9:55 p.m. to a mechanic's yard on South Union Avenue between White Lane and Pacheco Road for a fire of unknown origin.

**An alley fire behind Tulare Street drew crews before dawn**

Fire crews were sent at 3:08 a.m. to an outside fire in the alley behind 1515 Tulare Street in east Bakersfield.

**A car rolled onto its roof on Old River Road before daylight**

Fire crews and the CHP were sent at 4:52 a.m. to the 8,100 block of Old River Road in southwest Bakersfield for a single vehicle that rolled onto its roof. The driver was out of the car, and the patients refused to be taken to a hospital.
