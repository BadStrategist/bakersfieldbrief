# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A fight on Actis Street was the night's worst call: officers were sent to the 4,200 block in southwest Bakersfield at 1:27 a.m., where a man and his girlfriend were fighting and the woman was reported to be trying to stab him. A weapons call followed at 2:51 a.m. at Beale Park on Oleander Avenue, where a woman was reported to be with two men who had bats and knives near the restrooms and a sexual assault was feared, and at 2:02 a.m. officers went to North Meadows Park on McCray Street, where a woman was reported to have a facial injury after an argument with her ex-boyfriend. Three burglaries in progress landed on the day shift's doorstep: men seen on camera walking into a garage on Bedford Way in north Bakersfield at 1:47 a.m., one of them believed to have a pistol, a man who dropped into a property from a roof on China Grade Loop in north Bakersfield at 12:21 a.m., and a business on the 400 block of East 19th Street in east Bakersfield at 2:04 a.m. Two chases crossed the night, a reckless blue pickup on Pike Street in east Bakersfield at 8:45 p.m. and a car that stopped on Castro Lane in south Bakersfield at 1:08 a.m. with the occupants running and officers catching them. Fire crews had nothing larger than grass and field fires, a cart alight on State Road and an outside fire on Wisteria Street.

## The big one

**A fight on Actis Street turned into a report of a stabbing attempt**

Officers were sent at 1:27 a.m. to the 4,200 block of Actis Street in southwest Bakersfield for a physical fight between a man and his girlfriend. The woman was reported to be trying to stab him. It was the first of the night's two weapons calls, the second coming at 2:51 a.m. at Beale Park.

## Around the county

**A woman was reported to be with two armed men at Beale Park**

Officers were sent at 2:51 a.m. to a priority welfare check at Beale Park, 500 Oleander Avenue, where a woman was reported to be with two men who had bats and knives near the restrooms. Dispatchers were told a sexual assault was possible.

**A home camera showed men walking into a garage on Bedford Way**

A woman watching her home camera called officers at 1:47 a.m. to the 2,100 block of Bedford Way in north Bakersfield, where she saw men go into her garage and believed one of them had a pistol.

**A chase on Castro Lane ended with the occupants running**

A pursuit that started on Castro Lane about 1:08 a.m. reached only about 40 miles an hour before the car stopped near Castro Lane and Rose Marie Drive in south Bakersfield and the occupants ran. Officers caught up with them.

**A boy was found at a Niles Street gas station after a missing child alert**

Officers were sent at 4:44 a.m. to the Chevron on the 3,100 block of Niles Street in east Bakersfield, where a caller was standing by with a boy about 10 or 11 years old who was possibly autistic. An alert for a missing 9-year-old boy had gone out on Oswell Street.
