# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Deputies took the night's worst call from the county side of 7th Standard Road: shots fired at 7th Standard Road and Mayer Avenue at 10:43 p.m., where a caller said two people were shooting and trying to shoot his brother, suspects were thought to still be in the area and a victim was reported hiding in a field. Deputies were back out three hours later at the Edison Trailer Park on Edison Highway, where a 17-year-old was reported to have been hit with glass and had facial injuries and the man suspected of the assault left on foot. A vehicle hit a juvenile on Edison Highway westbound at Fairfax Road north just before 7:30 p.m. and left one patient down in the roadway, and a man who threatened the caller during a domestic disturbance at 10:28 p.m. walked out before officers arrived. Two burglaries in progress stood out: a man in a black hooded sweatshirt seen on camera at the Dollar Tree on the 2,500 block of South 8th Street at 8:32 p.m., and a man on a bicycle seen on camera at a Toyota dealership at 4:59 a.m. Fire crews ran a working fire behind a home on Woodmere Drive in southwest Bakersfield at 12:48 a.m., smoke inside a one-story apartment complex on East 11th Street in east Bakersfield at 3:58 a.m., and vehicle fires on Panama Road and Muller Road in southeast Bakersfield.

## The big one

**Deputies were sent to 7th Standard Road and Mayer Avenue for a shooting**

Deputies were sent at 10:43 p.m. to 7th Standard Road and Mayer Avenue for shots fired. A caller said two people were shooting and trying to shoot his brother. Deputies reported no injuries so far, said the suspects might still be in the area and that a victim was hiding in a field nearby, asked for a drone, and at 11:01 p.m. started looking for shell casings.

## Around the county

**A 17-year-old was reported hit with glass at the Edison Trailer Park**

Deputies were sent at 11:32 p.m. to the Edison Trailer Park on Edison Highway, where a 17-year-old was reported to have been hit with glass and had facial injuries. The man suspected of the assault left on foot.

**A working fire behind a home on Woodmere Drive in southwest Bakersfield**

Fire crews were sent at 12:48 a.m. to a fire burning behind a home on the 5500 block of Woodmere Drive in southwest Bakersfield, visible from the street.

**A vehicle hit a juvenile on Edison Highway at Fairfax Road**

Fire crews and medics were sent at 7:25 p.m. to Edison Highway westbound at Fairfax Road north, where a vehicle hit a juvenile and one patient was down in the roadway.

**Smoke was reported inside an apartment building on East 11th Street**

Fire crews were sent at 3:58 a.m. to an apartment building on the 1,600 block of East 11th Street in east Bakersfield for smoke reported inside the structure. Arriving crews found nothing showing from the one-story complex and went in to investigate.

## Night notes

Violence carried the small hours: shots fired at 7th Standard Road and Mayer Avenue at 10:43 p.m., a 17-year-old reported hit with glass at the Edison Trailer Park at 11:32 p.m., and a domestic disturbance at 10:28 p.m. in which the man involved left on foot. Fire crews spent the night on a working fire behind a Woodmere Drive home in southwest Bakersfield, smoke inside a one-story apartment complex on East 11th Street in east Bakersfield, a car fire on Panama Road, and a second vehicle fire on Muller Road in southeast Bakersfield. The last hours before dawn brought two burglary calls in progress, a welfare check at the Olive Drive overpass, and the day's first calls on Fiesta Avenue and East California Avenue after 6 a.m.
