# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

A traffic stop just after 4 a.m. turned up a vehicle linked to an earlier weapons case. Domestic violence carried the rest of the night: officers arrested a man at a home on the 2,100 block of Kentucky Street in east Bakersfield at 7:41 p.m. on suspicion of domestic battery and resisting, with a domestic violence restraining order already on file; deputies were sent at 10:58 p.m. to a domestic disturbance on Big Blue Road where a man was reported to have threatened to kill a woman; officers answered a domestic at an apartment on the 1,300 block of Oregon Street in east Bakersfield at 10:12 p.m. where the person reported was under the influence; and a woman on a domestic battery call at 11:56 p.m. said her boyfriend battered her. Fire crews held a working apartment fire on the 700 block of L Street in central Bakersfield at 8:29 p.m., evacuating the building and checking the attic for extension, and went back to the same block shortly before 2 a.m. for a second apartment call. A pursuit ran eastbound on Highway 58 at 10:39 p.m. at speeds reported near 90 mph and climbing, and a small pickup and a semi-trailer collided in the Love's Travel Stop lot on Taft Highway at 4:48 a.m., leaving one patient with a traumatic injury.
