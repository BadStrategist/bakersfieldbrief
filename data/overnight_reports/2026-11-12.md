# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Gunfire broke the quiet after 1 a.m. when callers reported multiple gunshots near El Encanto Court in central Bakersfield. Weapons calls ran either side of midnight: officers were sent at 7:26 p.m. to Rench Road in north Bakersfield for a teenager reported to be holding a knife in the living room of a home, and deputies were sent at 12:02 a.m. to the 11,500 block of Old River Road in southwest Bakersfield for a person reported to be brandishing a knife at a group in a gold pickup. Domestic violence carried the early hours, with a woman telling dispatchers at 4:56 a.m. that she was being abused by her child's father before she disconnected and an infant heard crying in the background, and deputies sent at 5:53 a.m. to a disturbance where a man was reported to have pinned his mother against a wall. Two foot chases ended in arrests, one on a roof at a storage yard on the 4,000 block of Chester Avenue at 10:19 p.m. and one on Sky Ranch at 11:40 p.m. Fire crews worked a vegetation fire of about three and a half acres off Washington Street at 7:22 p.m. and a fire on westbound Highway 58 just west of the Mount Vernon Avenue exit at 9:06 p.m., where a second ambulance was requested.

## The big one

**Callers reported multiple gunshots near El Encanto Court in central Bakersfield**

Multiple gunshots were reported near El Encanto Court in central Bakersfield at 1:09 a.m.

Weapons calls were the other thread through the night. Officers were sent at 7:26 p.m. to Rench Road in north Bakersfield for a teenager reported to be holding a knife in the living room of a home, in the company of his parents; it was not known whether he had brandished it or made threats. Deputies were sent at 12:02 a.m. to the 11,500 block of Old River Road in southwest Bakersfield for a person reported to be brandishing a knife, where a group was in a gold pickup and one person reported an assault.

## Around the county

**Domestic violence calls ran through the early morning hours**

Officers were sent at 4:56 a.m. to a domestic call where a woman told dispatchers she was being abused by her child's father and then disconnected; an infant was heard crying in the background. Deputies were sent at 5:53 a.m. to a disturbance where a man was reported to have pinned his mother against a wall during an argument with a juvenile; a court order was already in place.

**Two foot chases ended in arrests**

Officers chased a man at 10:19 p.m. who ran from a suspicious person call at a storage yard on the 4,000 block of Chester Avenue, reaching for his waistband as he ran. He climbed onto a roof on the 3,300 block of Anderson Street and, with Air 1 overhead and a K-9 in place, came down and was arrested, with charges for resisting added. A second chase at 11:40 p.m. ended on Sky Ranch, where a driver and a passenger were taken into custody at a residence on Suncrest Drive and one person was still outstanding.

**Officers interrupted a break-in at a market on Pioneer Drive**

Officers were sent at 11:46 p.m. to a market on the 3,400 block of Pioneer Drive in east Bakersfield after a caller reported two to three people removing boxes of merchandise through an open gate, one of them holding an object the caller believed was a firearm.

**Fire crews held a brush fire off Washington Street and worked a fire on the highway**

Fire crews worked a vegetation fire of about three and a half acres at 7:22 p.m. off Washington Street, a couple of streets south of East California Avenue, and reported good progress, expecting to tie in a patrol line within about ten minutes; crews also staffed a second fire nearby. Shortly after 9 p.m., crews were sent to a fire along westbound Highway 58 just west of the Mount Vernon Avenue exit, where a second ambulance was requested and a traffic break was set up so crews could approach.
