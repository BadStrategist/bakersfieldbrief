# What Happened Overnight in Bakersfield & Kern County

Agents working a burglary on a train container northeast of Woodford near Tehachapi came under fire shortly before 11 p.m., and deputies spent the small hours looking for a man who ran from the scene. The night's other gunfire calls came at 6:05 a.m., when officers took about four reports of shots heard in the area of Morrill Avenue and Airport Drive. Weapons calls ran the evening: officers were sent at 7:04 p.m. to the 2,700 block of Ming Avenue for a robbery at a clothing store, where a man in a ski mask took merchandise and left eastbound in a white sedan; officers were sent into the 10th Street area at 8:14 p.m. for a domestic argument where the other person in the home was reported to have a firearm; and deputies were sent at 7:14 p.m. to the 8,400 block of Panama Road for a man reported to be holding a kitchen knife. Fire crews worked four structure fires, starting at 7:31 p.m. on Ledgeview Lane in southwest Bakersfield. The last was an apartment on the 2,200 block of Nelson Street at 10:31 p.m. Deputies and fire crews searched the south side for a possible drowning at 3:18 a.m., and medics were sent at 4:12 a.m. to the 900 block of Olive Drive for a 2-year-old reported not to be breathing.

## The big one

**Agents on a train burglary came under fire northeast of Woodford near Tehachapi**

Deputies were sent at 10:49 p.m. to the railroad northeast of Woodford, near Tehachapi, after agents working an interrupted burglary of a train container reported that people inside the container shot at them. Two agents were reported not to be hurt and took cover behind their vehicles.

Units staged about a half mile out and worked out access from the Highway 58 side and off Woodford and Keene. Deputies were told that one suspect was believed to have fled south and then southwest, with no description, and crews asked a hospital to confirm there were no gunshot victims. The railroad was cleared to resume traffic about 4 a.m.

The only other gunfire calls of the night came at 6:05 a.m., when officers took about four reports of shots heard in the area of Morrill Avenue and Airport Drive.

Weapons calls were the other thread through the evening. Officers were sent at 7:04 p.m. to the 2,700 block of Ming Avenue for a robbery that had just happened at a clothing store, where a man in a ski mask took merchandise and left eastbound in a white sedan. Officers were sent into the 10th Street area at 8:14 p.m. for a domestic argument where the other person in the home was reported to have a firearm, and a roommate was reported to have threatened to shoot the person who called. Deputies were sent at 7:14 p.m. to the 8,400 block of Panama Road for a report of a man with a kitchen knife; he was reported to be inside the residence and no longer holding it.

## Around the county

**Fire crews worked four structure fires through the night**

Crews were sent at 7:31 p.m. to a structure fire on Ledgeview Lane in southwest Bakersfield, at 8:29 p.m. to a structure fire on North Alberta Street in south Bakersfield with three engines and a battalion chief assigned, and at 8:45 p.m. to a structure fire on Campus Park Court in southwest Bakersfield with two engines and a truck assigned. The last of the four was an apartment on the 2,200 block of Nelson Street in east Bakersfield at 10:31 p.m.

**Deputies and fire crews searched the south side for a possible drowning**

Crews were sent at 3:18 a.m. to South Mount Vernon Avenue and East Belle Terrace after a caller said someone had fallen at a water pipe, then said his brother had been thrown into a cement water tank and could be heard inside. Crews searched south along Mount Vernon, the caller reported he was climbing the water tower himself, and fire crews asked for an additional unit.

**Domestic and family crisis calls ran from the evening into the morning**

Officers were sent at 7:43 p.m. to the 2,400 block of Calloway Drive in northwest Bakersfield after a caller reported his wife was in crisis and had talked about harming their 7-month-old child; she was reported to have no firearm in the home. Deputies were sent at 8:48 p.m. to the 500 block of Shady Meadow for a domestic disturbance that had just happened, where the man left westbound in a blue semi truck.

**Crashes and a chase ran the small hours**

A vehicle struck a pedestrian at 8:56 p.m. at Sterling Road and Monterey Street, where CPR was under way. Deputies chased a vehicle northbound on Airport Drive in north Bakersfield from Lincoln Street at 2:20 a.m., at speeds reported from 35 to 67 mph past Woodrow and Ann Arbor, and called the pursuit off northwest of the airport. Fire crews and medics were sent at 2:33 a.m. to a single vehicle that rolled over into a house, with two people hurt and a second ambulance requested, and at 4:44 a.m. to a vehicle in a ditch just east of East Panama Lane and South Fairfax Road that was reported to be smoking.

## Night notes

Gunfire was the night's spine: agents on a railroad burglary came under fire northeast of Tehachapi just before 11 p.m., and about four callers reported shots heard near Morrill Avenue and Airport Drive at 6:05 a.m. Fire crews worked four structure fires between 7:31 p.m. and 10:31 p.m. and two smaller fires before dawn, while deputies searched the south side for a possible drowning at 3:18 a.m. The domestic and weapons calls were steady from 7 p.m. to dawn, and the crashes ran from a pedestrian struck at 8:56 p.m. to a hit and run on Highway 99 at 5:52 a.m.
