 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 6am

Overnight: shooting, Baker St

24 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The night’s most serious call came just after midnight when a witness reported seeing someone shot at a Taco Bell in the Baker Street area. Officers responded with an officer-safety broadcast after the witness described the suspect as a white male in his 40s to 50s wearing a black shirt and dark pants, sitting inside a white vehicle — possibly a Ford Explorer. Earlier, around 2:16 a.m., flames were visible inside a vacant single-story house on King Street with heavy smoke showing.

Multiple engines and a truck company responded, and the fire was under control by about 2:25. Just before 11 p.m., a two-vehicle collision left one vehicle on fire near Gosford Road and Harris Road. Engine 14 and Engine 9 responded — one car was smoking with another about a quarter mile back — and command dubbed it the Harris Incident.

Injuries were unknown. Multiple reports of a grass fire drew a large response — engines, patrol units, a water tender, a dozer and a helicopter — to southbound Highway 395 north of Brown Road. Crews found a small roadside fire that was quickly extinguished, and the extra equipment was canceled.

Around 10:38 p.m., an assault with a knife was reported with no injuries. The suspect — about 6 feet tall with neck tattoos, wearing black shorts, a gray shirt and white shoes — fled on foot northbound.

 Something Good

A local family is hosting a Saturday flea market to raise $8,900 for Villafana’s prosthetic leg. The event aims to support Villafana in obtaining a prosthetic leg, and the community is coming together to help make it happen. It’s a heartwarming reminder of the kindness and generosity that exists right here in Kern County.
