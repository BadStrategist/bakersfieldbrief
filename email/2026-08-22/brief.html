The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 6am

Overnight: shooting, Baker St

24 incidents tracked overnight — full report · locations generalized

The night’s most serious call came just after midnight, when a witness reported seeing someone shot at a Taco Bell in the Baker Street area. Officers broadcast an alert for a suspect described as a white male in his 40s to 50s, wearing a black shirt and dark pants, sitting inside a white vehicle — possibly a Ford Explorer. Earlier in the evening, around 10:38 p.m., an assault with a knife was reported; the suspect — about 6 feet tall with neck tattoos, wearing black shorts, a gray shirt and white shoes — fled on foot northbound.

Crews also knocked down a fire at a vacant house on King Street around 2:16 a.m., and just before 11 p.m., a two-vehicle collision left one vehicle on fire near Gosford Road and Harris Road. That call, dubbed the Harris Incident, drew Engine 14 and Engine 9 to the scene. Multiple reports of a grass fire along southbound Highway 395 north of Brown Road drew a heavy response — engines, patrol units, a water tender, a dozer and a helicopter — but crews found a small roadside fire that was quickly extinguished, and the extra equipment was canceled.

Elsewhere, a subject was reported with a firearm in his waistband on Houchin Road around 10:04 p.m., and an assault was reported on Birch Avenue in Wasco around 11:22 p.m. Medics were dispatched to that call. A hit-and-run involving a black Camry and a white sedan was reported on Monterey Street around 11:23 p.m., and two fire hydrants were reported spilling water on 22nd Street between Q and P streets around 11:53 p.m.

Around midnight, about a half acre was burning behind a residence on Windsong Street, with crews working and additional resources requested. A suspicious vehicle check on Culiacan Avenue around 12:09 a.m. involved a Honda Civic occupied by a woman in her 20s. A family disturbance on Suncrest Drive around 12:10 a.m. involved an intoxicated teenager who came home, with a family member holding him down.

And a welfare check was requested around 12:12 a.m. for a man lying on the ground near the curb on 26th Street.

Today’s Weather

We’re in for a hot Saturday across Kern County, with the high expected to reach about 100°. Right now at Meadows Field, it’s already 97°, and we’ll see temperatures drop to a low of about 74° overnight. There are no active weather alerts for our area today. Our air quality is holding at a moderate 74 AQI — not the best, but not the worst we’ve seen. Stay hydrated if you’re out and about, and try to stay cool.

Something Good

A local family is hosting a Saturday flea market to raise $8,900 for Villafana’s prosthetic leg. The community event aims to support a neighbor in need, and it’s a reminder of the kindness and generosity that make Kern County such a special place to call home. If you’re out and about today, consider stopping by to show your support.
