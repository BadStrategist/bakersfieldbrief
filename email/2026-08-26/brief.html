 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 6am

Overnight: suspicious_circumstances, 2400 block South M St

3 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Overnight activity was relatively light across Kern County, though a few notable incidents kept law enforcement busy. Around 9:18 p.m., officers responded to repeated intruder reports on the 2400 block of South M Street. A resident barricaded himself in his bathroom with his dog after an unknown subject was reported attempting to enter his apartment for a fourth time.

The situation kept officers on scene into the evening. Elsewhere, at 22:17, a juvenile fled in a vehicle in Bakersfield, prompting a brief search. A family member followed but had not yet provided a direction of travel or a description of the vehicle.

Later, at 05:09, police staged for a subject experiencing a mental-health crisis on the 2800 block of University Avenue. Overall, the night was calm with no major incidents reported elsewhere in the county.
