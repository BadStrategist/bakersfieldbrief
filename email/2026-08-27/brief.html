 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 6am

Overnight: medical_aid, White Ln

4 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Overnight activity across Kern County was light, with no major incidents reported anywhere in the county. Responders handled a possible overdose, two medical-welfare calls, and a downed tree branch over the course of the night. Around 4:35 a.m., medical responders were called to a motel on White Lane for a possible overdose and administered Narcan after the caller was transferred to medical aid.

Just after midnight, a medical aid call came in from Stine Road. Several people got on the phone asking for an ambulance, but no one could explain what the situation was. Shortly before 12:05 a.m., units were sent to check on an elderly woman with dementia who could not be reached at her home on Mountain Oak Road.

At 9:40 p.m., a tree branch came down onto a telephone wire and a vehicle in an unincorporated part of the county, reported as a hazard condition.
