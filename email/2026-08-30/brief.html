Overnight, Kern County crews responded to several incidents across the county. Around 5:59 a.m., firefighters were working an orchard fire in rural Kern County that was reported as actively spreading. Meanwhile, late Friday night, officers were sent to a strong-arm robbery near East California Avenue and Williams Street, where medical aid was also dispatched to the scene.

A vehicle crashed into a pole on Niles Street early Saturday morning, prompting a full rescue response. In east Bakersfield, a structure fire was tied to a burnt electrical outlet. Overnight calls also included a welfare check on a woman on oxygen whose husband hadn’t returned after stepping out to take out the trash.

 Something Good

23ABC highlights a heartwarming community event: Nova Sorella, a Bakersfield nonprofit, hosted a resource fair Saturday aimed at serving residents who are underserved and often overlooked. The event brought together organizations like Serving Hope and The Open Door Network to connect community members with essential services. Founder PJ Jones organized the fair in honor of her son, Ty Jr. Jones, who was killed by gun violence in January 2017. Saturday marked his birthday, and the fair was meant to bring hope to a community that is underserved, undervalued, and often overlooked.
