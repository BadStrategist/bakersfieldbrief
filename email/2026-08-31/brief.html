 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 6am

Overnight: working_fire, Cottonwood Rd and Watts Dr

32 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The night brought a mix of incidents across Kern County, from fires to welfare checks. Around 11:26 p.m., crews responded to a reported fire on Pacheco Road. By 11:35, units described the tractor as a total loss, with the trailer being salvaged.

Shortly before midnight, a caller reported hearing a shot from inside his living room, and medical units were on scene. Around 2:48 a.m., officers went to Ginger Oak Lane for a welfare check with medical aid requested. The reporting party was crying and unable to answer questions, with a baby heard crying in the background; a subject was later seen running through the carport toward a warehouse, and units held the perimeter.

Around 12:58 a.m., officers were sent to Potomac Avenue near Oswell Street for a large party that turned into a physical fight, with one caller reporting possible shots heard — unclear whether fireworks. Around 10:08 p.m., a man threatened to shoot the reporting party’s husband during a verbal dispute at a Bakersfield apartment complex. No weapon was seen at first, but the same subject was later seen walking through the complex with a baseball bat.

Other incidents included a collision near Panama Lane and Ashe Road, a verbal dispute during which a possible gunshot was heard, a physical fight at an apartment on H Street, a trucker on the highway needing CHP to arrange a tow, an attempt to locate a 7-year-old last seen on a residence’s front porch wearing a black shirt, a welfare check on a woman who told bystanders she had been thrown out of a vehicle, medical aid for an unknown complaint on South 8th Street, medical aid for a possible overdose where Narcan was administered, a search for a suspect armed with a switchblade who fled on foot north on Mount Vernon Avenue, medical aid on South Haley Street where interpreters were requested, medical aid for a seizure, and a man on a scooter reportedly armed with a knife — and possibly a gun — last seen near a 711 parking lot in the Olive Drive area.

 Something Good

The League of Dreams tipped off its 2026 basketball season with more than 140 athletes and introduced a new mascot. This community-focused event provides opportunities for individuals with physical and developmental disabilities to participate in sports. The gym filled with cheers as teams came together for the season opener, and the organization’s first-ever mascot, Dreamer, made its debut. The event also featured a special appearance by the Cal State Bakersfield women’s basketball team. Board Vice President Laura Avila noted that community support makes these programs possible and that donations and partnerships are the foundation of everything the league offers. This season marks a milestone for the organization and brings joy to many families in Kern County.
