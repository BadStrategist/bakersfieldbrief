 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 6am

What happened overnight: Vehicle rollover at Hwy 58 west Tucker Rd

14 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

It was a steady night for emergency crews across the county as Monday’s heat lingered into the evening and carried a familiar rhythm of calls into the early hours. Just after midnight, a vehicle rollover on westbound Highway 58 west of Tucker Road brought a multi-unit response, including a battalion chief, with an air unit standing by as a precaution. Westbound lanes were reduced while crews worked the scene; the extent of any injuries was not immediately available.

Around 1:48 a.m., officers were sent to a residence in the Coburn Ridge area on reports of five to six shots fired, with callers describing subjects in ski masks at the door and a vehicle fleeing the area. The reporting party was checking security cameras as officers responded. Elsewhere in the county, a grass fire off Wasco Pond Road north of Cecil Avenue drew an engine, a patrol unit, and additional assistance around 1:30 a.m., with crews stopping the fire’s progression and being released from the scene.

In north Bakersfield, officers ran a short foot pursuit of a male on a dirt bike along a Chester Avenue bike path, losing visual at Chester Avenue before units began searching the area. Deputies and medics also responded to a residence on Marble Street just before 11 p.m., where a 17-year-old was reported making suicidal statements; family members were contacted. A separate crisis call on West China Grade drew a mental health response, and a domestic disturbance on Meadows Street involving juveniles prompted officers to respond given the concern for children.

The overnight log included a steady stream of lesser calls as well: a small roadside fire on northbound Highway 99 south of Olive Drive, a vehicle fire on Elk Hills Road that turned out to be a dropped flare, a search for a subject in dark clothing last seen heading toward the Mount Vernon Bridge, and a welfare check on a missing 15-year-old last seen near Bernard Street. Crews also handled a court order violation with a firearm threat at Virginia Avenue and Powell Street, and CHP responded to a family member walking on the shoulder of northbound I-5 near the escape ramp. The night was busy, but by sunrise the county had settled into a routine, quieter Tuesday morning.

 Today's Weather

Expect a pleasant September day with a high near 85 degrees and a low around 58 degrees tonight. It’s currently 72 degrees at Meadows Field (KBFL), with no active weather alerts for Kern County. Air quality is sitting at 57, which is moderate — sensitive individuals may want to take it a bit easier outdoors, but most residents will find conditions just fine. Fire crews are currently monitoring one active fire in Kern, the Rose Fire, so smoke-sensitive folks should stay aware of their local conditions.

 Something Good

One of the more heartening items on the calendar this week is the Spanish Speaking Conference, which KGET 17 reports will connect families with special resources available in the community. The event offers local families a chance to find support, information, and connections in a setting designed to meet them where they are. It’s a reminder that in Bakersfield, help is often just a conversation away — and that the community’s strength is built on neighbors reaching out to neighbors. For families navigating the often-complicated world of special needs services, having a dedicated space like this can make all the difference.
