 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 6am

What happened overnight: Vegetation fire at Cottonwood Rd & East Pacheco Rd

17 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

It was a busy but thankfully non-fatal night across Kern County, with the most serious call coming just after midnight on Highway 58. Around 12:41 a.m., a rollover crash on westbound 58 west of Tucker Road drew a multi-unit response including a battalion chief, with an air unit standing by. Westbound lanes were reduced while crews worked the scene, though the extent of any injuries has not been released.

Just over an hour later, at roughly 1:48 a.m., dispatchers sent officers to a home in the Coburn Ridge area on reports of five to six shots fired. Callers said subjects in ski masks had been seen at the door, and the reporting party was pulling camera footage as a vehicle fled the area. Officers responded to the scene.

Elsewhere around the county, crews knocked down a grass fire off Wasco Pond Road north of Cecil Avenue around 1:30 a.m., with fire progression stopped after an engine, patrol unit, and additional help arrived. In Bakersfield, a short foot pursuit of a man on a dirt bike along a Chester Avenue bike path ended around 9:58 p.m. when visual contact was lost near Chester; units were searching the area. Earlier in the evening, a call at Virginia Avenue and Powell Street reported a male subject making threats to obtain a firearm, drawing additional officers to the scene.

The overnight log also included a steady run of welfare checks and mental health calls — a 17-year-old on Marble Street reported making suicidal statements around 10:48 p.m. drew officers and medics, a female in crisis on the West China Grade was attended to around 10:44 p.m., and a welfare check was conducted for a 15-year-old reported missing and last seen near Bernard Street just after midnight. Crews also handled a CHP-reported roadside fire on northbound Highway 99 south of Olive Drive at 9:32 p.m., and a report of a vehicle fire on Elk Hills Road at 11:32 p.m. that turned out to be a dropped flare with no active fire.

 Today's Weather

It's a mild start to September out there. The current observation at Meadows Field is 61 degrees, with today's high expected to reach only 82 and tonight's low dipping to around 60. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, and the clear conditions should hold through the day. Air quality sits at 53, which is moderate — fine for most people, though those sensitive to particulates may want to keep outdoor exertion light. Monday's heat has broken, and it looks like a pleasant, seasonable stretch ahead. No active fires are being tracked locally.

 Something Good

There's a bit of welcome news in the arts world this week — KGET 17 reports that the Portrait of a Warrior Gallery is moving to a new location and will become a neighbor to Woolworth's next year. It's an exciting new chapter for the gallery and a nice addition to a downtown block that's been growing steadily.

And for something a little different, Bakersfield College's new quarterback literally traveled around the planet to wear a Renegades uniform. It's a reminder that even in a city as rooted in tradition as Bakersfield, the community keeps finding new connections to the wider world — and that local sports can draw talent from anywhere.
