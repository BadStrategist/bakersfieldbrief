 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Vegetation Fire at railroad tracks north Hwy 58

20 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The night in Kern County was busy for first responders, with the most significant call coming just before dawn. Fire crews were dispatched shortly after 5 a.m. to a vegetation fire burning near the Union Pacific Railroad tracks north of Highway 58. The assignment grew quickly as two engines, three patrol units, a water tender, and two dozers were called in, along with multiple battalion chiefs, a division supervisor, and a safety officer.

A helicopter was placed on standby, and air-to-ground coordination was requested if needed, with additional strike-team resources held ready as the fire burned in the rail corridor. Earlier in the night, a pursuit through town kept officers occupied. Around 12:09 a.m., a chase ran westbound along California Avenue, with speeds called out from roughly 60 to 95 mph.

Dispatchers noted light traffic and no pedestrians as the vehicle turned off the avenue, headed north, and eventually pulled into a residence parking lot where units moved in. Just after 2 a.m., a separate pursuit ended at a home and prompted a neighborhood search. Officers checked a backyard and an attic after one person was reported in the backyard and a second was last seen walking west.

A K-9 unit and additional officers fanned out on foot, checking spots where someone might hide. In the river bottom, crews spent part of the evening on a brush fire burning in heavy grass and brush. Reported around 10:37 p.m., the fire covered roughly a quarter to a half-acre and moved slowly while crews weighed how to reach it.

Access from the river side was being considered as additional resources were requested. And around 4:06 a.m., a tractor-trailer went down in the number four lane of southbound Interstate 5, with the driver out placing warning triangles and a second semi on the right shoulder. Units at the scene asked for a heavy-duty tow to clear the crash.

The overnight log also included a domestic disturbance at Roseman Mobile Home Park just after midnight, in which the caller reported a man with access to a firearm refusing to let her leave; a report from a juvenile on a Quincy Street block who alleged her mother battered her; a shots-heard call in central Bakersfield tied to a prior weapons report; and an open-line call from a Delwood Avenue block that disconnected with no answer on callback.

 Today's Weather

Expect a pleasant late-summer day across Kern County. The high today should reach near 86 degrees, with overnight lows settling around 63. It's currently 63 degrees at Meadows Field (KBFL), with no active weather alerts for the area. Air quality is measured at 51, which lands in the moderate range — fine for most outdoor plans, though sensitive groups may want to take it easy during the peak of the afternoon. The weekend's cooling trend, fueled by a North Pacific trough easing SoCal heat, is expected to carry into the week ahead, keeping things comfortable.
