 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Structure Fire at Planz Rd block

15 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The overnight hours kept first responders moving across the county. Just after 5 a.m., a vegetation fire along the Union Pacific Railroad tracks north of Highway 58 drew a full wildland assignment. Two engines, three patrol units, a water tender and two dozers were dispatched, along with multiple battalion chiefs, a division supervisor and a safety officer.

A helicopter was placed on standby and air-to-ground coordination was requested, with additional strike-team resources held ready as crews worked to keep the flames from spreading. No injuries were reported, and the fire was knocked down before sunrise. Earlier, around 12:09 a.m., a pursuit ran westbound along California Avenue with speeds called out from 60 to 95 miles per hour.

Dispatchers noted light traffic and no pedestrians as the vehicle turned north and eventually pulled into a residence parking lot, where units moved in. Then at 2:11 a.m., a separate incident sent officers searching through a backyard and an attic after a pursuit ended at a home. One person was reported to be in the backyard, a second was last seen walking west, and a K-9 unit joined as officers fanned out on foot, checking spots where someone could hide.

In the river bottom, a brush fire burning in heavy grass and riparian vegetation was reported around 10:37 p.m. at roughly a quarter- to half-acre. The fire moved slowly, but crews had to figure out how to reach it, weighing access from the river side while requesting additional resources as the flames stayed in heavy fuels. Later, around 4:06 a.m., a semi truck went down in the number four lane of southbound Interstate 5; the driver was out placing warning triangles, and a second semi sat on the right shoulder while units called for a heavy-duty tow.

Anyone heading down I-5 this morning should expect potential delays in that area. The overnight log also included an armed robbery investigation at 10:15 p.m., with the victim describing a suspect in a green shirt, gray pants, green hat and white shoes. At 11:01 p.m., officers were sent to a convenience store parking lot for a man with an open alcohol container who was refusing to leave and harassing customers.

A theft reported earlier at a Ming Avenue home improvement store was passed to patrol units with a suspect vehicle description and left pending for service, and at 11:38 p.m., deputies requested backup for a large gathering at a lake recreation area.

 Today's Weather

Expect a mostly sunny day with a high near 91 degrees and a low around 64 overnight. It’s currently 64 at Meadows Field, with no active weather alerts and air quality at 57, which is moderate. That means it’s fine for most outdoor plans, though sensitive groups might want to take it easy this afternoon. There are no fires currently impacting the valley, and the breeze should stay light through the day.

 Something Good

There’s a bright spot on the CSUB campus this week: The Nest, the university’s new grocery-store-style food pantry, is open and free for students and staff. The expanded 2,500-square-foot space is the result of years of planning and a student referendum, and it’s already meeting a huge need — the pantry saw more than 25,000 visits last year and hands out roughly 3,000 pounds of food weekly. It’s the kind of resource that makes a real difference for the 4 in 10 students who face food insecurity.

And for those already thinking ahead to the holidays, organizers have started working on the 2026 Bakersfield Christmas Parade. It’s a beloved community tradition, and knowing plans are moving along is a cheerful thought for a warm September morning.
