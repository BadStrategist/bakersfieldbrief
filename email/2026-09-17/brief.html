 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Vehicle Pursuit at Hampton Ave area

19 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The overnight hours had one significant moment and it ended quietly. Just before 1 a.m., an officer broadcast a Code 33 — the radio signal that clears the channel for emergency traffic — while following a vehicle westbound on Hampton Avenue. Units called out their positions block by block as the vehicle appeared to yield, then made a quick correction on the location and confirmed the driver had stopped.

Multiple officers arrived behind the scene, and once the stop was complete the channel was opened back to normal traffic. The night’s most serious call came after 2:20 a.m., when officers and Liberty Ambulance paramedics were sent to a park on Hawthorne Avenue. Dispatch described a man in a black shirt and blue jeans with facial injuries who said he had been jumped.

He was last seen walking eastbound on Hawthorne from the park, and a short time later an engine was cleared to an updated address on the same street as the response shifted. Fire crews dealt with a familiar kind of nuisance late in the evening. Battalion 6 was sent to a refuse fire burning inside a decommissioned tank that crews recognized as one previously used for burning copper wire.

The battalion downgraded the assignment and kept it with the companies already on scene rather than sending a larger response. Other calls filled out the log. Officers were sent to an apartment in the 6200 block of Victor Street after a caller said a man and a woman were taking boards off the place and entering the residence.

A prowler report at a car wash in the 8100 block of East Brundage Lane brought descriptions of a woman in a tie-dye shirt and black shorts and a man in a hunter-green ball cap, green shirt and blue jeans; dispatchers noted the pair might be connected to an earlier vandalism call. The broader log included a 9:32 p.m. welfare check on a Smith Street block, a 9:46 p.m. verbal disturbance on Breezeway Drive involving an ex-boyfriend who would not leave, a 10:07 p.m. battery report east of Haley Street and Panorama Drive in which security said a woman in her mid-twenties was belligerent and battering her friend, and just after 11 p.m. a semi reported stopped on the right-hand shoulder of a county highway.
