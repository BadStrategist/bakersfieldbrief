 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Brush Fire at Lake Ming area

13 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The night was anything but still for Kern County’s first responders, with a dramatic water rescue, a foot chase, and a hit-and-run on the interstate keeping crews moving late into the evening. The most significant call came from the Kern River near the Kern River Golf Course, where a woman was reported to have gone into the water and floated for roughly an hour toward Lake Ming. The reporting party, who had been in the river with her, told dispatchers that both had gotten out and there was no reason she would have gone back in, but crews still launched a search of the bank behind the golf course.

After a tense period of searching, responders reported they had located her at a camp just southeast of the golf course area, bringing the incident to a safe close. Elsewhere, officers were involved in a foot chase along the canal bank near Rosedale Highway after a man with a satchel was tracked southbound and then eastbound toward the water. A caller reported seeing him discard items near a chain link fence in a clearing by a tree, and officers eventually recovered currency from the suspect.

They asked for a victim to be brought by for an in-field identification, though no further details were immediately available. On the roads, CHP was hunting a Freightliner Cascadia that struck a Kenworth on northbound Interstate 5 just north of the county line and kept going. The victim vehicle was still on the shoulder less than half a mile north of the county line about two hours later, a reminder of how close calls can linger on the highway.

Fire crews also stayed busy with a shed fire on the 800 block of Exchange Street, where people at the property were trying to put it out before engines arrived, and a rubbish fire along the railroad tracks on Edison Highway. A man on 19th Street told dispatchers he had been knocked unconscious and was bleeding, prompting an ambulance request. The overnight log also included a welfare check on East Cedar Street at Franklin Field, where a man was reported in a 2008 Ford Escape with a rope, and a vehicle-versus-pedestrian incident at Lincoln Street and Pierce, where the pedestrian was up and walking and did not appear injured.

 Today's Weather

It’s a classic late-summer day in Kern County, with the mercury climbing to a high near 91 degrees and dipping to a comfortable low around 66 degrees overnight. Right now, it’s a mild 66 degrees at Meadows Field, and there are no active weather alerts for the county. Air quality is sitting at a moderate 58 on the AQI scale, so sensitive groups might want to take it a little easier outdoors, but for most of us it’s a pleasant morning to get out and enjoy the sunshine before the heat builds.

 Something Good

On a lighter note, California State University, Bakersfield has launched a mariachi course, giving students the chance to learn and perform a beloved musical tradition right here on campus. It’s a joyful way to celebrate cultural richness and bring the community together through music. Whether you’re a student or just a fan of the genre, it’s a sweet reminder of the good things happening in our valley.
