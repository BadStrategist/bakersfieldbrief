 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Kidnapping / Assault With A Firearm at 1100 block 3rd St

15 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The most significant overnight incident came just before 11 p.m. when a city-wide officer safety broadcast was issued for a man armed with a knife at a hospital on Mount Vernon Avenue. He was escorted off the property and last seen heading toward East High School. Officers on both radio channels received his description, and the call was cleared once he was off the grounds.

Around the county, a caller stayed on the line with sheriff's dispatchers for nearly 20 minutes just before 10 p.m., reporting that people looking for him were armed and still in dark clothing in an east Bakersfield park. Deputies responded but were told to stand by while the call remained open. Early this morning at 1:45 a.m., two units responded to an assault with a bat in the 2700 block of White Lane.

No one answered when dispatchers called back, and a description of the person who was hit went out to responding officers. Shortly after midnight, a caller reported a woman screaming for help in the 100 block of Hart Street near the river bed. The caller rang back a minute later to say the screaming could still be heard and asked officers to keep coming.

Fire crews handled two outside fires near Mount Vernon Avenue overnight – one along westbound Highway 58 just west of the avenue at 12:31 a.m., and a second at Mount Vernon Avenue and Kentucky Street at 1:41 a.m. The overnight log also included a disturbance at a Motel 6 on Oak Street, a welfare check on children with a mother reported to be in mental health crisis in the 4600 block of Columbus Street, and a disturbance at a Circle K in the 5600 block of Auburn Street where two people were swinging at the air with a 17-year-old present.
