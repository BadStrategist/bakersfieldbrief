 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Structure Fire at 600 block Goodman St

19 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The night's most significant call came just after 5 a.m., when firefighters were dispatched to a house fire on Goodman Street. Arriving crews reported smoke showing, then fire coming from the front of a single-story, single-family home. The alarm was reinforced, and incoming units were held for assignments while crews went to work.

Command reported the fire knocked down within a few minutes and started a primary search of the home, with a truck preparing for vertical ventilation. No injuries were immediately reported. Earlier, at 3:08 a.m., units were sent to a shooting call on King Street.

Fire crews already on a medical aid at the same location relayed that a shooting had occurred there and that a victim had an injury. Deputies were dispatched to take the scene. Around 1 a.m., officers responded to the area of a hospital and the juvenile justice center after a report of a transient man with no shirt waving a machete and yelling.

The description was broadcast for officer safety, but officers checked the parking lot and surrounding blocks and did not find him. At 4:37 a.m., a truck, two engines and a battalion were sent to a vehicle into a pole at Calloway Drive and Tuscarora Drive. Crews reported the scene and cleared the call.

Just before 10 p.m. Sunday, dispatchers advised units that family at a command post were trying to find a 13-year-old girl whose phone last exchanged a text message with family about 9:08 p.m. and had not moved since, showing just east of one of the buildings at the fairgrounds. She was described as wearing a white long-sleeved shirt, dark blue jeans and white shoes, and was believed to be with two other teenagers and their families.

At 2:36 a.m., officers were sent to a location on North Chester Avenue after a caller reported being assaulted by two females. The caller said the suspects were across the street from the address, and dispatchers noted the call was tied to an earlier call for service at the same location. The overnight log also included a report of a man down on Union Avenue around 9:20 p.m., with units sent after a caller reported a man with no shirt and dark pants lying on the sidewalk.

The hours between 3 and 5 a.m. were mostly welfare checks and medical calls.

 Today's Weather

Expect a high near 80 degrees today and a low around 59 tonight. It's currently 55 degrees at Meadows Field. The National Weather Service in Hanford has issued a Lake Wind Advisory in effect until 7 p.m., so boaters and anyone near local lakes should use caution. Air quality is good, with an AQI reading of 44. No active fires are being reported in the county.

 Something Good

California has officially designated September 25 as Dolly Parton Day, with a Bakersfield senator leading the effort to honor the beloved entertainer. The recognition celebrates Parton's contributions to music and literacy, bringing a moment of statewide pride to Kern County. It's a nice reminder that even in a busy news week, there's room for a little joy and gratitude.
