 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Grass Fire at County Line Rd east Rd 40

21 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The largest call of the night came at 1:46 a.m., when crews were sent toward County Line Road east of Road 40 after callers reported a large column of smoke and a glow on the horizon. What began as a structure fire had spread into grass and was pushing north at a slow rate of spread, settling at about five acres. Crews anchored off the roadway and worked the heel and flanks while a second unit covered the structures on the south side.

Command reported the fire contained and crews in cleanup, and mutual aid requested from Tulare County was canceled. Earlier, at 10:18 p.m., a patrol, two engines and a battalion were sent to the Morning Drive area, where grass the size of a house was burning near a church off Auburn Street after multiple callers reported it. The full assignment went to the fire.

At 1:28 a.m., crews reported light smoke showing from one side of a building in the 900 block of Baker Street and said they were going in to investigate, with incoming units staged and an incident commander assigned. An earlier alarm at the same block was cleared after a crew walked the building and found no audible alarm and no sign of distress or hazard inside. Deputies had their hands full as well.

At 12:47 a.m. a priority one domestic call was broadcast for the 9500 block of Brimhall Road, behind a business; the suspect was described as a man in his 30s in all black who left in a gray pickup, with a second man in dark clothing in an unknown sedan. At 12:52 a.m. deputies were sent to a suspicious person call involving a man with a firearm reported knocking on doors. At 1:14 a.m., a resident reported that a neighbor had come into the home through an unlocked front door, gone as far as a bedroom door and then crossed the street — described as a man 18 to 20 years old in a black shirt and jeans, slim with curly hair.

And at 3:01 a.m., several units were called to assist after a robbery was reported; officers put one person in custody, and the victim told officers the robbery happened just east of where the stop was made. The rest of the overnight log was medical aids, alarm calls and traffic stops.

 Today's Weather

It's currently 79 degrees at Meadows Field, on the way to a high near 86 this afternoon and a low around 63 tonight. No active weather alerts are in effect for Kern County, and air quality is 72 — moderate, which is a reasonable day to be outside if you're not especially sensitive to smoke or dust. No active fires were reported in the county as of this morning beyond the overnight incidents crews handled. Expect a warm, still September day across the valley, with the usual evening cooldown after sundown.

 Something Good

For the second year, the director of the Mission at Kern County will hold a 24-hour conversation aimed at raising awareness about addiction, and this year he's adding a focus on prevention. It continues a local effort to keep the community talking about recovery and support — and the stories from last year, from both families in pain and people who came out the other side, are exactly the kind of thing that keeps that conversation honest. If you've been touched by addiction in your own family, you'll recognize the weight of it.
