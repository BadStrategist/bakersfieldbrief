 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Shooting at east Bakersfield

24 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The overnight log opened with a shooting call at 12:25 a.m. in east Bakersfield, where a caller told officers a man stepped out of a white four-door sedan, pointed a gun in the air and fired one round, and that a person was bleeding. A second party was reported to have driven a white four-door to a nearby motel, and units were checking the area through the early hours. Not long before that, at 12:18 a.m., crews were sent to an audible burglary alarm at a home on Millwood Way, near Thornwood Drive and Woodcastle Way, to cover the perimeter and the living room door.

At 1:19 a.m., a person walked into a business on Chester Avenue with a stab wound, and deputies and a fire crew responded to the area around Decatur Street and North Chester Avenue. On the highways, two semis — a maroon rig with a silver trailer and a Walmart semi — collided at 4:33 a.m. in the eastbound lanes of Highway 46 west of Interstate 5 and ended up stuck together in the roadway, forcing a backup and closing an exit while drivers were routed around. Earlier, at 10:39 p.m., a semi was reported on fire in the westbound lanes of Highway 58, and fire crews from a nearby station knocked down a grass fire in the same stretch shortly after.

At 5:12 a.m., a silver sedan rolled over near East Brundage Lane and Kincaid Street, drawing a full rescue response; all parties were reported out of the vehicles. The rest of the log was the usual mix — medical aids on Ponce de Leon Avenue, Primrose Avenue, and at White Lane and Hughes Lane, a vehicle fire north of Panama Road and Cottonwood Road, a shots-fired call on Hall Road near the Rasmussen Center, a robbery alarm at a Dollar General on the 2900 block of Niles Street, and a possible drunken driver reported in a white Mercedes SUV that couldn't hold its speed.

 Today's Weather

It's currently 88 degrees at Meadows Field, on the way to a high near 98 and a low around 68 tonight. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, but fire crews are on one active fire in the county — the Grapevine Fire — so the dry heat is worth keeping in mind if you're out and about. Air quality readings weren't available this morning. If you've got outdoor plans, the cooler hours are early and late; the middle of the day will feel like the valley in October usually does.

 Something Good

The Running with the Angels 5K is returning to Bakersfield, and it's the kind of event that matters quietly. It's a community gathering for families honoring babies lost too soon — a shared morning to remember and to stand with one another. If you've been looking for a way to support neighbors carrying that kind of loss, this is a gentle place to start.
