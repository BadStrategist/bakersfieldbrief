Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Shooting at 5400 block Pembroke Ave

35 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The night's biggest call came just after 1:20 a.m., when officers and firefighters were sent to a residence in the 5400 block of Pembroke Avenue in southwest Bakersfield for a report of a shooting. Crews arrived to find two patients with gunshot wounds and quickly set up a perimeter around the block, holding family members back while units moved in. Hall Ambulance staged to enter from the east, and officers asked firefighters to light up the roof to check it for evidence.

The scene was secured and turned over to investigators, and the department had not released any details on the victims' conditions by morning. Deputies in the same hour worked a knife attack on the 1500 block of Lincoln Street. Callers reported a man chasing a woman down the street and attacking others, and radio traffic indicated his mother was threatened with the knife and a sister stepped in front of it.

Deputies found the suspect in a cluttered shed area at the back corner of the property and were still sorting out who was hurt. A separate 911 call from nearby reported only a verbal disturbance, and the woman on that call denied anything on the callback. Elsewhere, officers picked up a newer white Honda Accord reported stolen and moved to stop it near College Avenue and Flint Ridge.

About four people bailed out and ran into an open garage, which was then pulled shut, and officers ringed the block in case the car started moving again. One man was described running southbound in a gray shirt, black shorts and a face covering toward Culver and Pioneer. A second stolen-vehicle report — another white Honda Accord, seen near Niles Road and McCurdy Drive — came in the same night.

Officers also responded to the Food Maxx on Niles Street for a man with a knife, and a negotiator opened a line with him, calling for him to put the weapon down. The man was described as white, in his late thirties, dressed in black and carrying a pocket knife. Units were moved in from Lamont to cover the area while the call was worked.

Earlier in the evening, deputies in the Frazier Park area handled a strong-arm robbery at a travel stop on Taft Highway. The rest of the night was alarms, crashes, disturbances and medical aids — the ordinary hum that keeps deputies busy between the calls that make the report.

 Today's Weather

It's currently 73 degrees at Meadows Field, on the way to a high near 97 this afternoon — a proper October scorcher, so plan your outdoor work for the morning and keep the water handy. Tonight's low settles around 66. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, and no active fires reported in the county. Air quality sits at 76 on the index, which is moderate — fine for most of us, but if you're sensitive to smoke or dust, take it easy during the hottest hours. If you're headed over the Grapevine or down the I-5 corridor, note that all lanes are impacted near Smokey Bear Road south of us due to a fire, with evacuation warnings issued in that area — worth checking conditions before you go.

 Something Good

Bakersfield is on track to record its fewest shootings and shooting deaths in years, city officials say — a milestone worth pausing on. Fewer triggers pulled and fewer families shattered is the kind of progress that doesn't always make headlines, but it changes lives block by block. Credit goes to the officers, outreach workers and neighbors doing the quiet, unglamorous work of keeping the peace. Let's hope the trend holds.
