The night's largest police response came out of south Bakersfield, where several callers reported gunfire near a court in the 900 block of Karma Court. Officers were told to broadcast for officer safety as they headed in, and once on scene they found a shell casing on the north side of the court. A witness told them two men had already left the area on foot to the north, and no victim was ever located.

It's the kind of call that ties up units for a while — a perimeter, a search, a report — and ends without an arrest. Fire ran alongside the police work all night. Crews were sent to a structure fire at a 1,500-square-foot single-story home on West Moyer Avenue in Ridgecrest, where the first engine arrived to find nothing showing and took command; a second engine was diverted from a call on Highway 14 to help, and the response drew a full assignment of engines plus a battalion chief.

In east Bakersfield, crews answered a structure fire reported behind a home in the 1000 block of Sumner Street, with two engines, a truck and a rescue assigned. Grass and lot fires kept other units moving around the county. Officers also worked a robbery at a mini mart in the 3300 block of Wible Road, where a man in a white shirt and red pants ran off on foot with cash and a metal rod; a person was found nearby shortly after.

A fight reported in front of a home in the 2700 block of Whitley Drive drew a caller's estimate of about 40 juveniles, one person with a cut above an eyebrow, and a neighbor's report of ATVs, fireworks and possible gunshots in the area. A vehicle struck a pedestrian on Fremont Street, and a foot chase through the alleys between Lincoln, Quincy and Baker streets ended with one person in custody and a perimeter held for another.

 Today's Weather

Expect a high near 90 degrees today and a low around 59 tonight. It's currently 66 degrees at Meadows Field, so the morning is comfortable before the heat builds this afternoon. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, and air quality is good — an AQI of 38, which is about as clean as the valley gets. Fire crews remain on one active fire in the county, the Cyrus Fire. If you're spending the day outside, the early hours are your friend; by mid-afternoon it will feel more like late summer than mid-October.

 Something Good

The Via Arté chalk festival turned an ordinary Bakersfield parking lot into a gallery this weekend, with local artists and neighbors working side by side to fill the pavement with color. Events like this don't happen by accident — they take organizers, sponsors and a lot of willing hands — and the result was a space that people who might never set foot in a museum could walk through and enjoy for free. If you stopped by, you saw the best version of this town: creative, unpretentious, and happy to share.
