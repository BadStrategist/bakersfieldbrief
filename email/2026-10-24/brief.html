The most serious call came at 3:55 a.m., when a drive-by shooting in the 500 block of East Belle Terrace in southeast Bakersfield left one person with major injuries. The suspect vehicle, described as a black four-door, was last seen heading northbound on South Real Road toward Stockdale Highway. Earlier, at 1:05 a.m., officers and fire crews were sent to Martin Avenue for a shooting where one person was reported hit.

Crews were held back while officers worked the scene, and an air ambulance was asked to stand by. Gunfire calls came in from several corners of the city. A caller near 10th Street and Lincoln Street counted about six shots over five minutes at 8:28 p.m., and officers went to the 7500 block of a residential street at 12:41 a.m. on a report of multiple gunshots — though a unit already in the area reported hearing none.

At 4:30 a.m., a fight at Flower Street and Alta Vista Drive in east Bakersfield grew until a caller reported roughly 30 people there with firearms and bats. Weapons calls ran the length of the night. A woman asked deputies to a parking lot at 7:31 p.m., saying her boyfriend had battered her and was armed.

A man on a scooter with a handgun was reported at a Family Dollar on Highway 58 at 8:20 p.m., and four juveniles with firearms were reported walking northbound on Airport Drive at Washington Avenue at 9:31 p.m. Deputies also went to a home on Southland Court at 12:16 a.m. for a man reported to have a large kitchen knife, and officers followed a lifted SUV on Gosford Road at 12:41 a.m. after a report of a possibly armed man before a subject ran on foot. The night's worst crash was a head-on collision on Highway 46 east of Curt Road at 1:50 a.m. that hurt three people.

One person was trapped in a vehicle and tagged red, a second was tagged yellow, and a third was also treated. Mercy Air was launched and the highway was shut down for a landing zone, but fog turned the helicopter back to base, and both lanes were left blocked. A second head-on crash, north of Sycamore Road at 11:38 p.m., left two cars off the roadway.

 Today's Weather

It's 55 degrees right now at Meadows Field, on the way to a high near 74 this afternoon and a low around 53 tonight. No active weather alerts are in effect for Kern County, and there are no active fires to report. Air quality is 56, which lands in the moderate range — fine for most folks, though anyone sensitive to particulates may want to keep an eye on it if you're planning a long stretch outdoors. In short: a good Saturday to be outside, with a light layer for the morning and the evening.

 Something Good

The new bike trail along the Friant-Kern Canal is nearing completion, and when it opens it will connect northwest Bakersfield to the Kern River trail. That's a real addition for anyone who rides, runs or just likes a long walk without traffic — a continuous recreational route linking two sides of the community. Worth watching for a ribbon-cutting soon.
