The night's only gunfire call came at 9:53 p.m., when a unit asked dispatchers to check whether there had been a ShotSpotter activation. An officer nearby reported hearing what sounded like 10 to 12 rounds — or possibly fireworks. Nothing further was confirmed from that call, and it stood as the lone gunfire report of the night.

Two minutes later, at 9:55 p.m., a caller reported that a man had been attacked with a pipe in the 700 block of Oak Street in central Bakersfield. The suspect — described as a white man in his late 20s or early 30s, wearing a black hooded sweatshirt and black pants — was still across the street at a bus stop with the pipe about 20 minutes after the call came in. Earlier in the evening, a man in his late 20s left a home on foot at about 7:10 p.m. and had not been located as of the overnight log.

He is 5-foot-7, about 130 pounds, and was last seen in a white shirt and black shorts, heading an unknown direction. He is listed at risk because of a medical condition. Officers chased someone seen on the roof of a house at 9:09 p.m., and at 1:25 a.m. two fire companies were sent to a structure fire in progress in the 4900 block of Corrine Street in south Bakersfield, with Metro units asked to respond as well.

Crews were still on that call more than 45 minutes later. Firefighters also worked a large haystack fire that started around 11:28 p.m. near Adobe Road and Brent Avenue in southeast Bakersfield. A pile of timber behind the stack was the remaining exposure, and the structure on the property was protected.

A water tender was added, crews ran a supply line from a hydrant about 200 feet away, and access had to be made through a locked gate. Elsewhere in the log: a quad and a sedan collided at 7:06 p.m., with juveniles riding the quad; one person was reported hurt and the sedan was towed. A crew was sent to the 600 block of Airport Drive at 7:09 p.m. for a person in a psychiatric crisis, with a sheriff's deputy already on scene.

Officers responded to the 1200 block of Olive Drive at 7:13 p.m. for a suspicious circumstance involving one man and three women around a pickup, and a multi-vehicle crash with major injuries was reported at 7:45 p.m.

 Today's Weather

Expect a high near 70 degrees today and a low around 50 tonight, according to the National Weather Service. It's currently 52 degrees at Meadows Field. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, and no active fires reported in the region. Air quality is running at 70 — moderate — so most folks can go about their day normally, though those who are sensitive to particulates may want to keep an eye on it if they're planning extended time outdoors. All in all, a mild late-October day across the valley.

 Something Good

Lamont marked a real milestone Monday morning with a tape-cutting ceremony on Di Giorgio Road and Rose Street, celebrating new sidewalks installed in front of 260 homes. Community leaders say the improvements give families a safe place to walk, give kids a safer spot to wait for the school bus, reduce flooding through new storm drainage, and cut down on street dust for cleaner air. Comite Progreso de Lamont, led by president Diana Mireles, led the push — and of the 13 road-related demands the group brought to the county, ten are now complete. Public Works manager Yolanda Alcantar said the sidewalk project is the latest piece of a $55 million investment in Lamont. Mireles told 23ABC that public projects take time, but with patience and collaboration, they happen.
