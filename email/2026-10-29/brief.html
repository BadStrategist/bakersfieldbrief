Read the full overnight log

The night's biggest call came at 1:55 a.m., when officers worked an apartment complex in northwest Bakersfield through the early hours. Residents of several units were brought out through a garage door at the rear of the building while an armored vehicle was moved into position out front and the entry team came in from the back. Once inside, officers found bullet holes in the doorway of an upstairs unit.

No further details were available by morning. Shots were reported twice in quick succession at 4:33 a.m., both in northwest Bakersfield. The first call described six to eight rounds in the 10800 block of Royal Ascot Avenue; the second, about 12 rounds in the 10000 block of Laurel Park Avenue.

Officers had no additional information on either report. Earlier in the evening, at 7:17 p.m., officers were sent to a mobile home park for a 15-year-old in a white shirt and black pants reported to be holding a gray handgun. At 9:08 p.m., a driver on northbound Interstate 5 north of Fort Tejon flagged down an officer and said a man had tried to force him out of his vehicle and take his keys and wallet.

The Grapevine kept crews busy late. At 9:58 p.m., a semi rear-ended another semi on northbound Interstate 5 half a mile south of the Grapevine, pinning one driver inside the tractor-trailer. Firefighters freed him, and crews asked for a hard closure of the lanes.

The log also included a 7:00 p.m. call to the 2500 block of Taft Highway for a man reported lying on the sidewalk in front of a business, a 7:09 p.m. verbal disturbance at a phone store in the 10300 block of Main Street that drew two callers, and a 7:22 p.m. welfare check on Hermosa Drive in east Bakersfield, where a man told officers his roommates were pressuring him to take hospital-prescribed medicine or move out.

 Today's Weather

Expect a high near 72 degrees today and a low around 56 tonight, according to the National Weather Service. It's currently 55 degrees at Meadows Field. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, and no active fires reported in the area. Air quality is running at 79, which lands in the moderate range — fine for most of us, though if you're sensitive to smoke or dust, it's a reasonable day to keep an eye on how you feel during longer stretches outside. Otherwise, it's a quiet, mild late-October day in the valley, the kind that makes the walk from the car to the office an easy one.

 Something Good

Shafter's Dual Immersion program is showing what bilingualism looks like in practice, with students in the community learning in two languages side by side. Programs like this one give kids a skill they'll carry for life — and they're a point of pride for a town that knows how to make things grow. If you know a student in the program, give them a high five this week.
