It was a busy night for officers and dispatchers across the city. The call that drew the most attention came at 9:26 p.m., when officers were sent to Kentucky Street and Bale in east Bakersfield for seven juveniles reported in the area, dispatch putting their age at 15. One of them was said to have a firearm and was last seen walking south toward the railroad tracks in a white jumpsuit, so officers searched the tracks and the surrounding streets for him.

Earlier in the evening, two people cut through a razor-wire fence and got into a business yard at 8:53 p.m. Units set a perimeter and brought in a police dog and an air unit, and one of the two was reported hurt getting over the wire. A hit-and-run crash at 8:34 p.m. left a person with moderate to major injuries, and the car that fled was described as a black Toyota sedan heading east.

A call about a possible unreported stolen vehicle with a firearm on the seat brought an air unit and a police dog to a business yard in the 5800 block of South Union Avenue at 7:01 p.m., where a man was found hiding under a fifth-wheel trailer and one person was taken into custody. Later, a pursuit was reported on Edison Highway at 11:40 p.m., past Sterling and then Fairfax, at speeds from 85 up to 120 mph, with an air unit launching to follow. Officers also responded to a man with a machete on a bicycle reported on Cedar Street in central Bakersfield at 11:45 p.m. and on Fillmore Street on foot a few minutes later.

Other calls through the night included a juvenile reported suicidal and holding a knife on Beardsley Avenue at 8:37 p.m., a fight between roommates involving a sword on Haven Avenue at 9:13 p.m., and a woman reported armed with a brick on Country View Lane at 3:53 a.m. Medical crews were sent to East Brundage Lane for difficulty breathing and to the Navigation Center for a maternity call, and a commercial fire alarm at a plant on Lerdo Highway was canceled.

 Today's Weather

It's 59 degrees at Meadows Field this morning, and the National Weather Service expects a high near 73 today with a low around 56 tonight — about as pleasant a late-October day as the valley gives us. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, and no active fires to report. Air quality is 91, which lands in the moderate range, so those with sensitivities may want to keep that in mind for extended time outdoors. Otherwise, it's a good day to be out and about.

 Something Good

Dagny's is celebrating 30 years of keeping Bakersfield caffeinated. Three decades at the same local coffee spot is a rare thing anywhere, and it's a milestone worth raising a mug to — generations of regulars, early shifts and long conversations have passed through those doors. Congratulations to the whole Dagny's crew.

And downtown businesses are teaming up for a donation drive to help women in need, 23ABC reports. Girls Girl Social Club and StreetFair have rallied four downtown spots — Twisted Coffee, The Mint, Jerry's Pizza and Guthrie's — to collect essentials for the Open Door Network. Twisted Coffee is offering 10 percent off to anyone who donates, and Guthrie's is gathering books, scrapbooks, crayons and art supplies to offer comfort and creativity. Coordinator Amy Luna said she wanted to create something that brings people together to help those who may be struggling.
