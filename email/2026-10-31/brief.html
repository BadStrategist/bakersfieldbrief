The night's most serious call came at 3:37 a.m., when a caller reported five shots and screaming on South Half Moon Drive in southwest Bakersfield. Deputies arrived and found a victim, and callers reported subjects fleeing the area — some by vehicle, and one on foot who was seen crossing toward an apartment complex across the street. A caller also reported blood in an alleyway, and a briefing was staged near Savory Lane and South Half Moon.

By 4:53 a.m., dispatch was working the call as a homicide. That investigation is active, and we'll have more as it develops. In east Bakersfield, officers were sent to the 3500 block of Country Club Drive at 5:43 a.m., where a man was reported to have stabbed his brother in the face with a nail and to still be trying to stab people.

He reportedly had several nails with him. Earlier, shots fired on Mohawk Street at 11:49 p.m. ended with two people detained; officers said no one else was outstanding, though a search of a nearby field and a house south of it continued. Deputies also spent about an hour, from 12:05 to 1:07 a.m., searching a backyard, shed, and overhang on Hageman Road at Ledgeview Lane for a man wanted on a served criminal protective order and reported to be hiding there.

An air unit watched the yard until the search was cleared. Four separate calls about people with firearms came in across the evening and early morning — at the Camilla Village apartments, at Valley Plaza and Castro Lane, at an apartment complex on Terrace Way, and on Quincy Street, where a neighbor was reported to have pointed a laser at the caller. A vehicle struck a pedestrian at 30th Street and Union Avenue just before midnight, and two traffic stops turned into foot chases — one at Fremont Street and 9th Avenue, another at South Fairfax Road and Oasis.

 Today's Weather

A pleasant fall Saturday is on tap. The high should reach near 70 degrees, with a low around 57 tonight — comfortable for trick-or-treaters once the sun goes down, though you'll want a jacket after dark. It's currently 62 degrees at Meadows Field. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, no active fires reported, and air quality is good at an index of 36. In short: about as nice a Halloween as the valley gives us.

 Something Good

If you know a veteran or someone on active duty, tell them about GET's November promotion — free rides all month, no fine print to decode. And if you've got a spare pack of diapers or a can of formula, the Bakersfield Pregnancy Center turns those donations into real help for parents who are nervous and doing their best. Kindness that practical is worth celebrating.
