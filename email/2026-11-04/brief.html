The night's most serious call came at 10:44 p.m., when officers were sent to a weapons report after a man was said to be holding a firearm. People could be heard screaming in the background, and the caller's line went down before dispatchers could gather more. Violence carried through the rest of the night: officers were sent to the 600 block of Casa Loma Drive in southeast Bakersfield at 11:39 p.m., where a man in his 30s was reported to have pushed his mother and then walked toward Brundage Lane.

A fight inside a home with several people involved was reported at 5:49 a.m., and a battery at a market in the 400 block of Baker Street in east Bakersfield came in at 7:29 p.m. Two searches ran alongside the violence. A 3-year-old boy was reported missing from a home on El Toro Drive in south Bakersfield at 7:45 p.m., last seen in a black Lightning McQueen shirt, green cargo pants and orange and red shoes.

A traffic stop turned into a pursuit at 10:47 p.m. and ended with two men running off on foot, and after 1 a.m. officers chased a store burglary from Niles Street to Pacific Street, where a clerk had yelled for a man to come back as he ran to a waiting car carrying a box. One person was taken into custody in the 900 block of Pacific. The two worst crashes arrived before daylight.

A car crossed from the southbound side of Interstate 5 into oncoming traffic near the Kern-Kings county line at 3:36 a.m. and hit a white Ford pickup head-on. The vehicles came to rest about 200 yards apart; of the four people inside them, three were unhurt and one suffered a serious head injury. An air ambulance was requested and then cancelled, and a ground ambulance took the patient.

At 4:52 a.m., fire crews were sent to the Norris Road on-ramp to northbound Highway 99 for a semi crash with a possible gas leak and found the rig across at least three lanes just north of the east off-ramp to Highway 58. The CHP stopped traffic while crews worked, and fire units caught in the backup reached the scene along the shoulder.

 Today's Weather

Expect a high near 68 degrees today and a low around 52 tonight, with the current observation at Meadows Field reading 54. No active weather alerts are in effect for Kern County, and air quality is good at an index of 35 — a comfortable, unremarkable valley day, the kind that makes the morning commute the only real obstacle. No active fires were reported in the county as of this writing.

 Something Good

The most positive headline this morning is that California's $11.25 billion affordable housing measure, Proposition 1, is poised to pass, according to the Associated Press. If the projection holds, the measure would direct significant state funding toward affordable housing across California — rental and supportive housing, veterans' home loans, and down payment help for families trying to buy their first home. For a valley where housing costs have climbed faster than wages for years, that is real money aimed at a real problem.
