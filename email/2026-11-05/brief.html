The small hours opened with an armed robbery on Highway 46, where deputies were sent at 12:11 a.m. to a gas station on the 21,900 block after two masked juveniles, at least one of them armed, took cash from the register and from a customer before running east on foot toward a nearby restaurant. Weapons calls carried much of the night. A driver reported a man who brandished a firearm from a white Honda at 1:55 a.m., and an officer was searching a call for suspected drugs and weapons at 9:51 p.m.

Violence kept officers moving. They were sent to the 8300 block of California Avenue at 10:10 p.m., where a man was reported to have hit his head on a wall during an argument with his father and was not moving; to Gilmore Avenue and Buck Owens Boulevard in north Bakersfield at 3:47 a.m., where a man was reported to have pepper-sprayed the caller over a payment dispute; and to an assault at an Elmwood Avenue home in east Bakersfield at 6:50 a.m. Three pursuits crossed the night — a car that fled an officer east on Pioneer Drive in east Bakersfield at 9:44 p.m. and was lost, a traffic stop on Castro Lane that became a chase onto Highway 99 at 12:25 a.m., and a wrong-way driver on Highway 99 who was spun with a PIT maneuver at 2:06 a.m. but kept creeping along.

The worst crash came in the dark, a single vehicle that rolled onto its roof on the 8,100 block of Old River Road in southwest Bakersfield at 4:52 a.m., with the driver out of the car and the patients refusing a hospital. Fire crews had two calls of their own: a fire of unknown origin in a mechanic's yard on South Union Avenue between White Lane and Pacheco Road at 9:55 p.m., and an outside fire in the alley behind Tulare Street in east Bakersfield at 3:08 a.m. Earlier in the evening, crews were sent to a person who fell and was unconscious near Au Chocolat Drive at 7:15 p.m., and the CHP was told about a pedestrian in the northbound lanes of Highway 99 just north of the airport at 7:56 p.m.

 Today's Weather

It's currently 50 degrees at Meadows Field, on the way to a high near 69 this afternoon and a low around 51 tonight. No active weather alerts are in effect for Kern County, and no active fires are burning. Air quality is 82, which lands in the moderate range — fine for most of us, though if you're sensitive to smoke or dust, it's a reasonable day to keep an eye on how you feel during longer stretches outside. Otherwise, it's a quiet, seasonable valley Thursday.

 Something Good

Sixteen men at Wasco State Prison spent months earning job certifications through CalCTRA, and Wednesday they graduated with trades in hand — facilities maintenance, data entry, industrial skills — and something harder to certify: the confidence to walk into an interview and ask for the job. One graduate, Rafiki Bogar, put it simply, saying the program helps people build belief in themselves. A former graduate who now runs his own business came back to tell them it can be done. That's a good morning's work, and a good reason to smile.
