Read the full overnight log

The night's most serious call came from the county side of 7th Standard Road, where deputies were sent at 10:43 p.m. to the area of Mayer Avenue for shots fired. A caller told dispatchers two people were shooting and trying to shoot his brother, and deputies reported no injuries so far but said the suspects might still be in the area and that a victim was hiding in a field nearby. A drone was requested, and by 11:01 p.m. deputies had begun searching for shell casings.

Deputies were back out about three hours later at the Edison Trailer Park on Edison Highway, where a 17-year-old was reported to have been hit with glass and suffered facial injuries. The man suspected in that assault left on foot before deputies arrived. Earlier in the evening, fire crews and medics were sent at 7:25 p.m. to Edison Highway westbound at Fairfax Road north, where a vehicle hit a juvenile and left one patient down in the roadway.

A domestic disturbance at 10:28 p.m. ended with the man who threatened the caller walking out before officers got there. Two burglaries in progress stood out in the log: a man in a black hooded sweatshirt caught on camera at the Dollar Tree on the 2,500 block of South 8th Street at 8:32 p.m., and a man on a bicycle seen on camera at a Toyota dealership at 4:59 a.m. Fire crews also ran a working fire behind a home on the 5,500 block of Woodmere Drive in southwest Bakersfield at 12:48 a.m., smoke reported inside a one-story apartment complex on the 1,600 block of East 11th Street in east Bakersfield at 3:58 a.m., and vehicle fires on Panama Road and Muller Road in southeast Bakersfield.

 Today's Weather

Expect a high near 80 degrees today and a low around 53 tonight, according to the National Weather Service. It's currently 55 degrees at Meadows Field. No active weather alerts are in place for Kern County, and no active fires are being reported. Air quality is 80 — moderate — so most people can be outside comfortably, though those sensitive to smoke or dust may want to keep an eye on conditions through the afternoon. A warm, quiet November Saturday is on tap across the valley.

 Something Good

23ABC is holding a Community Baby Shower on November 19, collecting donations for the Bakersfield Pregnancy Center. The center provides expecting Kern County families with free resources — pregnancy tests, ultrasounds and confidential consultations — and hosts a baby shower each quarter to supply parents in need with diapers, formula and clothing. 23ABC has partnered with the center since 2018, and donations can be dropped off at the station, 321 21st Street in downtown Bakersfield, from 5 a.m. to 7 p.m. that day.
