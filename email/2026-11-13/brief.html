The night's biggest call came at 10:49 p.m., when deputies were sent to the railroad northeast of Woodford, near Tehachapi, after agents working an interrupted burglary of a train container reported that people inside the container shot at them. Two agents were reported not to be hurt and took cover behind their vehicles. Units staged about a half mile out and worked out access from the Highway 58 side and off Woodford and Keene.

Deputies were told one suspect was believed to have fled south and then southwest, with no description available, and crews asked a hospital to confirm there were no gunshot victims. The railroad was cleared to resume traffic about 4 a.m. The only other gunfire calls of the night came at 6:05 a.m., when officers took about four reports of shots heard in the area of Morrill Avenue and Airport Drive.

Weapons calls were the other thread through the evening. Officers were sent at 7:04 p.m. to the 2,700 block of Ming Avenue for a robbery that had just happened at a clothing store, where a man in a ski mask took merchandise and left eastbound in a white sedan. Officers were sent into the 10th Street area at 8:14 p.m. for a domestic argument where the other person in the home was reported to have a firearm, and a roommate was reported to have threatened to shoot the person who called.

Deputies were sent at 7:14 p.m. to the 8,400 block of Panama Road for a report of a man with a kitchen knife; he was reported to be inside the residence and no longer holding it. Fire crews worked four structure fires through the night, starting at 7:31 p.m. on Ledgeview Lane in southwest Bakersfield. The last was an apartment on the 2,200 block of Nelson Street at 10:31 p.m.

Deputies and fire crews searched the south side for a possible drowning at 3:18 a.m., and medics were sent at 4:12 a.m. to the 900 block of Olive Drive for a 2-year-old reported not to be breathing.

 Today's Weather

Expect a high near 72 degrees and a low around 51 tonight. It's currently 61 at Meadows Field. The National Weather Service in Hanford has issued a Lake Wind Advisory running until 10 a.m. Saturday, so if you're headed out to the lake or anywhere exposed, plan for gusty conditions. Air quality is good, with an AQI of 35, and there are no active fires reported in the county this morning. A mild, clear Friday — a good one to get outside before the wind picks up.

 Something Good

Kern County has a gold medalist. A local parafencer won gold in international competition in Brazil, and has now set a goal of competing at the 2028 Paralympic Games. That's a long road from here to Los Angeles, and it starts with mornings like this one — so keep an eye on this athlete. We'd love to see a Kern County name on that stage.
