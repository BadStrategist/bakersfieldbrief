 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Large Fight at Main St block

13 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The night's heaviest response came just after 2 a.m., when officers were sent to a Mohawk Street block after a caller reported someone inside a vacant unit. The caller described a loud argument, a woman screaming for help and a man yelling, and two people were seen on a rear landing before fleeing toward a road to the north. A search of the area followed, with descriptions of both broadcast to responding units.

Minutes later, at 2:27 a.m., crews investigating a separate call requested a reinforced structure fire response and handled it as a structure fire. Earlier in the evening, around 9:20 p.m., a caller at a Bakersfield lodging property reported smoke coming from inside a room. Fire crews arrived to find a small fire in a restroom and took care of it.

Over at Hart Park, a passerby flagged down a park ranger about a motorcycle crash just beyond the east entrance; an engine was sent, but no patient was found at the scene, and the sheriff's office stayed on to handle the call. An officer working the Olive Drive overpass also called for a tow after a bicyclist was involved in a collision there, with eastbound traffic moving at the time. The rest of the log leaned on disturbances and welfare checks, with a string of domestic-related calls spread across the county.

Officers responded to reports of threats on Linden Avenue near Magnolia, a disturbance involving a woman and her ex-husband on a Meeks Avenue block, and a woman whose ex-boyfriend fled north toward the airport in an older black SUV after an incident on the Beardsley Avenue block. Deputies searched for a shirtless man in his 30s after an assault with a pole on North Lincoln Street north of Pierce Road, and looked into a threat on a Water Street apartment block. A welfare check in Bakersfield turned more serious when a family member reportedly made threatening statements and told dispatchers he had firearms and explosives.

Crews also handled an abandoned barbecue pit at a Mojave apartment complex on Barstow Road and a water heater leaking into a home on the Teal Street block.

 Today's Weather

Expect a warm one: the high should reach near 99, with a low around 70 overnight. At this hour the thermometer at Meadows Field reads 77, a comfortable start before the heat builds through the afternoon.

There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, and no fires are being tracked in the morning report. Air quality sits at 65 — moderate — so sensitive groups may want to take it easy outdoors in the heat of the day. The evening should cool off nicely for anyone heading to outdoor plans.

 Something Good

A World War II veteran in Bakersfield has reached a remarkable milestone: a 105th birthday celebration, with the community gathering to honor a lifetime of service and longevity. It's a sweet reminder that Kern County knows how to take care of its own.

There was also a warm moment this week on National Police Woman Day, when KGET 17 tipped its cap to the first groundbreaking woman to serve on the Bakersfield Police Department. Her trailblazing path opened doors for every woman who has worn the badge since — a small, worthy salute.
