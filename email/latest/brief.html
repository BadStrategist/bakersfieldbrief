 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Pursuit Ended Crash at Airport Dr and Olive Dr area

12 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Shortly after 1:30 a.m., Bakersfield Police officers with the Special Enforcement Unit tried to arrest a man wanted on a Ramey warrant near Clover Mountain Court and Pine Meadow Drive. The suspect climbed into the passenger side of a waiting car, and when the driver refused to stop, officers began a pursuit that ran south on Airport Drive, reaching speeds of 91 mph. At the intersection with Olive Drive, officers deployed spike strips.

The driver swerved, lost control, and plowed into an adult man standing on the west sidewalk. Paramedics performed CPR, but the pedestrian was pronounced dead at the scene. The two men inside the car fled on foot but were caught quickly; both were taken to a hospital for medical clearance before booking.

The California Highway Patrol is investigating the crash. Elsewhere, fire crews were busy throughout the night. A man with a shopping cart was reported lighting a string of small fires along East California Avenue, with separate blazes near Haley Street and Williams Street.

Investigators are treating the case as arson. A brush fire near Pozo Creek and the Granite area grew to 15 to 20 acres and drew a large Kern County assignment that included engines, a strike team and a type-2 helicopter; crews had to reach the flames through ranch gates and dirt roads. A report of a gunshot wound near Acres Road and Barbara Avenue sent emergency medical units to stage, with officers positioned nearby.

On northbound Interstate 5 just south of the split, a crash involving up to three vehicles left cars in the center divider and shut down the freeway while rescue crews worked. The rest of the overnight log was a mix of the usual: a vehicle-versus-bicycle collision on White Lane between Monitor and Union avenues, a domestic dispute on Chester Lane, copper theft in the Bell Terrace area where utility poles were being cut down, a street takeover of roughly 30 vehicles in a Colony Drive parking lot, a panic alarm on Adelaide Avenue, and a fire along the railroad tracks south of East Truxton Avenue. Dry conditions kept engines rolling, and no rain is in the forecast.

 Today's Weather

Expect a high near 89° under sunny skies, with a low around 65° overnight. At this hour, Meadows Field is already at 86°. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, and the air quality index is 54 — moderate, so keep that in mind if you're prone to breathing issues. No major fires are threatening the valley, and the brush fire near Pozo Creek is the one to watch. No rain is expected through the weekend, and the fire danger will stay elevated in the hills.

 Something Good

Something good to carry into the weekend: music is filling classrooms again across the Bakersfield City School District, thanks to Proposition 28 funding. The measure, passed by voters three years ago, sends an estimated $800 million to $1 billion a year to arts and music education. At Myra Noble Elementary, a second grader says music class has made it easier to talk with her teacher and classmates, and her teacher hopes students carry a passion for creativity with them. Another music teacher at Jefferson Elementary says hearing piano chords ignites listening skills right away. District officials also report a practical benefit: students in music programs have higher attendance than their non-music peers, and they're working to build that same momentum at the elementary level.
