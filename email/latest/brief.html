 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Structure fire at Mount Vernon Ave near Niles St

9 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Fire crews and deputies had a busy night across Bakersfield and the county. Just after midnight, a passerby spotted smoke coming from a building near Mount Vernon Avenue and Niles Street in east Bakersfield, in the area of a nearby discount store, and the call drew multiple engines, two trucks, and battalion chiefs to the scene. Crews knocked the fire down within about 15 minutes, completed a primary search, and stayed on site for roughly half an hour; no injuries were reported.

Around 5 a.m., a vehicle in a hotel parking lot on Marriott Drive struck a parked car and caught fire, and a caller worried that someone might still be inside the burning vehicle, so firefighters and an ambulance were dispatched. In the Hageman Road area, a grass fire smoldered with light smoke overnight, but crews said it was contained to an irrigation area with no immediate threat; they made contact with the landowner and planned to return in the morning with equipment to finish putting it out. Deputies also responded to two possible domestic incidents.

One was in the Paramount Colony area, where a caller said a driver holding a toddler had left an In-N-Out parking lot in a dark sedan; the vehicle’s direction remained unknown. Another was on White Lane before dawn, where a neighbor heard a woman screaming and a man shouting, and dispatchers kept an open line with a crying woman while officers made their way to the apartment complex. Other calls included medical aid staged near 2nd Street after an apparent assault, a welfare check on a man and woman reported sleeping in the canal area along Gosford Road, an officer response to a Brown Street home for a man with blood on his clothing who was off his medications, and a request from a stranded motorist on Interstate 5 north of Quebec who said she had been broken down on the shoulder since the previous day and wanted help getting off the freeway.

 Today's Weather

The current reading at Meadows Field is 77 degrees, and the valley is heading toward a high near 100 with an overnight low around 75. No heat advisories or other weather alerts are active for Kern County. Air quality checks in at a moderate 62 on the AQI scale, so anyone sensitive to particulate matter might want to limit time outdoors in the afternoon. No active fires are burning in the county this morning, and conditions look calm, dry, and hot.

 Something Good

Dignity Health is stepping up for flu season with free vaccines at locations throughout Kern County this September, a practical boost for families who want to stay healthy. And while the news cycle has been heavy, the nearly $7 million aerospace and defense investment heading to Eastern Kern is a bright economic sign — it means upgrades to the Mojave Air and Space Port, a new innovation hub at CSUB, and a Ridgecrest workshop for local startups, with hundreds of jobs expected. For anyone carrying a heavy weight, KGET 17 also reminds residents that the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline is available with Kern County-specific ways to seek help. Good things are brewing, and help is out there.
