 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Water Rescue at Kern River behind Kern River Golf Course

12 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The Kern River drew emergency crews late into the evening after a caller reported that a woman had gone into the water near the Kern River Golf Course and floated roughly an hour toward Lake Ming. A blue and white raft was mentioned in the call. The reporting party, who had been in the water with her, told dispatchers that both of them had gotten out and that there was no reason she would have gone back toward the river.

Crews searched the bank behind the golf course and later reported they had located her, at a camp just southeast of the golf course area. Elsewhere overnight, officers chased a man along the canal bank and Rosedale Highway corridor after he was spotted with a satchel. A caller reported that he was discarding items near a chain link fence in a clearing by a tree.

Officers recovered currency from the suspect and asked for the victim to be brought by for an in-field identification. On Interstate 5 near the county line, the California Highway Patrol searched for a Freightliner Cascadia that struck a Kenworth whose occupants were on the right-hand shoulder, then fled northbound. The victim vehicle remained on the shoulder less than half a mile north of the county line about two hours later.

Fire crews handled a shed fire in the 800 block of Exchange Street, where people at the property were trying to put it out when units arrived. A rubbish fire was also reported along the railroad tracks on Edison Highway. On 19th Street, a caller told dispatchers he had been knocked unconscious and was bleeding; an ambulance was requested to the address.

Around 9:22 p.m., a welfare check was conducted on East Cedar Street at Franklin Field, where a unit checked on a man reported to be in a 2008 Ford Escape with a rope. A vehicle-versus-pedestrian incident on Lincoln Street at Pierce was reported at 9:29 p.m., but the pedestrian was up and walking and did not appear to be injured. A traffic hazard on southbound Interstate 5 north of the Grapevine involved a white pickup with hazard lights on that was initially reported in the slow lane and later blocking the number 4 lane.

 Something Good

CSUB’s new mariachi course is a wonderful addition to the campus, giving students a chance to study and perform a tradition that runs deep in Kern County’s cultural fabric. It’s a reminder that our local university continues to find ways to celebrate the diversity of our community. Also worth a smile: Week 5 of the high school football season wrapped up last night, and KGET 17 has the scores — a great night for local athletes and the families who came out to cheer them on.
