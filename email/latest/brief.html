 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Structure Fire at 2200 block C St

20 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Calls kept emergency crews moving from last evening into the early hours, with most of the night’s traffic staying inside the city and the busiest stretch running from 10 to 11:30 p.m. At 10:43 p.m., crews were sent to a downed power pole with transformers and active power lines just north of East California Avenue and Washington Street. The caller asked that the area be blocked off so nobody would walk into the wires, and the utility — which already had a work order open on that line — was asked to respond.

The night’s fire calls began just after 11 p.m., when a full structure assignment went out for a vehicle burning next to a house in the 200 block of South King Street. Crews were told the fire was spreading to the house and then to the roof, and a caller told fire dispatchers that someone had lit the house on fire and might still have been at the scene. After a separate report that an ex-husband had tried to get into the home, dispatchers set alarms at that address to priority one for the rest of the night.

A structure assignment followed at 11:48 p.m. for the 700 block of Height Street, and two more structure fire assignments came in between midnight and 2:30 a.m. Just after 1 a.m., the sheriff’s office reported a brush fire at Magnolia Avenue and 7th Standard Road, and an engine, a patrol unit and a battalion were sent. The first unit on scene found about a quarter acre burning at a slow rate of spread, moving east and south along the road, and a second engine and another patrol unit were added to the call.

The most serious call came at 3:30 a.m., when a caller told city dispatchers that a woman had been forced into a vehicle at gunpoint and made to drive. The suspect was described as under the influence of methamphetamine, wearing a gray sweater and jogger pants, and the blue sedan was last seen heading eastbound on California Avenue. A second caller, on a different phone number, reported a woman yelling for help near South Oleander, and dispatchers could hear a female voice saying a man was attacking her.

Around the same time, a domestic violence call over a divorce situation came in from the 400 block of Cypress Street. Officers were given the suspect and vehicle descriptions and searched the area, while city officers also handled a foot pursuit that ended in custody near South H Street and a welfare check on Columbus Street.

 Today's Weather

Expect another hot one. The National Weather Service calls for a high near 99 degrees with a low around 69 tonight, and it’s currently 68 at Meadows Field (KBFL) — a comfortable start before the afternoon heat builds. No active weather alerts are in effect for Kern County. Air quality sits at 71, which is moderate, so sensitive groups may want to take it a little easier outdoors in the heat of the day. There are no active wildfire reports for the county this morning.

 Something Good

One of the sweetest traditions in Kern County is back in its familiar spot: the beloved cinnamon roll vendor has returned for the 50th Kern County Fair. For fairgoers who have visited year after year, that stand is more than a place to buy a pastry — it’s part of what makes the fair feel like home. And the volunteers from Bakersfield Search and Rescue patrolling the fair’s parking lots are a quiet reminder that neighbors look out for one another.
