 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Shooting at 10400 block area

19 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The overnight hours brought a flurry of activity across Bakersfield and Kern County, with law enforcement and fire crews responding to incidents ranging from a shooting to a missing child. Just after 1:30 a.m., units were dispatched to a shooting southeast of Bakersfield where a victim suffered a gunshot wound to the arm; dispatchers had no suspect description, and crews on scene also reported a gas leak inside the structure, prompting additional units to stage nearby. Earlier in the evening, fire crews responded to a senior living complex in the Brookside Drive area after reports of a burnt smell from vents and smoke inside one of the buildings — about 50 to 60 vehicles remained in the lot as crews investigated and asked for the main breaker to the condenser unit to be shut down.

Downtown, officers were sent to 21st Street and Chester Avenue for a strong-arm robbery where a woman reported her phone taken; the suspect briefly left in a black SUV before returning, and the argument continued as officers arrived. In the county, deputies responded to a park for a disturbance involving roughly 20 juveniles with unknown weapons, though a fire engine initially dispatched was canceled. A kidnapping report came in from the 10500 block of Meacham Road, where the caller said juveniles had gone missing again after entering through a side entrance and the suspect remained outstanding.

At a carnival area in Bakersfield, units took a report of a missing 6-year-old girl last seen near the kids' carnival wearing a pink cowboy T-shirt, and deputies were told the subject was part of a missing juvenile case from the agency. A priority welfare check in the 4500 block of Columbus Street escalated into a report of someone on the hood of a car while the driver moved backward trying to throw her off. Deputies also responded to a domestic violence report in the Park Drive area, where a caller said a 52-year-old man had kicked her in the ribs and punched her.
