 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 6am

Overnight: vehicle_fire, Pacheco Rd

25 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Overnight, Kern County saw a mix of incidents that kept law enforcement busy. The most significant was a tractor-trailer fire on Pacheco Road around 11:26 p.m. By 11:35, crews reported the tractor as a total loss, though the trailer was salvaged.

Elsewhere, shortly before midnight, a caller reported hearing a shot from inside his living room, and medical units were on scene. Around 2:48 a.m., officers responded to Ginger Oak Lane for a welfare check with medical aid requested. The reporting party was crying and unable to answer questions, with a baby heard crying in the background; a subject was later seen running through the carport toward a warehouse, and units held the perimeter.

Around 12:58 a.m., officers were sent to Potomac Avenue near Oswell Street for a large party that turned into a physical fight, with one caller reporting possible shots heard — unclear whether fireworks. And around 10:08 p.m., a man threatened to shoot the reporting party’s husband during a verbal dispute at a Bakersfield apartment complex. No weapon was seen at first, but the same subject was later seen walking through the complex with a baseball bat.

Other incidents included a collision near Panama Lane and Ashe Road requiring a tow, a verbal dispute with a possible gunshot heard, a physical fight at an apartment on H Street, and a trucker on the highway needing CHP to arrange a tow.

 Today's Weather

Today’s forecast calls for a high near 94° and a low around 69° tonight. It’s currently 93° at Meadows Field (KBFL). There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, and air quality is at a moderate 77. Skies will be mostly clear, with light winds expected throughout the day. Overnight, conditions will cool down, with lows dipping into the upper 60s. Overall, it’s shaping up to be a typical late-summer day in the valley — warm and dry, with no significant weather systems on the horizon.

 Something Good

On a brighter note, Nova Sorella’s resource fair in Bakersfield brought hope and valuable resources to the community. The event, held in honor of the founder’s son who was killed by gun violence, connected residents with services ranging from job assistance to hygiene products. One attendee, Manuel Crespo, shared that the fair helped him explore his options for work and housing, expressing gratitude for the organization’s efforts. It’s a reminder of the positive impact that local nonprofits can have, especially when they honor loved ones and give back to those in need.
