 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Burglary at Breckenridge Rd

28 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Deputies had a busy night across the county, starting with a burglary on Breckenridge Road that ended with a homeowner holding a suspect at gunpoint. According to the Sheriff's Office log, the resident called dispatchers shortly before 11 p.m. to report he had the suspect detained after an earlier break-in at the home. A second suspect ran to a black GMC and left westbound on Breckenridge Road; a neighbor told deputies the truck had turned south on Comanche Drive.

Units staged at Breckenridge and Comanche while a patrol car followed the vehicle westbound at normal speeds, and the search through the Comanche Drive foothills carried past midnight before the truck was reported heading south out of the area. Elsewhere, traffic units were sent at 9:49 p.m. after roughly 50 vehicles moving eastbound on Westside Parkway approaching Calloway Drive. Officers were asked to identify anyone involved and to check Coffee Road at the parkway as the group split, with part of it reported heading toward the Truxtun Avenue off-ramp and Highway 99.

Departments were advised to keep units free while the group was tracked through the area. Fire and medical crews answered several calls overnight. At 9:55 p.m., crews were told a crash had started as a vehicle versus a tree before the vehicle went down into a ravine and came to rest with power lines down nearby; the occupants were out of the vehicle and were reported possibly trying to reach the water, and a second vehicle was reported down at the riverbank a short time later.

At 4:28 a.m., a three-vehicle collision was reported in front of a business in the 1100 block of East Truxtun Avenue, with a dark-colored SUV involved and a possible pin-in. Engine 2 and Rescue 2 were on scene and reported the driver out of the vehicle with minor to moderate injuries. At 4:19 a.m., a woman whispering on the phone told dispatchers she was locked inside a car parked in front of a home in the 2500 block of Ledgeview Lane.

Deputies also worked a hit-and-run whose driver ran off on foot, an assault where a man showed a pistol and fled in an SUV, alarms at a District Boulevard business and a Truxtun Avenue building, and a string of medical aids. The rest of the log was disturbances, welfare checks and a handful of fights.

If you're out and about, plan for a genuinely hot October day: shade, water, and a break during the peak hours will go a long way.

 Something Good

Arvin High's Ballet Folklorico dancers have something real to celebrate this week. The program that began with five students is getting a dedicated studio nearly three times the size of its current room, complete with dressing rooms, a laundry room and proper storage for costumes and shoes. Students saw the space for the first time Monday, and senior Noemi Lopez Gonzalez summed it up best — looking at the room, she said, it's exciting to know this will be their future practice place.
