 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 6am

Overnight: vehicle_fire, Pacheco Rd

25 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The night brought a mix of incidents across Kern County. Around 11:26 p.m., crews responded to a tractor-trailer fire on Pacheco Road. By 11:35, units described the tractor as a total loss, with the trailer being salvaged.

Shortly before midnight, a caller reported hearing a shot from inside his living room, and medical units were on scene. Around 2:48 a.m., officers went to Ginger Oak Lane for a welfare check with medical aid requested. The reporting party was crying and unable to answer questions, with a baby heard crying in the background; a subject was later seen running through the carport toward a warehouse, and units held the perimeter.

Around 12:58 a.m., officers were sent to Potomac Avenue near Oswell Street for a large party that turned into a physical fight, with one caller reporting possible shots heard — unclear whether fireworks. Around 10:08 p.m., a man threatened to shoot the reporting party's husband during a verbal dispute at a Bakersfield apartment complex. No weapon was seen at first, but the same subject was later seen walking through the complex with a baseball bat.

Other incidents included a collision near Panama Lane and Ashe Road requiring a tow, a verbal dispute during which a possible gunshot was heard, a physical fight at an apartment on H Street, a trucker on the highway needing CHP to arrange a tow, an attempt to locate a 7-year-old last seen on a residence's front porch wearing a black shirt, a welfare check on a woman who told bystanders she had been thrown out of a vehicle, medical aid for an unknown complaint on South 8th Street, medical aid for a possible overdose where Narcan was administered, a search for a suspect armed with a switchblade who fled on foot north on Mount Vernon Avenue, medical aid on South Haley Street where interpreters were requested, medical aid for a seizure, and a man on a scooter reportedly armed with a knife — and possibly a gun — last seen near a 711 parking lot in the Olive Drive area.

 Today's Weather
