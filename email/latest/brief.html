 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Foot pursuit at Golden State Ave corridor

9 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

It was an active night across Kern County, with law enforcement and fire crews moving from one call to the next from the north end of Bakersfield to the far edges of the county. Officers with helicopter support chased two subjects who fled on foot across a property near Golden State Avenue in north Bakersfield, setting up a perimeter as the search stretched into the early hours. A separate foot pursuit wound through residential yards near Decatur Avenue, where officers boxed the suspect in between two fenced properties and brought the chase to an end.

In eastern Kern, crews responded to a pair of vehicles that struck power poles in the span of the night. One vehicle left the roadway and hit a pole in a vineyard off King Street, shearing it and bringing down power lines; another rolled onto its roof after crashing into a pole along Edison Highway just west of the main corridor, drawing a combined response from fire and law enforcement. Utility crews were left to sort out the damaged lines as the scenes were cleared.

Firefighters also spent the early hours on a vegetation fire burning on both sides of the railroad tracks and riverbed just east of Golden State Avenue, with crews sent in to knock it down before it could spread. In the Fremont Street area on the south end of the street, multiple units responded to a fire at an apartment complex, and fire crews were dispatched to a reported vehicle fire on Highway 99 just north of 7th Standard Road in the south county. Two other calls kept officers busy through the night, both ending without injury.

On Pacheco Road in south Bakersfield, a person reported to be armed with a knife was located in an alley area; the weapon was found and the situation was resolved peacefully. In the 18th Street area, a possible intoxicated driver fled a location after claiming to have a firearm, last seen heading southbound, with units searching the area.

 Today's Weather

It's a comfortable 72 degrees right now at Meadows Field, and the valley should climb to a high near 86 this afternoon before settling to a low around 64 tonight. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, air quality reports are not posting a reading, and no active fires are burning in the region.

One thing to keep in mind for the rest of the holiday weekend: Bakersfield Now reports that forecasters are staying "weather aware" through Labor Day as moisture from Hurricane Marie could work its way into the region. Nothing is set in stone, but it's worth keeping an eye on the sky if your Monday plans take you outside.

 Something Good

There are a couple of things worth smiling about in Kern County this weekend. The Kern Autism Network's three decades is one of them — an anniversary that started with parents refusing to accept a bleak prognosis and turned into a community standing beside other families walking the same road. The Greek Food Festival volunteers are the other: 40 years of cooking, dancing and sharing culture with the whole county, with the rice pilaf already prepared and frozen, ready to be brought back to life for the crowds. It's the kind of week where the best of the valley is on full display.
