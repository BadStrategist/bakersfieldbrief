Gunfire calls carried through the small hours. At 3:37 a.m., officers were sent to a large party after a caller said his father had been shot and reported a victim down in a backyard. Multiple callers told dispatchers they had heard gunshots.

Officers arrived and found no one in the yard. About half an hour earlier, at 3:01 a.m., callers counted roughly five gunshots in the 1800 block of Hendricks Lane in south Bakersfield. A four-door car was seen driving away afterward, and a witness reported that four or five people got out of it and went into a home at the end of the cul-de-sac.

The hours around those calls were busy with weapons reports. At 7 p.m., a caller told deputies his mother was armed with a firearm and he believed she was preparing to use it while a 2-year-old and a 4-year-old were with her; she refused to come down. At 8:16 p.m., officers were sent to a home where a man had barricaded himself in a back room with two juveniles and would not come out — he had said his 12-year-old daughter let someone into the house.

At 8:25 p.m., a man doing donuts in a Mustang was reported to have a firearm in his waistband. A fight in the 5500 block of College Avenue in east Bakersfield at 9:12 p.m. brought reports that adults had supplied juveniles with alcohol and that a man had a knife. At 9:30 p.m., a woman with a knife jumped a fence onto church property on North Chester Avenue.

At 12:27 a.m., someone locked themselves in a restroom at a hospital emergency room lobby and appeared to be in a mental health crisis. The night's most serious medical call came earlier. A crash at a park at 8:41 p.m. left at least four people hurt, two of them tagged black, and pinned a child's legs under the front of a vehicle.

Crews called three units to the scene.

 Today's Weather

It's 54 degrees at Meadows Field this morning, on the way to a high near 78 and a low around 55 tonight. No active weather alerts are in effect for Kern County, and no active fires are being reported. Air quality sits at 80 on the index — moderate — so most people can be outside without trouble, though those who are sensitive to particulates may want to keep an eye on it as the day warms. It's a good Sunday to be out and about.

 Something Good

The Latination exhibit is more than a weekend of vendors and dance performances. It's a reminder that the Arts Council of Kern takes the arts to people rather than waiting for them to come downtown — into prisons, into school districts around the county, into neighborhoods that don't always get a gallery on their block. More artists joined this year than last, and every dollar raised goes back into that work.
