Read the full overnight log

The night's most serious call came at 11:45 p.m., when officers and an ambulance were sent to the 4000 block of Rosedale Highway for a shooting with two victims. Units moved in on emergency traffic, and the suspect vehicle was described as a black four-door Mercedes. No further details were available by morning.

Gunfire reports kept dispatchers busy across the city. Callers in the 12200 block of Margalo Avenue reported three to four loud explosions and three gunshots at 7:53 p.m., followed by four shots and a vehicle driving east at 8:19 p.m. Three to four shots were reported near Del Pino and Belle Terrace at 8:27 p.m., and a possible shooting toward a person came in at 10:38 p.m.

Just after midnight, two men were reported setting off fireworks at each other in the middle of a street. A business in the 3700 block of Ming Avenue was robbed at 8:47 p.m. by a suspect reported to have a knife, who fled east in a gray van. At 4:16 a.m., a man walked into a store in the 300 block of South Martin Luther King Boulevard covered in blood and asking for help; officers responded to what was logged as a possible assault.

Kern County Fire crews and an ambulance were sent to a structure fire at 2:05 a.m., and crews were called to the 12000 block of Lamberton Street in northwest Bakersfield at 5:49 a.m., where a woman was reported dead. Weapons calls came in through the evening and early morning — a man with a firearm in a red pickup who left before officers arrived, a man in a gray sedan reportedly armed with two juveniles in the car near Geraldine Street and South Union Avenue, a caller who said his wife was being threatened by a woman with a gun, a resident reportedly telling another woman to shoot her, and a call at a Rio Morada apartment. Wrong-way drivers were reported twice: southbound in the northbound lanes at 2:24 a.m., and westbound in the eastbound lanes of Highway 58 at Fairfax Road at 3:59 a.m., where dispatch took multiple 911 calls.

 Today's Weather

It's 57 degrees at Meadows Field this morning, and we're headed to a high near 62 with a low around 49 tonight — a genuinely pleasant fall Sunday, the kind that makes the valley feel like it's finally exhaling. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, and no active fires reported. Air quality is 26, which registers as good, so it's a fine day to be outside, take the dog a little farther than usual, or get the yard work done before the light goes.

 Something Good

Golden Empire Transit is offering free rides to veterans and active-duty military members throughout November. The promotion gives local service members and veterans no-cost access to public transit across Bakersfield and Kern County — a small, practical thank-you that can make a real difference for folks getting to appointments, work, or family. If you or someone you know qualifies, it's worth taking advantage of all month long.
