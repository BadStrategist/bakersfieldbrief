 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Loud Explosion Report at La Cresta Ave area

8 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The last night of the holiday weekend was a mostly quiet one for dispatchers, though firework complaints kept crews checking neighborhoods into the early hours. The most dramatic call came shortly before 10 p.m., when residents near La Cresta Avenue reported a loud blast strong enough to shake doors open. Multiple neighbors phoned in the same boom, and responders treated it as a firework report rather than something more serious.

Two calls drew the biggest responses of the night. In the early hours, deputies were sent to a rural property near 230th Street and Gaskell Avenue after three people in dark clothing were reported cutting through a chain-link fence toward heavy equipment and storage sheds. The group was described as having arrived in a pickup and an SUV pulling flatbed trailers.

In northwest Bakersfield, a property owner reported that a white 2018 Freightliner semi truck had been stolen, telling officers a firearm was inside the cab when it was taken. Just before midnight, a two-vehicle collision left both cars off the roadway; no injuries were reported, and one driver was taken into custody on suspicion of driving under the influence. The rest of the overnight log was routine: a vehicle struck a horse along Wheeler Ridge Road and left the scene, a man in dark clothing was reported in the median on Union Avenue yelling threats while holding a piece of sheet metal, a masked person was caught on surveillance near North Chester Avenue, and officers checked on a woman near Jefferson Park after a friend reported hearing impact sounds during a call.

 Today's Weather

Forecasters expect a high near 93 today and a low around 69 tonight. The current reading at Meadows Field is 70 degrees, so it will warm up quickly but stay comfortable by September standards. No active weather alerts are posted for Kern County.

Air quality is good, with the index at 48. No active fire information was listed in this morning's briefing, leaving the valley with a clear, calm start to the week.

 Something Good

One bright spot this morning comes from the boxing gym. 23ABC has the story of Jayd Soto, an 8-year-old Bakersfield boxer who is already 6-0 with three titles, including the Junior Olympics and Junior Golden Gloves. He trains five days a week at Warmup Athletics under professional boxer Joel Iriarte, who says Jayd picks up technique quickly — especially the "step back two" that sets up a standing eight count.

And for anyone looking ahead to the next community gathering, volunteers are already preparing for the Greek Food Festival, which will feature new dishes and a chance to win a trip to Greece. It's a joyful reason for the valley to come together, and more details should be coming soon.
