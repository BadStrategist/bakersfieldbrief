What happened overnight: Structure Fire at Silver Saddle Ranch & Club

30 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Firefighters spent the small hours at the old Silver Saddle Ranch & Club in California City, where a second-alarm fire broke out just after 12:30 a.m. at the two-story hotel resort. Crews arrived to find three buildings fully involved, an exposure threatened to the east, and four spot fires in vegetation to the south backing into the wind. The only hydrant on scene was dead, so a water tender was called in along with support from Edwards.

Firefighters kept the flames out of the main exposure and turned their attention to a smaller on-site building, while roughly an acre to an acre and a half of grass and brush burned around the complex. The initial fire buildings were reported 100 percent involved, and crews settled in for an extensive mop-up as light southwest winds turned erratic on one side of the fire. Elsewhere, a semi caught fire on Interstate 5 near Copus Road, south of the Grapevine, with the trailer still attached.

Crews reported the fire near the wheel wells before the whole trailer went up in heavy smoke, blocking lanes and routing traffic off the freeway onto Highway 99 while heavy towing was called in. One lane was expected to reopen within about ten minutes, and the ambulance was cancelled with no one hurt. In south Bakersfield, a hysterical caller reported that two men and a woman forced entry through the front door of an apartment in the 7000 block of Di Giorgio Road.

Officers searched the unit and the surrounding area while medics staged nearby. Deputies also were sent to the North Edwards area for a man in a black shirt and gray sweatpants standing in the middle of a roadway with a large firearm, possibly a rifle or shotgun; the caller, who was picking up juveniles from a school event, told dispatchers she left with them and filed a report. And a caller reported a man in an orange Chevrolet pickup dragging a woman from a residence in the 1800 block of Pacific Street, with the woman heard yelling as she was put into the truck before the vehicle left the area.

The rest of the night was crashes, disturbances, alarms and medical aids.

 Today's Weather

It's 79 degrees right now at Meadows Field, on the way to a high near 100 and a low around 69 tonight. No active weather alerts are in effect for Kern County, and there are no active fires reported. Air quality sits at 83, which is moderate — fine for most of us, but if you're sensitive to smoke or dust, it's a reasonable day to keep the strenuous outdoor work to the cooler hours. With the valley running this hot in October, keep the water handy and check on neighbors who work outside.

 Something Good

The Kern County Veterans Stand Down returns for its annual event at Stramler Park, offering support and services to local veterans. It's one of those gatherings where the paperwork, the health checks and the conversation all happen in one place, and volunteers show up year after year to make it work. If you know a veteran who could use a hand, this is a good week to pass the word along.
