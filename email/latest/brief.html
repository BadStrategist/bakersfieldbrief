 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 6am

Overnight: vegetation_fire, south Hwy 46 near Wildwood Rd

5 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The night's most significant call came around 4:30 a.m. when a grass fire broke out in a field near a small trailer community just east of Wildwood Road, reachable off Highway 46. Crews reported three to four acres burning with roads on three sides of the fire; Battalion 3 was on scene and asked for additional wildland units as Engine 931 worked its way in from the south. Before that, just after 3:30 a.m., a vehicle rolled over in the median on a northbound highway just south of Twisselman Road, with reports of power lines down across the lane.

Units were en route to that incident as well. Earlier in the night, around 1:55 a.m., an outside fire was reported on the 100 block of Sumner Street near the railroad tracks, and engine companies were dispatched. About 2:10 a.m., someone was reported coming into a campground wearing a gray t-shirt, gray pants and no shoes; units were checking the area.

Hot, dry late-August conditions kept fire crews on the move overnight: the three-to-four-acre grass fire south of Highway 46 near Wildwood Road drew a battalion-level wildland response, and an outside fire was reported on Sumner Street near the railroad tracks. The Twisselman Road rollover with downed power lines prompted a quick highway response, and overnight temperatures stayed warm across the valley.
