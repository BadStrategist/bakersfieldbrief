 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Burglary In Progress at Mohawk St block

16 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Fire and police crews were called to a Denny’s on Buck Owens Boulevard shortly before 4 a.m. after a person was reported not breathing in an apparent fentanyl overdose. The caller was moved over to fire crews for CPR instructions while an ambulance was dispatched, and units worked through the early-morning call as traffic began to stir along the boulevard. It’s a sharp reminder that the opioid crisis is still very much present in Kern County, showing up in the earliest hours of the day.

Just after midnight, a vehicle collided with a wall in the 5500 block of Silver Crossing Street, drawing a full fire assignment alongside Bakersfield police; both occupants were already out of the vehicle when crews arrived. Firefighters also handled two brush responses overnight — one off Gephardt Road, reached by a dirt road that passes abandoned structures, where command kept a visual on the flames from a distance, and another in the 7300 block of District Boulevard next to Dreyer’s Ice Cream that brought in county resources. Neither fire was reported to have threatened structures.

Officers had a busy night on the crime side as well. They responded to a report of a person armed with a knife on the Goodman Street block and moved to make an arrest. In an alley off North Chester Avenue near Washington Avenue, a report of two men beating a victim on the ground, one holding an unknown object, drew a quick response.

Deputies also handled a burglary in progress at a White Lane home, where a caller reported someone entering through the roof and described a second subject in a ski mask; crews requested a ladder and sought a K9, though one wasn’t available. Around the county, a medical call sent crews to the Miracle Hot Springs area after a person went down while diving, and firefighters were sent to Brentwood Drive for a person reported possibly unconscious. A box truck carrying about 20,000 pounds was stopped in the slow lane on northbound Interstate 5 near the Grapevine with mechanical issues, and a tow was arranged.

Officers also flagged an area near East 19th and Baker streets as potentially connected to a homicide investigation and moved in to check the scene, and a dispute on Beardsley Avenue involved a reported attempt to take someone’s vehicle.

 Today's Weather

 Something Good

The Kern County Sheriff’s Office and SAL are teaming up for a pair of candy drives to support a Halloween event for local families. The drives are meant to make sure children and the community can take part in the seasonal celebration — a small kindness that gives everyone a reason to smile as the spooky season approaches. Keep an eye on the Sheriff’s Office social pages for drop-off details.
