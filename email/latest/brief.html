 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Structure Fire at Pond Rd and Minner Ave area

25 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The night's biggest call came at 9:27 p.m., when fire crews were sent to a report of a house fire near Pond Road and Minner Avenue. A battalion chief arriving on scene found multiple structures involved and power lines down across the property, and crews shifted into a defensive mode, with water tenders shuttling in to supply the fight. Before they could work the property, crews asked the utility to cut the downed lines.

Later in the evening, they reported the resident had been contacted and gave a verbal all clear for the property. No injuries were reported in the initial dispatch. Earlier in the evening, at 11:29 p.m., deputies were sent to a mobile home park after a resident who uses a wheelchair reported that two men took $200 in cash at gunpoint roughly fifteen minutes earlier and then ran off on foot.

Around 12:32 a.m., officers were sent to an apartment on the 400 block of White Lane after a resident who lives alone reported that someone had come in through her front door, that she heard an item fall inside her kitchen, and that she was hiding in her closet. Units checked the apartments around the call and did not find anyone. In the early morning hours, at 2:20 a.m., a semi pulling a dry van trailer and a day cab truck pulling a trash trailer were involved in a crash on southbound Interstate 5 north of Gorman.

One truck was reported to have right-side damage, and the other driver pulled over on the wide shoulder just south of Gorman. Then, at 2:57 a.m., a ShotSpotter activation reported eight rounds fired in the 1700 block of Niles Street, and officers were sent to check the area. The rest of the log was a mix of medical aids, alarms and traffic checks.

At 9:13 p.m., deputies were sent to a store on 20 Mule Team Road in Boron, where a man had facial injuries and could not stand; crews started medical aid. At 9:18 p.m., callers reported people in a white SUV inhaling from balloons in an apartment complex parking lot on the 2300 block of South K Street. At 9:35 p.m., a woman on Plymouth Avenue reported hiding from people who live at a home and said a man in his 20s was in the back alley.

At 9:58 p.m., a car was reported stolen minutes earlier from the 2300 block of Niles Street, and the description went out to units. And at 10:11 p.m., a caller reported a white SUV running recklessly on westbound Highway 58 near Oswell Street.

 Today's Weather

Expect a high near 100 degrees today and a low around 71 tonight. It's currently 77 degrees at Meadows Field, which is a comfortable start before the heat settles in this afternoon. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, and air quality and fire information were not available at the time of this report. If you're headed out, plan around the heat — morning is the better window for yard work, errands, or anything that keeps you in the sun.

 Something Good

A good note for Condors fans: Seth Griffith is back for a seventh season in Bakersfield, still chasing the Calder Cup. His continued commitment to the local team gives the city another season of professional hockey to look forward to — and a captain who clearly cares about the players coming up behind him. Heading into a season that starts with four road games, that kind of steady leadership is worth a smile.
