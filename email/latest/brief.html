 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 6am

Overnight: fire, East Brundage Ln near Hwy 99

7 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Overnight activity across Kern County was moderate, with a grass fire, a possible overdose, a domestic disturbance, a rooftop welfare check and several medical calls keeping responders busy. Around 10:55 p.m., fire crews and a battalion chief were sent to the area of the Hampton Inn on East Brundage Lane near the freeway for a reported grass fire. Shortly after 4 a.m., medical crews were called to the front of Brookside on Coffee Road, where a person outside at the tables was unconscious but breathing in what was reported as a possible overdose.

Just after 11 p.m., officers responded to an apartment on Lincoln Street for a domestic disturbance in which a woman reported being prevented from leaving. About 11:30 p.m., deputies checked on a Grand Prairie Drive home after the resident reported their alarm sounding and said someone was on the roof. Overnight calls also included a diabetic emergency on West Mariposa Avenue and a check on a vehicle passenger near Wofford Heights.
