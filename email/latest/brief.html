 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 6am

What happened overnight: Pursuit at Madison St corridor

16 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

The night was busier than the quiet weather suggested, with the Kern County Sheriff's Office and Bakersfield police handling a string of incidents that stretched from the west side of the city out toward the high desert. The most significant call came just before 9:30 p.m., when a driver refused to yield and led officers northbound along Madison Street. The pursuit stretched past 10 o'clock, with speeds reaching roughly 55 mph on roadways that were otherwise empty of traffic and pedestrians.

Units picked up the chase after the initial stop was refused, and there was a brief concern over a possible gunshot before the pursuit continued north. Around 2:55 a.m., deputies and medical units responded to a home near Bluestone Riverway for a domestic assault with a weapon. A man was accused of attacking his girlfriend with scissors and throwing a vase at her hand during a dispute, leaving minor injuries.

Juveniles were present at the residence, and one reportedly had a nosebleed; a caller disputed a claim that one of the juveniles was pregnant. Just after 12:26 a.m., reports of smoke coming from a building near Edwards Air Force Base pulled a large fire assignment — multiple engines and a battalion chief. Crews arrived to find only light residual smoke reaching into the attic, cancelled the reinforcements, and scaled the response back to a regular structure fire.

Minutes later, around 12:37 a.m., an employee watching live surveillance footage saw two hooded males force their way into a business on 15th Street West and begin taking items. The employee then lost connection to the cameras — possibly because the suspects had removed them — and units began clearing the area. Earlier in the evening, a domestic call on East 21st Street turned urgent around 11:40 p.m. when a caller could be heard yelling in the background before the suspect began chasing her.

Officers responded to the area, where the caller reported being struck near 21st and D Street. The overnight log also included a possible DUI involving a white service truck swerving after exiting the freeway around 9:20 p.m., an outside fire on E Street at 9:53 p.m., a welfare check on St. Mary's Street involving a juvenile who said an older brother had choked him, and a 2:13 a.m. call from a woman who said a man had tried to strangle her during a verbal dispute.
