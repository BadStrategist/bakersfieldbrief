 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Assault With A Weapon at Flower St at Lincoln St

12 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Sunday evening into early Monday brought a mix of urgent calls and routine checks across Kern County, with deputies and fire crews responding to an assault, a welfare check tracked by phone ping, and a rollover on Interstate 5 that vanished before help arrived. Just after 11 p.m., a caller reported an assault with a baseball bat at the intersection of Flower Street and Lincoln Street on the east side, and officers were given a suspect description as they headed to the scene. Late Sunday, a woman told dispatchers her husband had said he planned to kill himself, and deputies were sent to an area east of Bakersfield after locating the man through a ping on his phone; units were still checking the area as the night went on.

A rescue, engine, truck and battalion assignment rolled out with CHP for a reported rollover on southbound Interstate 5 south of Bakersfield, but the first crew on scene found the vehicle had already left, and the rest of the response was canceled. Fire crews also responded to a gray SUV burning on Mount Vernon Avenue. Earlier in the evening, at 9:33 p.m., units checked a vegetation fire near the Grapevine on Interstate 5 and found nothing showing on the southbound side, canceling the response.

A gas leak at a home in Kern County at 9:57 p.m. sent crews to a report of gas coming from inside, possibly from the stove, with the homeowner saying they couldn’t turn it off; the response was scaled back to a single engine. At 10:04 p.m., a unit cleared debris from Wilson Road in Bakersfield. Los Angeles County dispatchers asked Kern units to help with a grass fire at Highway 138 and Highway 14 at 10:11 p.m., and an engine and water tender were sent.

A disturbance at an apartment on the 300 block of Chester Lane in Bakersfield drew a unit at 10:51 p.m. Deputies and an ambulance were sent to the 2100 block of Coventry Drive at 11:02 p.m. for a welfare check. At 11:06 p.m., three vehicles pulling jet skis were reported parked in the westbound lanes of Highway 178 at Alfred Harrell Highway, creating a traffic hazard.

Officers also assisted at a home on the 800 block of 8th Street at 11:52 p.m. The busiest stretch on the fire channels came between 9 and 11 p.m., with crews juggling the gas leak, the Grapevine vegetation check and the mutual aid request from Los Angeles County.
