Read the full overnight log

The biggest call of the night came on the southbound Highway 204 connector onto Highway 99, where a single vehicle rolled over. Fire crews reported four patients, all tagged green for minor injuries, and called for a second ambulance to the scene. One engine was canceled once things were under control.

No names or exact locations beyond the connector were released. Elsewhere in the county, a vehicle hit the front of the mountain and rolled over on northbound Highway 155 in the Kern River canyon, about a mile south of Hungry Gulch. Radio traffic described poor cell phone reception with the patient, and the wrecked vehicle was reported blocking the roadway.

Multiple callers reported brush burning along eastbound Highway 58 near South Union Avenue, drawing county engines and city equipment to the grass fire. In east Bakersfield, firefighters found light smoke at a house fire on North Kern Street. Crews were also sent to a structure fire response at a cold storage building in the 5500 block of Valpredo Avenue, where Truck 54 reported water flowing from one room and nothing from another; crews laddered the building looking for a heat source and got inside without finding anything visible.

In north Bakersfield, officers were sent to Beardsley Avenue for a disturbance involving a man on a motorized bike who was reported to be carrying a machete. He was last seen fleeing eastbound with a checkered backpack and was not located. A caller also reported impact sounds at a building on Westwind Drive in central Bakersfield, followed by a sedan leaving the area.

The rest of the log ran the usual wide range: a large debris pile burning in Wasco around 7:32 p.m., with crews calling for one additional engine and reporting power lines nearby that did not appear affected; a driver who went off the road after hitting the mountain in the canyon; a car window broken by an object thrown from a semi on Highway 166; a stolen pickup out of Los Angeles; and medical aids scattered across the county.

 Today's Weather

It's 52 degrees at Meadows Field this morning, on the way to a high near 73 and a low around 50 tonight. No active weather alerts are in effect for Kern County. Air quality sits at 57 — moderate — so most folks can go about their day normally, though anyone sensitive to air quality may want to keep an eye on it. Fire crews are on one active fire in Kern, the Cyrus Fire. Otherwise, it's a quiet, comfortable October day in the valley.
