The night's most serious call came early. At 1:24 a.m., officers were sent to Chester Lane on a report of an assault with a baseball bat, where a 40-year-old man was listed as a possible victim. About half an hour later, officers asked a local hospital to check whether anyone had come in with injuries from an assault with a deadly weapon — a routine step when the details of a fight are still coming together.

The biggest response of the night belonged to South Union Avenue. Just after 11:30 p.m., a caller reported hearing a gunshot and seeing three people fighting across the street, one of them in a red shirt and possibly armed. Officers set a perimeter around South Union and Serrano Drive, posting units on Acres, Millwood Court, Birchwood Court and Juniper while the air unit circled overhead.

They held the block, confirmed an offense, and then searched the alley behind the fire station for shell casings. Two weapons calls came in hours apart. At 10 p.m., officers were sent after a man who told a caller he had a firearm in his pocket and had arrived in a gray SUV.

Then at 1:07 a.m., deputies went to the 8200 block of Kimber Avenue in southeast Bakersfield over reports of people with a small black handgun; a newer blue Subaru SUV left the area, and no firearm was recovered. Before dawn, at 5:25 a.m., two callers reported a man trying to force a woman into the back seat of a car in an alley behind a residence. There was no description of the vehicle, and officers were sent back to the block.

The night's heaviest fire call came at 8:57 p.m., when crews found heavy smoke pouring from a roughly 1,500-square-foot building in the 2000 block of Lake Street in east Bakersfield. The fire was reinforced and worked as a high-priority search, and one engine stayed on scene with smoke still in the attic.

 Today's Weather

It's 55 degrees right now at Meadows Field, on the way to a high near 75 this afternoon and a low around 52 tonight — about as pleasant an October Monday as the valley gives us. No active weather alerts are in effect for Kern County, and there are no active fires to track. Air quality sits at 69, which the index rates as moderate; if you're sensitive to particulates, it's a reasonable day to keep an eye on how you feel during longer stretches outside. Otherwise, expect a calm, dry day with plenty of sun.

 Something Good

Hundreds of families turned out Sunday for Clinica Sierra Vista's free Trunk-or-Treat in southwest Bakersfield, with a line of costumed kids stretching around the parking lot. Grandmother Jamie Lujan told 23ABC the best part was bringing the whole family without worrying about the cost. Community organizations were on hand too, connecting parents with services across Kern County — and the host was marking its 55th anniversary while handing out candy.
