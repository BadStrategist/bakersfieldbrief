The loudest call of the night came at 1:09 a.m., when multiple callers reported gunshots near El Encanto Court in central Bakersfield. Officers responded to the area; no further details were available by morning. Weapons calls ran on either side of midnight.

At 7:26 p.m., officers were sent to Rench Road in north Bakersfield, where a teenager was reported to be holding a knife in the living room of a home while his parents were present. It wasn't clear whether he had brandished it or made any threats. Just after midnight, at 12:02 a.m., deputies headed to the 11,500 block of Old River Road in southwest Bakersfield, where a person was reported to be brandishing a knife at a group gathered around a gold pickup, and one person reported an assault.

Domestic violence calls carried the early morning hours. At 4:56 a.m., a woman told dispatchers she was being abused by her child's father, then disconnected; an infant could be heard crying in the background. Deputies were sent at 5:53 a.m. to a disturbance where a man was reported to have pinned his mother against a wall during an argument involving a juvenile.

A court order was already in place in that case. Two foot chases ended in arrests. At 10:19 p.m., officers chased a man who ran from a suspicious person call at a storage yard on the 4,000 block of Chester Avenue, reaching toward his waistband as he ran.

He climbed onto a roof on the 3,300 block of Anderson Street, and with Air 1 overhead and a K-9 in place, he came down and was taken into custody, with resisting charges added. A second chase at 11:40 p.m. ended on Sky Ranch, where a driver and a passenger were arrested at a residence on Suncrest Drive; one person was still outstanding. Fire crews stayed busy too.

A vegetation fire of about three and a half acres was worked off Washington Street at 7:22 p.m., and crews responded to a fire on westbound Highway 58 just west of the Mount Vernon Avenue exit at 9:06 p.m., where a second ambulance was requested.

 Today's Weather

It's 54 degrees at Meadows Field this morning, on the way to a high near 84 and a low around 54 tonight. The National Weather Service in Hanford has a Lake Wind Advisory in effect until 10 a.m. Saturday, so expect gusty conditions around the lake and open stretches of the valley. Air quality is 111 — unhealthy for sensitive groups — so folks with heart or lung conditions may want to keep outdoor time short this afternoon. No active fires were reported as of this morning.

 Something Good

Bakersfield marked Veterans Day with its 107th annual parade, a tradition that once again brought neighbors out along the route to honor local service members. The American Legion also hosted a free breakfast and lunch for veterans in Bakersfield as part of the day's observances — a simple, generous way to say thank you to the people who served. If you know a veteran who couldn't make it out, a call this week still counts for plenty.
