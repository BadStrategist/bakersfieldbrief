The night's most serious call came from the county side of 7th Standard Road, where deputies were sent at 10:43 p.m. to the area of 7th Standard Road and Mayer Avenue for shots fired. A caller told dispatchers two people were shooting and trying to shoot his brother. Deputies reported no injuries so far, said the suspects might still be in the area, and noted that a victim was hiding in a field nearby.

They asked for a drone and, at 11:01 p.m., began searching for shell casings. Deputies were back out about three hours later at the Edison Trailer Park on Edison Highway, where a 17-year-old was reported to have been hit with glass and had facial injuries. The man suspected of the assault left on foot.

Earlier in the evening, just before 7:30 p.m., a vehicle hit a juvenile on Edison Highway westbound at Fairfax Road north, leaving one patient down in the roadway, and fire crews and medics responded. A separate domestic disturbance at 10:28 p.m. ended with the man who threatened the caller walking out before officers arrived. Two burglaries in progress stood out in the log: a man in a black hooded sweatshirt seen on camera at the Dollar Tree on the 2,500 block of South 8th Street at 8:32 p.m., and a man on a bicycle seen on camera at a Toyota dealership at 4:59 a.m.

Fire crews ran a working fire behind a home on the 5500 block of Woodmere Drive in southwest Bakersfield at 12:48 a.m., smoke reported inside a one-story apartment complex on the 1,600 block of East 11th Street in east Bakersfield at 3:58 a.m., and vehicle fires on Panama Road and Muller Road in southeast Bakersfield.

 Today's Weather

Expect a high near 75 degrees today and a low around 51 tonight. It's currently 66 degrees at Meadows Field, according to the National Weather Service. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, and no active fires reported. Air quality is 82, which lands in the moderate range — fine for most of us, but if you're sensitive to smoke or dust, it's worth keeping an eye on it as the day warms up. All in all, a good Sunday to be outside.

 Something Good

Fifty years after hoisting the Junior Rose Bowl trophy, the men who wore those Renegade uniforms got to walk back into Memorial Stadium on Saturday night and watch the current team win 40-21. The college marked both the 50th anniversary of that 1976 title and its 100th football season in one evening — a rare chance to see a program's whole history standing on the same field. The Renegades close the regular season 6-4 and bowl-bound for the first time since 2024.
