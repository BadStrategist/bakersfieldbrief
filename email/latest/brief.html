 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Vegetation Fire at Westside Canal area off Hwy 58

19 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Overnight crews in Bakersfield had a full plate, and the biggest call came just after midnight. Multiple callers reported a garage fire on the Planz Road block, attached to a house and already burning into the home. The full response rolled in — two trucks, an engine and two battalion chiefs — and crews got the flames out, but the address wasn't done yet: a vehicle was reported on fire there about 40 minutes later, and fire debris was cleared from the area before dawn.

Around 1 a.m., officers headed south of the city near the river on reports of shots fired. A second caller said the situation was still active and that someone outside was carrying a firearm. One person was wounded in the leg, and a tourniquet was applied at 1:04 a.m.

Earlier in the evening, deputies and search-and-rescue units were sent to Westdale Park around 10 p.m. after a caller reported a woman in the water near the California Aqueduct; crews made a long approach on foot across the park toward the water. Minutes later, fire crews responded to a home on the Meacham Road block where residents reported a major gas leak inside the house. And late in the night, officers searching east Bakersfield railroad tracks for a missing person at risk closed in on foot, asking whether train traffic needed to be stopped; the tracks were placed under manual control while they worked the area.

The rest of the log had its quieter moments: a small fire behind a Kern County ranch property around 9 p.m. that crews found dying down and cleared with nothing more; a weapons call behind a convenience store on the South Union Avenue block at 11:06 p.m.; a 12:45 a.m. standoff near Center and Prospect Streets, where a teenager barricaded himself in a bedroom after assaulting his parents and the call came in from a group home line; a Boron 911 hang-up around midnight in which the caller rambled about a man trying to open a door and nobody answered the callback; a disturbance on the Stockdale Highway block at 1:13 a.m. involving a woman heard yelling in a bank parking lot; and a robbery report on the Pacific Street block at 1:38 a.m.

 Today's Weather

It's 70 degrees at Meadows Field this morning, with today's high expected near 89 and an overnight low around 62. There are no active weather alerts for Kern County, and the air quality reading sits at 47 — squarely in the "good" range and a welcome change from hazy summer days. No active fires are listed in this morning's conditions report, so expect a textbook September valley day: sunny and warm through the afternoon, cooling off comfortably after sundown. It's a good day to get outside.

 Something Good

There's a new Goodwill in the northwest, and the community turned out for it. The Bakersfield Chamber of Commerce joined Goodwill to cut the ribbon on the new Rosedale store — a grand opening that gives the neighborhood a welcome new retail destination and a bright spot on the local retail landscape. It's a small thing, but a new storefront opening its doors is always a good sign in this valley.
