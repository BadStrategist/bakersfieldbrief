Read the full overnight log

Two or three semi tractors burned at a trucking school on South Union Avenue at Pacheco Road, the biggest call of the night for Bakersfield firefighters, who worked on tank water because the yard had no hydrants and asked for an arson investigator to respond. Police earlier broadcast a shooting call in the 2700 block of White Lane and put out a description of a green Chevrolet Tahoe that left the area westbound. A person on the Highway 58 overpass at South Union Avenue drew a joint police, CHP and fire response that stopped the westbound lanes.

Officers worked a domestic violence call in the 1200 block of Texas Street, in east Bakersfield, where a man fled on foot, and a report of a person with a firearm sent a code 3 response to Gosford Road and White Lane, in southwest Bakersfield. The rest of the night ran from a woman pushing a shopping cart into cars at an Albertsons on Roseman Boulevard and an 86-year-old woman in crisis on Robert Street to a two-vehicle crash on Interstate 5 north of Highway 223, a fence cut at a Brundage Lane recycling business, and small fires from Beale Park to Union Avenue and Wible Road.

Semi tractors burn at a trucking school on South Union Avenue: Multiple callers reported a large fire at an industrial yard on South Union Avenue at Pacheco Road, and crews arrived to find two or three semi tractors burning at what radio traffic described as a trucking school, with the building uninvolved. There were no hydrants inside the yard, so engines ran on tank water, and the third tractor was protected as an exposure while firefighters knocked the fire down.

An arson investigator was requested to the scene.

Shooting call in the 2700 block of White Lane: Police broadcast a shooting call at a home in the 2700 block of White Lane and put out a description of a green Chevrolet Tahoe that left westbound on White Lane. The vehicle had been gone about 18 minutes when the call went out.

Person on the Highway 58 overpass at South Union Avenue: A person on the overpass brought police, CHP and fire crews to the scene. CHP stopped the westbound lanes of Highway 58 while firefighters stayed in contact with the person and requested a rescue cushion, and the person was brought down.

Domestic violence call in the 1200 block of Texas Street, in east Bakersfield: Officers were sent to a domestic violence call in which a man was reported bleeding and a woman was reported to be in danger.

The man fled on foot, described as shirtless and in pajama pants.

Person with a firearm reported at Gosford Road and White Lane, in southwest Bakersfield: Officers went code 3 to Gosford Road and White Lane for a report of a person with a firearm, an incident that had happened about 10 minutes earlier. Fire crews carried a share of the night, from the tractor fire on South Union Avenue and a grass fire off the bike trail near River Run Boulevard to small outside fires behind a building on Union Avenue, in the 3800 block of Wible Road, at the end of a cul-de-sac, and near Beale Park, plus a field reported burning north of Highway 178. Highway calls ran from the two-vehicle crash on Interstate 5 north of Highway 223 and a wrong-way driver reported at 24th Street and Oak Street to a semi with double trailers cutting off traffic on northbound Interstate 5 and a reckless driver broadcast southbound on Highway 99.

Officers and deputies also worked a man with a knife on Niles Street, a prowler report on Lotus Lane, a fence cut at a Brundage Lane recycling business, and several welfare checks. Details remain preliminary until they are confirmed by an official source.
