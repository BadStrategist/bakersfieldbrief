Read the full overnight log

The night began with a standoff in Wasco, where police asked for a K9 unit at 8:49 p.m. for a man barricaded inside a home in the 2200 block of 3rd Street. He was wanted on a protective order violation. Earlier in the evening, at 7:40 p.m., a vehicle struck a cyclist at North Chester Avenue and Roberts Lane and kept going — the car described as a maroon or red Toyota with front-end damage and no bumper, last seen heading east on East Roberts Lane.

A white Honda CR-V was later reported trying to pick up the missing bumper. Crews also handled a vehicle fire in the 3600 block of Sewell Street at 7:42 p.m., and Delano police responded to a rollover at 10:47 p.m. where a vehicle landed on its roof in the roadway with one patient reported. The overnight hours turned domestic and violent.

At 11:22 p.m., a man with a gun was reported threatening people near an auto shop. Eleven minutes later, officers were sent to the 5000 block of Pierce Road in north Bakersfield after a juvenile reported his mother had hit him with a meat cleaver; two juveniles were to be checked on. At 11:49 p.m., officers responded to the 1400 block of East 9th Street, where a victim was reported assaulted with an iron object.

A fight between a man and a woman in the 3000 block of Glenwood was reported out of control at 12:41 a.m. Then came a priority domestic at 2:36 a.m. in the 8100 block of Seminole Court in southwest Bakersfield, where one person reported being cut with a box cutter and the other was reported sitting on a bed holding it. Two more priority domestic calls followed — the 300 block of Dr Martin Luther King Jr Boulevard at 2:50 a.m. and the 2900 block of Descanso Street at 3:08 a.m.

A battery at a grocery store in the 7900 block of White Lane was reported at 3:58 a.m., and at 4:19 a.m. crews were sent to a market in the 9500 block of South Union Avenue, where a woman was reported to have set a fire at the back of the building and was being argumentative. Deputies also checked on three young children, ages three to six, reported outside with three adults at 7:19 p.m., and officers ran a welfare check at a motel on the frontage road at 7:31 p.m. for a man who called 911 saying he was scared.

 Today's Weather

It's 50 degrees at Meadows Field this morning, on the way to a high near 68 and a low around 51 tonight. No active weather alerts are in effect for Kern County, and there are no active fires to report. Air quality sits at 61 — moderate — so most folks can go about their day normally, though sensitive readers may want to keep an eye on it if they're planning hard outdoor work this afternoon. Light layers this morning, short sleeves by lunch.

 Something Good

A Kern County nonprofit is working to educate Spanish-speaking voters about the propositions on the upcoming ballot, according to 23ABC. The effort is aimed at helping more residents understand what they're being asked to decide and take part in the election — the kind of quiet, patient work that strengthens a community one conversation at a time. If you know someone who'd welcome help understanding the ballot, this is a good week to point them toward it.
