 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Outside Fire at Roberts Ln area north Bakersfield

23 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Overnight, Kern County emergency crews handled a heavy fire shift and a moderate police load, with most calls concentrated before midnight and thinning out after 3 a.m. The most significant incident was a structure fire in an alley behind the 1000 block of Flower Street, dispatched just before 1 a.m. when arriving companies reported flames in an alley off Owens Street. Crews checked the building for extension and held the address through the initial attack.

Shortly after 4 a.m., multiple callers reported a grass fire south of the 48th station area along East Bear Mountain Boulevard, with concern that it could reach an electrical pole; an engine, two patrols and a battalion were assigned, and crews approaching on Interstate 5 reported being just south of the fire. Two vehicle fires burned at opposite ends of the county in the pre-dawn hours, though no major injuries were reported. On the law enforcement side, officers responded around 12:58 a.m. to a motel on Union Avenue for a person reportedly armed with a firearm; the subject was described as moving on foot, and units coordinated on arrival.

Earlier, at 10:26 p.m., a disturbance involving five or six females against one person drew a large response after a caller reported one woman holding a firearm near a black pickup parked in front of a residence. A victim also told dispatchers around 2:47 a.m. that he had been struck with a crowbar by a man in his 20s wearing all black clothing; the report was taken for follow-up. Additional calls included a disturbance at the 100 block of 20th Street West around 10:10 p.m., where a manager relayed a report of employees trying to separate two parties outside a business, and a loud music complaint on the 2700 block of Holland Street at 10:17 p.m.

 Today's Weather

Expect a dry, warm Saturday with a high near 92 degrees and a low around 65 tonight. As of early morning, Meadows Field (KBFL) reported a current temperature of 64 degrees under mostly clear skies. Air quality is moderate at an AQI of 60, which is typical for this time of year and shouldn’t pose a significant concern for most residents, though those sensitive to particulate matter may want to limit prolonged outdoor exertion. No active weather alerts are in effect for Kern County, and no major fires are burning locally.

 Something Good

There’s a bright note for fans of country music and literacy alike: California has officially designated September 25 as Dolly Parton Day, an honor championed by a Bakersfield state senator. The recognition celebrates the beloved entertainer’s legacy — including her Imagination Library program that has given millions of free books to children — and brings a little extra pride to Kern County, where the senator’s efforts helped make it happen. It’s a reminder that even in a busy news cycle, our community can rally around something joyful.
