 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Pursuit And Crash at South H St to South Union Ave area

27 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Two police chases kept officers busy before dawn, one of them ending in a crash on South Union Avenue and a foot search through backyards, and a stabbing sent deputies to Oildale's Linda Vista area. A carnival drew a run of missing-child calls and a fight, crews handled vehicle fires on the southwest side, and a man was reported throwing knives and striking a relative with a bat at a home on Oregon Street. The rest of the log was medical aids, alarms and traffic checks.

Just after midnight a silver sedan reported at about 80 mph on southbound Highway 99 led officers onto city streets. The driver turned south on South H Street, went east on McKee Road and hit a dead end just east of Highway 99, made a U-turn, then crashed into the median on South Union Avenue near the 7500 block. He ran west along a fence line into a residential block near Acoma Way, and a sheriff's air unit followed him over backyards near Lazy River Drive before losing sight of him behind a home.

Officers set a perimeter and worked through the yards. At 12:38 a.m. a traffic stop on westbound Niles Street turned into a chase when a vehicle reported to be carrying at least five people took off. It ran east on Pioneer Drive through a red light at Fairfax Road, turned north on Meadows, and ended near the 4900 block of Pioneer Drive, where the occupants jumped a fence and ran.

Officers detained three people and reported one still outstanding. At 11:27 p.m. officers were sent to an apartment on the 3900 block of Oregon Street after a caller reported her brother was having a mental breakdown, was throwing knives around, and had hit another relative with a baseball bat. A verbal argument could be heard in the background and the call drew a heavy police and fire response.

At 9:06 p.m. deputies were sent to the Linda Vista area for a stabbing; the caller reported a man had stab wounds to his arm and that the man's girlfriend was with him, and a suspect was reported leaving in a white Chevrolet Silverado. Through the evening a carnival drew a run of missing-child reports. A 3-year-old girl in a Dodgers jersey was reported missing and found at the main gate, a 9-year-old boy was missing for about 15 minutes before he was located, a 2-year-old was reported missing before being found, and an 8-year-old girl was reported missing later in the night.

Officers also handled a fight between two groups of women at the Ferris wheel. Crews were sent to a report of multiple vehicles burning at Playfair Road and Ashe Road around 9:35 p.m., and employees and security reported a man attempted to take merchandise in the Chester Avenue area just after 10 p.m.
