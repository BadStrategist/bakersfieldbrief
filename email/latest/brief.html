It was a busy night across the county, with 30 incidents logged between 7:09 p.m. and 6:44 a.m. Fires led the way with 11 calls, followed by six medical and overdose responses, three crashes and traffic incidents, three disturbances and welfare checks, and two weapons or assault calls. The evening started early with a fire reported just after 7 p.m., then a pair of medical aids in the 7 and 8 o'clock hours, including one on Oregano Road.

Three separate structure fires were logged between 7:59 p.m. and 10:54 p.m., with additional fire calls at 8:54 p.m. and 11:02 p.m. A traffic collision came in at 10:27 p.m., another at 11:32 p.m., and a domestic call and welfare check rounded out the late evening. Deputies also responded to a suspicious person report around 8:31 p.m. and an officer assist at 8:09 p.m.

After midnight, the pace didn't let up. A welfare call came in on Taft Highway just past midnight, followed by an outside fire on West Niles Street at 12:25 a.m. and more fire calls at 12:29 a.m. and 2 a.m., the latter a vehicle fire. Two shootings were reported — one at 1:29 a.m. and another at 5:58 a.m. — and a warrant service and probation check were both logged at 3:12 a.m.

A fire on Jefferson Street came in at 3:55 a.m., with medical aids following at 4:04 a.m. and 4:05 a.m. on Wilson Road. The final call of the night was a traffic collision at 6:40 a.m., just as the morning commute was getting started. All details remain preliminary until confirmed by an official source.

 Today's Weather

It's a mild November morning in Bakersfield, with the current observation at Meadows Field reading 73 degrees. Today's high should top out near 76, and tonight's low will settle around 52 — comfortable sweater weather, nothing more. No active weather alerts are in effect for Kern County, and there are no fires burning in the area. Air quality is moderate at an AQI of 85, which is fine for most folks, though those with sensitive respiratory conditions may want to keep an eye on it if they're planning extended time outdoors. All in all, a good day to be outside.

 Something Good

23ABC brought us a wonderful story out of Memorial Stadium this weekend, where Bakersfield College celebrated the 50th anniversary of its 1976 Junior Rose Bowl championship. Some of those same players from five decades ago returned to the field, including Michael Georgino, who said walking back into the stadium felt like yesterday. The Renegades capped the night with a 40-21 win over College of the Canyons, finishing the regular season on a four-game winning streak and a 6-4 record. The team is expected to play in a bowl game for the first time since 2024 — a fitting way to honor a legacy that still means something in this town.
