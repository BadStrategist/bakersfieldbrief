Read the full overnight log

The night's biggest call went out as an assault on an officer. Officers working near Monitor Street and Pacheco Road in south Bakersfield reported that someone had fired roughly five shots at them, and a man went down with a gun still in his hand. A second person in a black hoodie ran north through the apartment complex just west of the scene, and police said three others may have slipped into the neighborhood to the north.

A drone and a Bearcat were brought in, and officers worked through the buildings, the carports and the parking area by hand before reporting the complex and the neighborhood to the west clear. The search ran close to an hour. Fires kept crews moving into the early morning.

Just after 3 a.m., a large stack of pallets burned in a yard off Kernita Road
