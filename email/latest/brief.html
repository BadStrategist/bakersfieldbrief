The most serious call of the night came at 9:32 p.m., when officers were sent near Wible Road for a man in a tan shirt and black pants armed with a knife, reported to be trying to stab someone. Medical units staged nearby in case they were needed. In north Bakersfield, officers were sent to a home on Wainwright Drive at 1:52 a.m. for a woman in her late 30s, known to carry a knife, who was trying to climb through a window.

Two domestic violence calls came in during the evening: one at 9:13 p.m. at an apartment in the 9200 block of California Avenue, where two juveniles were inside and the address had a history of domestic violence, and another at 11:25 p.m. in the 5000 block of Pierce Road, where the man left on a red and black scooter. A person listed as at risk left a home in the 2600 block of Cherry Street in central Bakersfield on foot at about 6 p.m. after a disturbance, saying he wanted to die. He was last seen in a burgundy or black sweatshirt and dark shoes, and was still missing as of the overnight report.

The night's heaviest fire came at 2:08 a.m., with heavy fire from the rear of a structure that crews could not reach with an attack line. Battalion 1 took command, a truck was asked to ventilate the building, and building utilities and the gas company were notified. Earlier, at 7:04 p.m., a full structure response went to the 800 block of Piute Street in southwest Bakersfield for a gas leak inside a home with a fire detector sounding.

A rescue crew was sent outside the rescue mission on East 21st Street at 7:05 p.m. for a person close to fainting, and at 7:11 p.m. an adult woman was reported hurt in the 1700 block of South Chester Avenue near a gas station; her boyfriend took her for care in a private vehicle and the ambulance was not needed.

 Today's Weather

It's 52 degrees at Meadows Field this morning, on the way to a high near 70 and a low around 53 tonight — an easy, classic late-October day in the valley. No active weather alerts are in effect for Kern County, and there are no active fires to report. Air quality sits at 67, which is moderate, so most people can be outside without worry, though those who are sensitive to particulates may want to keep an eye on it if they're planning a long stretch of exertion. Light layers this morning, short sleeves by afternoon.

 Something Good

There's new green space to enjoy north of the river. The North of the River Parks District and Lennar, the homebuilder behind the Gossamer Grove and Marcona Preserve communities, celebrated the opening of two new parks at Marcona Preserve. Together they span 2.75 acres and include putting greens, chess tables, a ping-pong table and cornhole. Lennar says the parks are meant to be places where neighbors connect and families make memories, and with the addition, North of the River now counts 27 parks in its district.
