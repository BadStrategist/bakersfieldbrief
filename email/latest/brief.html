 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Structure Fire at Bakersfield

18 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Deputies were called to a Main Street location around 3:22 a.m. after reports of a large fight involving as many as 15 people. A neighbor told dispatchers they could see the group outside, and a firearm was mentioned in the reports. The call was handled as an investigation, with no further details released by early morning.

Fire crews had their own busy stretch. Just after midnight, a grass and brush fire west of Rosamond was reported at three to four acres with a moderate rate of spread and wind out of the west. Command requested a water tender and two additional wildland engines to support the initial attack.

Closer to home, engine and truck companies were sent to the East Terrace Way block at 2:11 a.m. for a fire call, and a second fire call in Bakersfield also kept crews moving through the night, though dispatchers did not provide further information on either. Officers also responded to a handful of other calls across the city and county. A woman at a Quincy Street apartment reported at 3:57 a.m. that her daughter’s boyfriend was assaulting her.

In the Norris Road area, a report came in of a young person going door to door seeking help before running east. The overnight log also included a semi pulling an aluminum trailer on Interstate 5 north of Highway 46 that was reported unable to maintain its lane, a full closure of eastbound Highway 58 at Mohawk that was expected to last until 5 a.m., and a neighbors’ dispute on Terrace Way over a bearded dragon that had been missing for months. Two units were sent to that one.

A disturbance on Donna Avenue involved a brother-in-law at a front door, and a suspicious person was seen in the east part of the city with a crowbar trying to open a tan sedan. Elsewhere, an ambulance crew was flagged down on an F Street block for a patient in an alley, crews went to a San Marino Drive address for a pesticide exposure, and a caller at the Wheeler Ridge Road travel center reported a man who appeared to be under the influence in the store.

 Today's Weather

Expect a high near 86 today and a low around 61 tonight. Right now it’s 66 degrees at Meadows Field. The National Weather Service in Hanford has issued a lake wind advisory in effect until 11 p.m., with the strongest gusts expected on area lakes. Air quality is moderate, with an AQI of 100, and the only active fire in Kern County is the Rocky Fire, according to CAL Fire. No other fire-related alerts are in effect for the valley.

 Something Good

The annual Kern Cancer Run/Walk brought more than 1,500 people to the Park at Riverwalk on Saturday, a turnout that included over 200 cancer survivors. As 23ABC reported, the Kern County Cancer Foundation has raised more than $2 million through this event over the years. Executive director Michelle Avila said the foundation pays patient bills, sometimes for as much as $10,000, and provides transportation so people can get to treatment. Survivor Melody Saberon Ibarra, a Delano native who has been cancer free for more than two decades, said the foundation was a blessing when she was a young college student with no income. Another survivor, Trella Linley, said the foundation that once helped her is now helping her son with his medical expenses during his cancer treatment. It’s a good reminder that in Kern County, a lot of people show up for each other.
