 The Kern Overnight

Scanner brief from 9pm to 5am

What happened overnight: Robbery at 2600 block Mount Vernon Ave

32 incidents tracked overnight · locations generalized  Full Report

Dispatch logged 32 incidents overnight, led by six crashes, six medical and overdose calls, and four disturbances and welfare checks. The one that drew the most units was the robbery in progress in the 2600 block of Mount Vernon Avenue, where a machete was used at the Dollar Tree. Two suspects were last seen on foot heading toward the fast-food lot next door, and officers were working the area.

If you were near Mount Vernon early this morning, that's what the patrol cars were doing. Out on the roads, a crash on southbound Interstate 5 north of the rest area left two people ejected from a vehicle. Traffic was held while crews worked, and one patient was flown out while the other went by ground.

A vehicle rolled over on northbound Rosedale Highway just north of Jenkins Road; crews checked the area but couldn't find a reported black or blue sedan on the right shoulder. Wasco police handled a crash where the driver tried to flee and was detained — nobody was pinned, and the driver complained of generalized pain. Officers also went to Panama Lane and Pebble Point for a vehicle against a pedestrian, with a person reported down in the roadway on the southbound side.

Deputies were sent to the 2100 block of Lancer Drive, where a caller said her husband and four friends had gone dirt biking and none had returned or answered their phones — crews responded for a possible search and rescue. A woman called 911 from Yuma Way to report she had been strangled, then disconnected; officers went to check on her. Firefighters handled a house fire at a county address, finding several spots inside showing heat but no hazard to anyone, and released units from the call.

The evening log also included a domestic disturbance in the 1300 block of Princeton Avenue with a criminal protective order listing the caller as a protected party, a man found unconscious and bleeding on a bicycle at Manchester Lane, and a theft in progress on the 3400 block of Mall View Road reported as five juveniles loading up.

 Today's Weather

Expect a high near 69 and a low around 49 tonight — a genuinely fall-feeling Monday after that stretch of triple-digit heat. It's currently 57 degrees at Meadows Field. No active weather alerts for Kern County, and air quality is 44, which is good. Fire crews remain on one active fire in Kern, the Cyrus Fire. If you've been waiting to open the windows or get back outside, this is the week for it.

 Something Good

The third annual Cultura Fest brought families to Stramler Park on Saturday for a free celebration of heritage — traditional dancing, live music and local vendors — organized by Raices of Kern. Proceeds go to scholarships for students, including first-generation college students. Maria Torres watched her daughter perform traditional Mexican dances and said it reminded her of the celebrations she grew up with, and that without events like this, families simply don't get exposed to those traditions. That's a good note to start the week on.
